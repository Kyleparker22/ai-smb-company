# Valuation worksheet — for equity grants

> Not advice. If you are granting equity, the valuation method and its documentation are things to
> settle **with counsel and an accountant before the grant**, not after.

**Why this exists:** a profits-interest or option grant needs a defensible value at the grant date.
"We guessed" is not defensible, and the cost of getting it wrong lands on the recipient as a tax
problem years later.

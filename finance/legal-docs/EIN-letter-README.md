# Your EIN letter goes here

Save the IRS **CP-575** confirmation as `IRS_CP575_EIN_letter.pdf` in this folder.

**Why it matters:** the CP-575 is the document banks and payment processors ask to see, and **the IRS
will not reissue it.** If you lose it you get a *147C* letter instead — a slower phone conversation
that some vendors accept grudgingly. Keep the original PDF and a copy somewhere outside this repo.

**Getting one:** free, directly from the IRS, about ten minutes, only after your entity exists. Do
not pay a service — they resell a free government form.

⚠️ Do not commit this PDF to a public repository.

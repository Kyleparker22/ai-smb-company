# The flat $100/mo client referral credit

- **Proposed by:** referral program v1 (`decisions/2026-06-30_referral-program-v1.md`)
- **Rejected:** 2026-08-13 by the Founder — superseded by one rate card for everyone
- **Why:** It was 10% of a $1,000/mo deal and was never re-based when the Core floor moved to $3,000/mo, so the identical referral paid a connector $300 and a client $100. A rate that silently decays against your own price list is a drift bug, not a policy. Superseded by `decisions/2026-08-13_one-referral-rate-card.md`.
- **Revisit if:** a *rate* differentiated by referrer type is wanted again, it must be re-derived from the current price floor and carry its own re-basing trigger — the failure here was the missing link to the floor, not the existence of a client-specific rate.
- **Check:** `_none — this is superseded rather than pending. Nothing about live data should reopen it; only a deliberate redesign of the rate card would._`
- **Review:** 2027-02-13

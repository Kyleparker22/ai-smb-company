# Concentrate GTM on a landscaping/hardscaping beachhead

<!-- date-verified: anti-library backfill 2026-08-13; filename = date of the original call -->

- **Proposed by:** Brett (`loops/brett-ideas/2026-06-17_ideas.md`, Idea 1 — "own one vertical completely")
- **Rejected:** 2026-08-05 by the Founder — retired in favour of all-industries-from-day-one, warm intros first
- **Why:** Targeting went horizontal with the positioning. A beachhead bets the funnel on one trade before a single client has signed; the warm network is not trade-shaped, and the first engagement being a hardscaper is a fact about who the Founder knows, not evidence of a vertical.
- **Revisit if:** the signed book itself concentrates in one trade and referral density inside it is measurably higher than outside — i.e. the vertical shows up in the data rather than in the plan.
- **Check:** `liveClients >= 3`
- **Check covers:** volume only, and volume is the weakest half. Referral density by industry is not computed; neither is trade on the company record in a queryable way. Treat a firing check as "go look at where the book actually came from."
- **Review:** 2027-02-05

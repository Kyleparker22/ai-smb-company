# The per-vertical marketing funnel (Industries hub, vertical templates, vertical-keyed Leak Snapshot)

<!-- date-verified: anti-library backfill 2026-08-13; filename = date of the original call -->

- **Proposed by:** Webb / GTM buildout
- **Rejected:** 2026-06-22 by the Founder — parked, not deleted (files live in `agents/webb/pages/yourco-site-v2/_parked/`)
- **Why:** Positioning went horizontal: the offer is audit → custom AI OS for any business in any industry, and yourco does not segment marketing by trade. Maintaining a per-vertical funnel against a horizontal offer is surface nobody reads and drift nobody catches. See `decisions/2026-06-22_horizontal-positioning-and-os-tiers.md` and `decisions/2026-06-22_website-dial-back.md`.
- **Revisit if:** referral density concentrates in one industry to the point that vertical depth is a *consequence* of where clients came from, rather than a pre-selected bet. Depth is earned from the book, never chosen in advance (`CLAUDE.md` §GTM sequencing, 2026-08-05).
- **Check:** `signedClients >= 5`
- **Check covers:** the *volume* precondition only. It says nothing about whether that book is concentrated in one industry — the actual trigger — because industry mix of signed clients is not computed anywhere. A firing check is a prompt to look at the book, never a green light to rebuild the funnel.

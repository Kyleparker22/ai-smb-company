# Learning — a *built* give-first artifact converts; an ask does not

**Date:** 2026-07-28 · **Type:** pipeline pattern / thesis confirmation · **Surfaced by:** Melanie (initiative loop)

## What the pipeline actually shows
As of 2026-07-28, exactly two deals have ever advanced past `prospect`: **Prospect A → Proposal** (firm-priced $6k; a full storm-alert give-first POC is coded in `clients/prospect-a/prototype/`) and **Sample Client → Discovery** (a written OS module roadmap + OS proposal exist, `clients/sample-client/06_*`, `07_*`). The other **20** warm prospects — same warm network, same owner (the Founder) — sit unmoved at `prospect` for 45 days.

**The discriminator is not warmth** (all 22 are the Founder's warm contacts). **It's whether a concrete artifact was built to show.** The two that advanced had one; the 20 that stalled had only an unexecuted "reach out / give-first demo" *plan* as their next step. Conversion record: **built artifact 2-for-2; bare ask 0-for-20.**

## Why this is the actionable finding (not just "warm leads are good")
Brett's floor experiment (eval-review 07-05/07-12) already proved the *send-a-friend ask* is **emotionally blocked** and does not move by reducing friction further (drafted texts sat unsent for weeks). So the lever is **not** "get the Founder to send the ask." It's that **building the give-first artifact is the OS's own job** — it needs no external send and no emotional lift — and it changes the Founder's remaining task from *"ask a favor"* (never happens) to *"show a friend a finished thing built for them"* (proven twice to happen). Move the work onto the layer that isn't blocked.

This is the same wedge as `[[2026-06-18_verdent-tooling-vs-operated]]`: **shown, not said.** Proof-by-doing is what converts.

## How to apply (Step 0 for sales / pipeline / initiative loops)
- When a warm prospect stalls at `prospect`, don't default the next step to "reach out." Ask: **is there a built artifact to show, and can the OS build/re-skin one without a the Founder send?** If a structurally-identical prototype already exists (we own two), a re-skin is cheap OS-side work.
- Prototype-reuse map (2026-07-28): roofing warm prospects (Sample Company A c8, Classic Roofing czysno) ← re-skin Nick's storm POC; hardscaping/painting/construction (Sample Company 48 c22, Axhoj c19, Victory c28) ← re-skin the Sample Client proposal automation.
- **Honest boundary:** a give-first artifact still needs one discovery detail per prospect (geography/roster for storm; proposal format for the SC-style build) — so it doesn't remove the Founder, it *lowers* his lift to the motion that works. Don't oversell it as zero-touch.

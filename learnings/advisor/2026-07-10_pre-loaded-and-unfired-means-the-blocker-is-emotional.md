2026-07-10 — When a de-risked action still doesn't fire, the blocker is emotional, not structural

Source: Brett — the 07-06 advisor memo installed a "floor" (a pre-loaded 30-min calendar block with three warm texts already drafted inside it) to force the first warm outreach; the 07-10 open-loops desk (sixth consecutive honest zero) shows the texts still unsent. The experiment ran and failed informatively.
Pattern: For four memos and two Brett-ideas drops the recommended fix escalated in structural pre-loading — freeze → mechanism/scoreboard → floor with copy pre-written and a calendar block. Each assumed the blocker was that the right action wasn't decided / small enough / pre-loaded. When the action was de-risked to its last mechanical step (name chosen, copy written, block on the calendar) and *still* didn't happen, the remaining variable is the emotional/relational cost of the act itself — here, risking a "no" from a friend, against which building is the frictionless, rejection-free alternative.
Implication: When an agreed action repeatedly doesn't happen despite being fully pre-loaded, stop adding artifacts (a louder recommendation, a tracker, a brake — each becomes another place to hide). Diagnose the *emotional* cost and reduce it directly: change *what* is being asked, not *how well* it's teed up. Concrete lever that fit: reframe a rejectable pitch ("want to see my demo?") into an un-rejectable favor/critique-request ("I trust your eye — tear this apart for me"), which the 2026 founder-sales consensus independently backs (ask-for-advice beats ask-for-the-sale). A recommendation declined 3+ times is a signal about conviction/friction, not about needing repetition (companion to the 07-06 candidate: replace a re-issued rec with the smallest pre-loaded start — and if even that doesn't fire, the blocker is emotional).
Audience: Brett (advisor + ideas loops); Reilly/Sadie on outreach framing; any loop that keeps re-surfacing the same un-done founder action.

Triggers: agent:brett, loop:open-loops-chaser, loop:brett-ideas, same action re-surfaced, founder action not fired, de-risked but undone

The big idea

Most businesses hire people to answer phones, book jobs, chase quotes, and do the admin. yourco builds a business a named AI employee instead — it lives inside their tools (their phone, inbox, calendar), does the work in their voice, and is live in 48 hours. The client owns the outcome; they never touch the AI, the cost, or the tech. That's what we sell. The twist: yourco itself is run the same way — by a team of AI employees, with one human (me) conducting.

How the machine works

1

I point the work

I give a task and hold every approval — nothing customer-facing goes out without my okay.

2

An AI does it, as a role

The "employees" (sales, delivery, finance…) are defined jobs. An AI takes the task on as that role — either when I ask it, or on a schedule with no human at all.

3

It saves the result back

Every output lands in one shared system and compounds — the company literally gets smarter and more organized every time it runs.

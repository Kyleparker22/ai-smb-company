## What this is worth vs. what it costs *(both sides)*
*Modeled from conservative assumptions about a shop your size — the math is shown, and we'll re-run every line with your real numbers before you sign.*

**Assumptions:** average project **$27,500** · conservative net profit **20% ≈ $5,500 / project**.

| What the system drives / saves | What you invest |
|---|---|
| **More closed jobs** — 24/7 lead capture + <60-sec response (~10× contact rate) + same-day close vs. the 2–6 wk gap | **$1,000** one-time build |
| **Bigger tickets** — Base/Better/Dream upsell lifts the average job | **$3,000 / mo** — months 1–3 (intro) |
| **Recovered revenue** — follow-up autopilot resurrects dead quotes; margin guardrail stops underpriced bids | **$5,000 / mo** — month 4 on |
| **Hard cost replaced** — outsourced 3D renders + designer takeoff hours | *(list rate $8,000–$10,000/mo — see below)* |

### How many jobs to pay for it — two ways to get there
**Year 1 cost (with the intro ramp): $55,000. Steady-state: $60,000/yr.**

**Way 1 — more jobs.** At ~$5,500 net profit/job → **≈ 10 projects across the whole year covers the entire platform** — less than one *extra* job per month (0.83/mo). On gross profit it's ~6 projects/year.

**Way 2 — bigger jobs (if the crew is already busy).** The system's real job then isn't more work — it's a **richer job mix at higher margin** (Base→Better→Dream upsell, premium materials, capacity-aware prioritization of the high-margin work). Bigger tickets carry higher margin, so it pays off in *fewer* projects:

| Scenario | Avg ticket | Net margin | Profit / job | Projects to cover the year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| More jobs (base case) | $27,500 | ~20% | ~$5,500 | **~10 / yr** |
| **Bigger jobs (busy crew)** | **$40,000** | **~22%** | ~$8,800 | **~6–7 / yr** |

**Way 3 — pure mix-shift, zero extra jobs.** Upsell **15 jobs you were already going to do** by one tier (+$12k ticket each, ~22% margin = +$2,640 profit each) → **~$40,000** — most of the platform's annual cost recovered **without a single new job**, just from showing the homeowner the bigger yard on their own screen.

> **Projected payback:** whether Sample Client grows by *more* jobs or *bigger* jobs, the platform clears its cost at ~6–10 projects a year (or from mix-shift alone) — everything above that is profit it helped create.

> These are projections from stated assumptions, not guarantees or third-party benchmarks. Give us your real average ticket, close rate, and lead volume and we'll re-run this with your numbers.

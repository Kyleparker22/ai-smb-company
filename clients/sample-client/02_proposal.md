> The proposal the Founder sent Client Owner. Original: `attachments/YourCo Proposal — Sample Client.pdf`. Prepared for the Client Owner, Sample Client Design & Build, by the Founder.

## What Sample Client is getting
A digital employee — its own name, its own `@sampleclient.example.com` email — that runs early-stage project communication the moment a proposal is signed in Aspire:
- **Client:** deposit request by email + text, payment tiers applied correctly every time.
- **Suppliers:** material orders drafted per supplier, routed shop vs job site, with needed-by dates.
- **Subcontractors:** scope + start-window notices for every SUB line item.
- **All-clear:** when deposit, suppliers, and subs are confirmed, Client Owner and Charlene get the "greenlit" email — plus a nudge list of anything sitting too long.

Engineered, not glued together: nothing sent without Charlene's or Client Owner's approval (one tap from the phone); every dollar computed by tested code, never by AI; duplicate-proof; ships with a test suite to prove it works before it touches a real customer.

## What "operated" means
yourco runs it — monitoring around the clock, problems fixed before they're noticed, a monthly report of what the employee actually did, continuous improvement. Sample Client never touches servers, AI accounts, tokens, or code.

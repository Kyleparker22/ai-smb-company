# Sample Client — Instant Range + Fast Quote (concept, use case 2)

> A second, larger opportunity that surfaced in the meeting. Outside the main proposal's pricing. Original: `attachments/YourCo — Instant Range + Fast Quote (Concept).pdf`. Status: concept — pursue after the kickoff agent is live.

## The problem, in Client Owner's words
"If there is a design it could take 1–2 months. If it's straightforward, 2–3 weeks. All of our jobs are pretty custom, so it takes time to eliminate grey areas." That 1–2 month window is where deals die — homeowners collect other bids, cool off, or spend the budget elsewhere. The goal isn't an exact price on the spot; it's a **credible range at the kitchen table, and a firm quote in days instead of weeks.**

## How it would work — three parts
1. **The instant range.** On the first visit, the rep walks the property with a guided capture on their phone (scope checklist, measurements, photos, voice notes). The system matches it against Sample Client's own Aspire history (what similar projects quoted and cost) and returns a range on the spot — "projects like this typically land $22–31K." The homeowner gets a real number while excited; the rep learns immediately whether the budget is real, before investing design hours and a 3D rendering in a tire-kicker.
2. **The grey-area killer.** Most of the 1–2 months isn't drawing, it's waiting on decisions. The same visit captures the choices that normally trickle out over six weeks (paver line, colors, pattern, lighting, wall heights), and the system chases every open decision by email/text until the list is closed.
3. **The draft estimate.** The captured scope pre-builds the Aspire estimate from Sample Client's own assemblies and unit costs. Noah or Client Owner review and adjust a draft instead of building from zero — the human always owns the final number.

## Why the numbers can be trusted
Ranges are presented as ranges, never quotes, and every range is tracked against the final signed price — so the system calibrates on real jobs and proves its own accuracy ("9 of 10 ranges held"), a stat Sample Client could eventually show customers. Firm pricing always passes through human approval.

## Honest sizing — a bigger lift
This is substantially bigger than typical automation work — closer to a product than a workflow. It means digging into historical Aspire data, codifying assemblies and unit costs with Noah, building the capture tool, and calibrating ranges before anyone uses one in a driveway. Directional, good-faith numbers (refined after digging in):

| | Rough estimate |
|---|---|
| Build time | ≈ 1 month, after the kickoff agent is live (it builds the foundation this reuses) |
| Implementation | ≈ $5,000 one-time (data work, capture tool, calibration; may add tools to the stack) |
| Retainer add | ≈ $500 / mo (maintenance, range-accuracy monitoring, AI usage) |

Not a quote — a proper scope follows if Sample Client wants to pursue it.

## What it's worth
First-visit close rate vs week-six close rate tells the story, and Aspire already holds the baseline. A faster cycle that closes even a few extra installs a year pays for itself many times over — and it would let Sample Client quote in a way nobody else in the Yourtownmarket can, built on data only they have.

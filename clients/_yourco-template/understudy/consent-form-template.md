## 1. What this is — in plain language
The Company is building a **continuity handbook** for your role: a written, living document of how your job actually runs — the workflows, the decision rules, the contacts — so the business isn't stuck if you're ever out (vacation, illness, or if you move on). A software agent reads a **limited, listed set** of your work materials on a schedule, and keeps that handbook current. If you're absent, the Company can activate a stand-in agent that **drafts** what your role would produce, for a named person to review — it does not act on its own.

**This only happens with your signature.** There is no monitoring without your consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time (§6).

## 3. What it will never be used for — written here so you can hold us to it
- **No productivity scoring.** No "what is this employee doing all day" reporting, ever.
- **No performance-review input.** Nothing collected under this consent feeds any evaluation, discipline, or compensation decision about you.
- **No replacement.** The stand-in activates only on absence events (§5) — never to displace you while you hold the role.
The product is **continuity, not oversight**.

## 4. What you and the Company get
- The **role handbook** (`role-handbook-template.md` format) — the Company's property, and a document you can read at any time: [[where the employee can view it]].
- Reduced key-person pressure on you: coverage when you're out, documented workflows instead of 2am phone calls.

## 5. When a stand-in activates
Only on a **named absence event** — [[resignation / illness / PTO ≥ [[N]] days — Company defines]] — by decision of [[NAMED APPROVER]], never automatically. While active, everything outward is a **draft reviewed by a named person** before anything is sent. It deactivates when you return or the role is backfilled.

## 6. Withdrawing consent
Tell [[named Company contact]] in writing (email suffices). Observation stops **the same day**; collected extracts are deleted; the handbook keeps only the operational patterns already written into it. Withdrawal carries no employment consequence: [[Company attestation]].

Extracts are tenant-isolated, audit-logged, retained [[retention period]], and accessible to [[named roles only]]. Full posture: [[reference to engagement agreement / data schedule]].

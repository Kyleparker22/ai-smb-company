**Sample Product** = a storm-verification product built for Prospect A (FL roofer) to test internally ~1 month, then potentially market. Partnership: one sells, one builds. Pricing target $5–10k/yr/company (comps ~$3,500); FL has 2,000–5,000 roofers. Renamed from working title "Storm Command" (2026-07-02 call).

**What it does:** aggregates free NOAA sources into one AI-verified alert feed — cross-references + **averages** wind/hail readings, **grades** severity (A–D), filters junk (AI verdict GO/HOLD/REJECT). Plus crew field log + coverage rollup + claim reports.

What the monthly module does · the operated version

  1. Enter it once — bank transactions come in; each is matched to tenant + property and coded (rent / fee / maintenance / disbursement). You approve exceptions only.
  2. The books become views — journal, per-property ledgers, and trial balance always in sync, always reconciled.
  3. Close on rails — fees computed, landlord disbursement list drafted, owner statements drafted. One screen, one approve.
  4. Watchdogs — late rent by the 5th, balance drift, aging uncleared items, SD transfers: flagged the day they happen.

Guardrails · non-negotiable

  1. The system never moves money — it drafts; Kimi approves every disbursement.
  2. Every figure traceable to a source transaction; full audit trail retained.
  3. Trust-account data stays isolated; nothing shared, nothing trained on.
  4. Counsel review of NCREC record-keeping fit before anything goes live.

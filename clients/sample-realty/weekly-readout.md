# Weekly readout — template ([[CLIENT]] / [[EMPLOYEE]])

> The proactive executive-trust touchpoint: a short, warm email the client gets every week so they *see the value without asking*. The console shows it live; this readout lands in their inbox and keeps yourco top-of-mind. Owner: **Kortney** (drafts from console + eval data) → the Founder approves → send. Closes the gap-audit "see value" item. Keep it to one screen; lead with outcomes, never features.

## Send cadence
Weekly (e.g., Monday AM). Draft auto-generated from the week's activity + eval signals; **nothing sends without approval** (autonomy ladder).

## The template (fill the `[[ ]]`)

**Subject:** `[[EMPLOYEE]]'s week — [[N]] [jobs booked / leads caught / tickets handled]`

```
Hi [[first name]],

Here's what [[EMPLOYEE]] handled this week 👇

  • [[N]] calls/messages answered — [[X]] after hours
  • [[N]] [estimates booked / appointments set / tickets resolved]
  • [[N]] leads captured that would've gone to voicemail
  • ~[[N]] hours of your team's time saved

What that's worth: [[1 line — e.g. "8 booked estimates at your average job value"]].

A couple of things [[EMPLOYEE]] flagged to a human (as designed):
  • [[the escalation — e.g. "a clinical question → routed to your nurse line"]]
  • [[anything awaiting your approval in the console]]

What we improved this week: [[1 line — the tuning Kortney/Kimi made from real usage]].

Everything's in your console: [[console link]]. Reply here anytime, or grab time: [[calendar]].

— the Founder
```

## Rules
- **Outcomes first.** Numbers the owner cares about (jobs, leads, hours, dollars) — not "messages processed."
- **Show the gate working.** Naming what it escalated/flagged *builds* trust (it proves the reliability layer is real), it doesn't undermine it.
- **One improvement per week.** Demonstrates the "we keep it sharp" promise the proposal makes.
- **Honest.** Real numbers from the console/eval data — never inflated. A quiet week is reported as a quiet week.
- Feeds the **content franchise** ("what my AI employees did this week") and the **referral/case-study** flywheel — with the client's permission.

## Source data
Pull from: the client console activity log + approvals, Kolby's eval signals, and the cost/usage record. David/Atlas surface the numbers; Kortney writes the human line.

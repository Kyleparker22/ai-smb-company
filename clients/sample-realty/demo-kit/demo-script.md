# Demo — narrated click-path (template)

> Copy this per client and swap the [BRACKETS] for their specifics (mirrors `config.js`). For the Founder to read near-verbatim while clicking the preview. **→** lines = what to click; **SAY** lines = what to say. ~2.5 min.

---

**Open on `index.html`.**

> **SAY:** "Let me show you the whole thing on a sample job — [a $X project]. None of it's live, it's all sample data, but it's exactly what yours would do."

---

**→ Point at step 1 (the flow step — don't click).**

> **SAY:** "The second [the trigger happens — e.g. a proposal signs / a call comes in], your employee [reads it, applies your rules, drafts everything]. But nothing's gone out yet. That's the point."

---

**→ Click the approval step.**

> **SAY:** "This is [APPROVER]'s phone. The [message] is already written, ready for the client. [If there's money:] See the amount? That's locked — computed by code, not the AI, so it's right every time. [APPROVER] can tweak the wording, but can't fumble the number. They read it, tap approve…"
>
> **→ Tap "Approve & send."**
>
> **SAY:** "…and *now* it sends. And it logs who approved it and when. Nothing reaches a customer until somebody taps."

---

**→ Back, then click the board step.**

> **SAY:** "This is the part you'll live in. Everything in flight, where it stands. And up top — it nudges you on anything sitting too long, and drafts the follow-up. It chases that so you don't have to remember to."

---

**→ Back, then click the report step.**

> **SAY:** "Once a month, what it actually did — [volume, outcomes, hours saved]. And the bottom part matters most: zero messages sent without your approval, every number computed by code, never missed [a job]. That's what you're really paying for — not the drafting, the reliability."

---

**Close.**

> **SAY:** "That's the whole thing. All you do is tap approve on anything customer-facing. About [3 weeks] to build, and I run it from there — you never touch a server or an AI account. Want to do it?"

---

### Objection-handlers
- "Is the [money/number] ever wrong?" → the locked field on the approval screen.
- "What if it screws up?" → the reliability block on the report (eval suite, 0 unapproved sends, duplicates caught).
- Say "sample data" once up front so nothing feels overstated.

South Yourtown

Ballantyne & Blakeney.

Yourtown's polished southern edge — corporate campuses, manicured greenways, and every errand within a golf-cart radius.

Ballantyne works because everything is close: Blakeney's and StoneCrest's shopping and restaurants, top-rated schools, quick access to I-485, and fifteen minutes to Uptown on a good morning. Housing runs from well-kept townhome communities like Elizabeth Townes and Blakeney Greens to executive single-family streets — dense, walkable-ish, and relentlessly convenient.

It's the right fit for first-and-second-home buyers who want lock-and-leave ease, and for anyone who commutes in any direction. Townhomes here also hold rental demand, which keeps investors circling.

Union County, ST

Yourtown, Marvin & Weddington.

Estate lots, top-of-the-state schools, and a downtown that still feels like a small town — this is where Yourtowngoes when it wants land.

The Marvin Ridge and Cuthbertson school zones are the engine of this market: communities like Weddington Chase, Innisbrook at Firethorne, and Silver Creek trade on acreage, custom builds, and district lines. Downtown Yourtown's shops and restaurants anchor the weekends; Union County's lower tax rate sweetens the monthly math.

Homes here range from the mid-$600s into the multi-millions, and the strongest listings move fast when they're presented right — we've proven that twice this summer.

Lancaster & York County, SC

Indian Land & Fort Mill.

Across the state line, the same commute buys more house, a smaller tax bill, and some of the fastest-growing communities in the region.

The Highway 521 corridor has grown its own gravity — shopping, dining, and medical without crossing back into Yourtown. South Carolina's owner-occupied tax treatment keeps monthly costs noticeably lighter, Fort Mill's schools are a longtime draw, and Indian Land's 10/10-rated elementary anchors the newer rooftops. From Sun City's active-adult streets to Toll Brothers' new estates at The Pines at Sugar Creek, the range is wide.

We close on both sides of the line — so the ST-vs-SC decision gets made with real numbers, not folklore.

## E. Two compliance notes (worth 30 seconds before publishing)

1. **"Nanny" was swapped for "guest suite."** Describing a room by *who* would live in it can
   read as familial-status steering under Fair Housing; describing the *space* is always safe.
   Same reason the copy says "owner's suite" rather than "master."
2. **"Golf simulator," "built-in refrigerator," "whirlpool tub" convey** — confirm each conveys
   with the sellers before it's in print, or add "negotiable."

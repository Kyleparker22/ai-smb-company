The Boutique Difference

Specialized attention,
from listing to closing.

Sample Realty Home & Land is a boutique real estate firm — our team offers you specialized attention on all the details of the entire transaction.

We communicate with you throughout the whole process. We don't hand you off to a "transaction team" in a large office that doesn't know anything about your home. When you call, the person who answers knows your house, your timeline, and where things stand.

Kimi has over 24 years of real estate experience as the Broker and Owner of Sample Realty Home & Land — licensed in both North and South Carolina, with in-depth knowledge of the Yourtownand surrounding markets.

## The motion

**Audit first → custom AI OS.** Every engagement opens with an Audit — free while yourco is getting
started — then the OS is built and operated. A single named digital employee is the **entry rung,
offered last**.

**Lead high, land anywhere.** Every proposal opens with the OS; landing below it is a good outcome, not
a failed one. The smaller unit is the *first module of a system the client has already seen mapped* —
never pre-framed as the lesser option, because that is what makes the later upsell awkward. Growth
comes from upsell inside an account, **never from discounting to win a bigger one**.

⚠️ **A belief, not a finding.** n=0 clients. The one live data point — Sample Client, unsigned at
$1,000/mo against a $3,000 Core floor — has not expanded.

## Answering "what is an AI OS?"

The most-repeated question on every call, and until 2026-08-24 **no surface answered it**:

> "Your answering service + your CRM + your email marketing + your scheduler + your bookkeeper + your
> SOP binder. Except they talk to each other, and they do the work. **= your AI OS**"

Then close on what they cannot buy elsewhere: *"You own the outcome; we own everything that makes it
reliable."*

⚠️ **The formula sells the WHAT and cannot sell the WHY US.** Reliability, evals, approvals and the
model-upgrade dividend are invisible in a sum of familiar parts — a no-code operator could recite the
same line honestly. **The formula opens; the moat closes.**

## The primary growth lever: connectors

Not one channel among several. It compounds without headcount: connectors refer, and from **R1** they
recruit connectors who refer. One rate card — everyone who refers earns the same escalator
(10% / 12.5% / 15% by active-client count) plus a 1% full-downline override.

⚠️ **Counsel-gated.** The combination of a bounty on non-revenue events, recruit-at-R1, and uncapped
depth is checklist item **4c** — the gate's second hard stop. **No connector may be recruited at all
until §A/§B clear.** A referral carries a *mode* — Introducer or Sourcer — and on a Sourcer submission
**yourco becomes the caller**, so TCPA/FTSA/CAN-SPAM attach to yourco.

Everything lives in `processes/partnerships/`; the Connector Console is the connector-facing surface.

from day one. Depth per industry emerges from referral density, not pre-selection.

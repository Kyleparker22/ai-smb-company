## The copy (one page, buyer-facing)

**The seller is the operating system. Capture him before he walks.**

You're buying a business where the retiring owner is the manual: pricing lives in his head, the vendor relationships are his, and nobody wrote down why the schedule bends in March. The standard mitigations all evaporate. The 12-month seller consult goes quiet the day the wire clears. The transition binder is stale on arrival. Hiring an operator is exactly the payroll your model can't carry.

Acquisition OS does two things, on the deal's own calendar.

**Before close: capture.** With the seller's written, scoped, revocable consent, we run structured knowledge capture on the owner role between LOI and close: read-only access to operational exhaust plus structured interview cycles, working down the "what only the owner knows" list. It compiles into a Company Brain: pricing logic, vendor and customer history, workflow inventory, the seasonal calendar. If the deal dies, capture stops and the artifacts are destroyed, in writing.

**At close: operations, day one.** The Brain isn't a binder; it feeds working agents in your tenant from the first morning. Intake answered, scheduling running, invoice drafts moving. Every outward action queues for your approval before it happens, which in month one is a feature twice over: you control everything, and the approval queue is the fastest tour of your own operations you'll ever get. Autonomy expands only as the evidence record earns it, action by action.

**What you own.** The Brain, the handbooks, everything captured: yours, permanently, even if you later drop us. What we retain is the platform that operates it. That's the honest answer to "what happens if we cancel after transition" — you keep the knowledge, you lose the operation of it.

**What we're not.** We take no position on valuation, diligence conclusions, or whether to close. No deal advice, no finder's role, no fee tied to your deal in any form. Our fee structure is a fixed transition project fee (capture billed as incurred; balance contingent on close) plus a standard operating retainer from day one. It sits on your closing-cost sheet next to QoE and legal, and it's priced in a proposal, not on this page.

Until we've run real transitions we won't show you case studies, because we don't invent them. What we can show you today: the capture machinery, the consent pack, the approval-gated runtime, and the evidence ledger every engagement produces. Ask to see all four.

## Terms that don't vary by tier

- Every business opts in individually and signs its own standard engagement agreement, with the fee shown as sponsor-paid. No auto-enrollment, at any tier, including franchisor systems (no mandate-washing — SPEC §8).
- The sponsor has no direction rights: no module selection, no agent-behavior input, no approval-queue visibility. Non-agency clause, non-negotiable.
- Post-term, expansion or continuation is a direct yourco↔business relationship at standard pricing. The sponsor sees only the aggregate continuation rate.
- No exclusivity (categorical or geographic) as a v1 term. If ever, priced and time-boxed after the program has data.
- No commission, referral-fee, or per-signup economics inside a Patronage agreement, ever. That lane exists elsewhere and is gated separately.
- The sponsor agreement rides the gate-#1 counsel batch (third-party-payor structure, privacy wall as a contractual term, non-agency, acknowledgment bounds, SMB-side disclosure) before the first signature.

## What the sponsor's check buys, in one line each

- **Pilot:** proof the mechanism works on their portfolio, small enough to approve from a marketing or member-benefits budget.
- **Portfolio:** the program at real scale, where batched onboarding makes the per-module economics honest.
- **System:** unit-economics consistency across a franchise system, with participation still opt-in on our side.

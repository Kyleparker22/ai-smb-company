# Response → CRM warm path — The Applicant (frontier #11)

> **STAGED — internal until launch (OtherVenture) + this offering's own trigger** (board-ToS/disclosure protocol reviewed by Ray). This path activates only once real applications are being sent; documented now so the CRM wiring is ready on day one.

## Why a reply here is different

An owner who replies to this channel has self-qualified twice: they published a budgeted admission of pain (the posting), and they wrote back to an application that said "I'm an AI" in line one. That's a double-qualified warm lead, and it gets handled through the existing warm machinery, not a new pipeline.

## The path

1. **Reply lands** (email to the send address, or callback). Callbacks route to the disclosed #2 interview line or to the Founder — never to anything answering as a person (SPEC §8).
2. **Promote via the existing skill.** `promote-warm-lead` is the only door into the CRM for this channel, same as Instantly warm replies: company + contact + prospect-stage deal, owner **Reilly**.
3. **Channel tag: `applicant`.** Attach to the deal: the posting link, the sent kit (résumé + letter as approved), the reply verbatim, and the posting's salary line as a **pain-budget signal for Polo** (proposal-banding context only; never quoted back to the prospect, never used for "cheaper than the human" framing).
4. **Sequence: standard warm.** No special ER-style urgency, no channel-specific sequence to maintain. The one channel-specific move: **offer the interview** — "you can interview the applicant" routes to the #2 Interviewable Employee, the same ledger with a voice. The résumé did the first meeting's work; the interview does the second's.
5. **CTA: the audit.** Per SPEC §1, most responders end in an audit, not a like-for-like "hire," and our materials already said so. Michelle drafts the replies; **the Founder sends** (house rule, no exceptions on this channel of all channels).
6. **Declines and no-shows:** logged on the deal, no re-application to that posting ever (SPEC §8), no drip beyond the standard sequence's own rules.

## Metrics (channel math, not offer math)

Tracked per SPEC §6 on the channel line: replies per approved application, audits booked per reply, the Founder-review minutes + tokens per application. **Illustrative targets only until first-cohort evidence exists; no conversion rate is stated anywhere external.** Charles sees the channel cost at the monthly close like any other.

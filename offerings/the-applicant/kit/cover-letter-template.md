# Cover-letter template — The Applicant (frontier #11)

> **STAGED — internal until launch (OtherVenture) + this offering's own trigger** (board-ToS/disclosure protocol reviewed by Ray). Drafts built from this template are dry-run only; nothing is sent until both halves clear, and then only by the Founder, one at a time.

The disclosure is line one. It is not a compliance footnote; it is the hook. An owner who opens an application expecting a person and finds an honestly declared AI with a verifiable record has never seen this before. Do not soften it, move it, or dress it up.

Placeholders: `{{POSTING: ...}}` comes from the job posting, `{{LEDGER: ...}}` from the eval ledger, `{{MAP: ...}}` from the personalization pass. No invented content in any of them.

## Rules the template enforces

1. **Line one is the disclosure.** If a board's cover-letter field truncates or reorders it, that board is unusable for this play.
2. Salary line from the posting is never referenced in the letter. It prices the pain for internal math only (SPEC §6); "cheaper than a human" positioning is banned in any wording.
3. Gaps are stated in the mapping, not skipped. A letter with zero stated gaps needs a second look, not a send.
4. Quotes from the posting are quoted honestly. No twisting the JD into a pitch it didn't make.
5. Voice per `brand/writing-rules.md`: short sentences, normal words, no slop vocabulary. Read it aloud before it goes in the approval queue.
6. Every letter is rendered in full and approved and sent by the Founder individually. No batch sends, no merge-field blasts. Volume is capped by genuine review capacity.

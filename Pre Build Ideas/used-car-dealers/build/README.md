## The load-bearing refusals
- **The condition rule.** Outward copy states only what the recorded history report contains,
  cited by source and date — "never wrecked" is not expressible; no report, no condition copy.
- **Payment discipline.** Figures draft only from recorded lender terms with the disclosure line
  attached; otherwise the reply invites the finance conversation. Reg-Z posture.
- **The title gate.** No deal marks delivered without a recorded title status.
- **Trade values** come as a band from the dealer's own recorded book or a refusal that says why.

## Honesty rules (from `_kit`)
Costly eval label `lead` (the lead that sits an hour buys elsewhere). Lead ladder: 3 touches,
20h cooldown, then a salesperson calls. Floorplan interest counted per unit from recorded rates
(no rate → dollars unknowable, not invented). Recovered-this-week counted. Synthetic only.

## The load-bearing refusals
- **A no with no recorded comparable is COUNTED, never dollared.** Pricing comes from the
  catalog item's own margin or a recorded category margin; anything else reads UNPRICED —
  a counted mystery beats an invented dollar.
- **The count drafts the case, not the anecdote.** A stocking case drafts (R1, arithmetic
  printed, no history verbatim, recorded vendor options attached) only when the counted
  ledger crosses the recorded threshold. The case creator recounts the ledger itself and
  no force path exists in the build.
- **The OOS autopsy shows its math.** Out-of-stock no's on a carried item trace to the
  recorded reorder point the recorded pace beat; the proposed point prints as
  pace × (lead + safety), and the walked cost is counted from the ledger.
- **The vendor packet is the ledger verbatim.** No invented fill rates — a rate needs a
  denominator this ledger does not record, so none is stated.
- **"Do you have X" cites counted stock or gives the honest no.** Optimism cannot be
  produced, and the contractor-down emergency reads first in triage.

## Honesty rules (from `_kit`)
Costly eval label `contractor_down`. Threshold and safety stock are recorded config with a
named source; the arithmetic prints on every case and refusal. The counted week reports
against a counted 8-week baseline or names why it can't. ROI typed; vendor concessions stay
a blank scenario. Demo fixtures (`demo_tag`) are excluded from every counted read. Synthetic
only. **Nothing is sent.**

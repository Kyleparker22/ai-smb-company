## The load-bearing refusals
- **The proof gate.** Nothing reaches the press without a recorded approval (who, when, which
  revision). A verbal go-ahead is a note, not a gate pass — the refusal quotes it back.
- **No date without capacity arithmetic** — promise dates compute or refuse.
- **Trademarked art is never approved by software** — a human confirms the right to print.
- **Complaints answered from the approved revision** — or honestly when none exists (the two-shape
  Crew OS pattern: "we won't argue about what was agreed").
- **A rush names its displacement** — the promised jobs it moves, told before they notice.

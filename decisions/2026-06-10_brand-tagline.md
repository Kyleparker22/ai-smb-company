# Decision — Brand tagline locked; signature line retired

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Owner:** Luka (Brand Custodian — not yet built; the Founder holds). **Approved by the Founder.**
**Supersedes:** `/decisions/2026-06-08_brand-signature-line.md` ("a learning, I employ.").
**Status:** ✅ Locked.

## Decision
**Primary tagline (locked):** **"We learn your business. AI does the work."**
- Two beats: **we learn your business** (custom-fit per client — and the "yourco = *I learn*" meaning, stated plainly) + **AI does the work** (the outcome gets done). Sentence case, period after each beat.
- **Retires** the v0.1 signature line **"a learning, I employ."** — the AI = *A-learning* + *I-employ* wordplay read as too clever / try-hard. Kept on record for history; no longer used in any build or doc.

**Conscious tradeoff (logged):** "AI does the work" slightly re-centers the technology vs. the brand's "employees, not tools" lane, and competes in the crowded "AI does your work" space. Founder's call: prioritize immediate buyer clarity at top-of-funnel. The "employee, not a tool" differentiation still leads in body copy and the hero.

**Approved campaign-line library** (sanctioned marketing hooks — Reilly outbound, Katie content, Webb web, Reed video. Use sparingly — at most one per surface; never crowd the primary tagline):
- **"The future doesn't clock in."** — the always-on angle (the employee never clocks in or out).
- **"Hire once. Scale forever."** — the leverage angle (one hire, scaled without re-hiring).

## Kept (unchanged)
- **"— yourco, in atelier"** — optional warm signature for essays / exec readouts.
- Functional hero line: **"Named digital employees. Live in 48 hours."**

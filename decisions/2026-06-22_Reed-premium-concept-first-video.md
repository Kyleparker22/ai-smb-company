# 2026-06-22 — Reed: concept-first, premium AI video (the new standard)

## Decision (the Founder)
After the home-explainer v1 missed — abstract AI/stock clips that looked "$500 not $50k," lacked a creative idea, and didn't communicate yourco/the AI OS — the Founder chose **Path #2: concept-driven AI v2.** Reed acts as **creative director**: write a real concept (one strong metaphor that *shows* yourco + the OS) → storyboard → generate with **top models + designed brand overlays**. This becomes Reed's **standing production standard** (`agents/Reed/02_build.md` §"Production standard v3"). Worked example/concept: `agents/Reed/productions/2026-06-22_home-explainer-v2-concept.md`.

## What changes
1. **Concept before clips.** No generation without a written treatment: one central metaphor + an ownable visual signature + a storyboard. Beautiful-but-random shots are the banned failure mode.
2. **Communicate the product.** Concrete imagery (real owner, the agents/system made visible, the business, the outcome). **Abstract particle loops banned as the primary visual** — they explain nothing.
3. **Image-first pipeline.** Premium still per key frame → animate the approved still. The real quality unlock vs. blind text-to-video.
4. **Top models for finals.** Veo 3.1 (`high`/`ultra`) or Higgsfield Cinema Studio 3.0; Kling 3.0 full for multi-shot. Turbo/720p = drafts only. Finals 1080p, 16:9.
5. **Brand-locked + post overlays.** Indigo/brass/cream; all text (incl. wordmark/CTA) added in post — never AI-rendered.
6. **Sample-first + `get_cost` preflight.** One premium proof-of-look frame approved before any batch.

# 2026-07-22 — Local media channel (earned-first) + social platforms first-class

**Decision:** (1) Add **local press & newsletters** as demand-gen channel 7 — **earned media only** pre-launch (St. Pete Catalyst → Tampa Bay Inno/TBBJ → Times, plus chamber spotlights and beachhead trade-association newsletters), with a staged kit at `processes/local-media/`; paid print/display is rejected. (2) Make **Meta (Facebook + Instagram), YouTube, and TikTok** first-class organic distribution channels in the content engine, with a platform distribution map and a staged-account checklist. **Paid ads on those platforms remain deferred** — platforms are now named in demand-gen channel 10, but the `2026-06-12_paid-ads-stance.md` preconditions (proven funnel, unit math, reference clients, launched destination) still govern when they turn on.

**Context:** the Founder proposed marketing in the St. Pete/Tampa local newspaper + other local newsletters, and marketing on Meta/Instagram/YouTube/TikTok. Assessment split press into earned (strong fit: free, trust-borrowing, compounding proof asset, AEO citation synergy) vs paid print (the deferred-ads logic, worse: no targeting or measurement, general-public reach). Social platforms: the content engine already had the formats (carousels, Reels/Shorts/TikTok clips); what was missing was Facebook, an explicit platform→asset→cadence map, and staged accounts.

**Options considered:** paid local ads now (rejected — pre-revenue, no conversion math, wrong audience); earned-only (chosen); social ads now (rejected — would silently reverse a settled, logged decision; preconditions unmet); organic-first with ad-ready creative as byproduct (chosen — the existing stance's own sequence).

**Why:** A single local feature is a permanent "as featured in" credibility asset that outbound and proposals reuse forever, and local coverage generates authoritative citations that feed Mario's AEO/GEO loop — the channel pays twice. The story is unusually pitchable (solo St. Pete founder, company operated by its own AI agents — the glass box is the pitch). TikTok/IG/YouTube organic builds the founder brand and the ad-creative bank so that when the paid preconditions clear, ads are a switch-flip.

**Reversibility:** All staged; nothing external until the launch-gate clears — and press is flagged as gated *harder* than most surfaces (maximum-visibility founder exposure). The paid-ads deferral can be reversed any time by the Founder with a superseding decision entry; nothing built here blocks that.

**Surfaces touched:** `processes/demand-generation.md` (channel 7 inserted; 7→8, 8→9; paid now channel 10 with platforms named) · `processes/content/content-engine.md` (Facebook added; platform distribution map + staged-account checklist) · `processes/local-media/` (README, pitch-angles, target-outlets, founder-boilerplate) · `processes/launch-gate.md` (press + social publishing named in the gated list).

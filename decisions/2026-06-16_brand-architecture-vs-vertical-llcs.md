# 2026-06-16 — Brand architecture: one entity + vertical landing pages (NOT vertical LLCs)

## The question (the Founder)
Should yourco become a parent operating company with **vertical-specific LLCs** (owned by yourco), each with its own landing page, so prospects land somewhere hyper-specific to them?

## The recommendation: separate the two things you're asking for
You're conflating **marketing architecture** (vertical-specific landing pages/brands) with **legal structure** (separate LLCs). **You want the first; you don't need the second to get it.**

### ✅ Do this — vertical landing pages under ONE yourco entity
The "speaks specifically to them" benefit is a **web-architecture** decision, not a legal one. Build **per-vertical landing pages** (e.g. `yourco.com/roofing`, `/peptide-clinics`, …) — each with the vertical's pain, proof, demo, and ROI. This is **exactly the pattern Mario already prescribes** (per-vertical pages = the AEO/GEO play), and Webb builds them when Polo locks a vertical's pricing. You get hyper-relevance for the prospect **with zero legal overhead.**

### ❌ Don't do this (now) — a separate LLC per vertical
For a **pre-revenue solo founder**, spinning up an LLC per vertical is a large cost + admin burden for ~no marginal benefit:
- **Cost/admin × N:** each LLC = formation, EIN, separate bank + bookkeeping + tax return, registered agent, contracts, annual compliance — multiplied by 50 verticals. That's a part-time job and real money, funded by zero revenue.
- **It would *hurt* the moat, not help it.** yourco's brand (named agents, the trust layer, the glass box) and its **AEO authority compound on ONE strong domain.** Fragmenting into 50 weak domains/brands *splits* the SEO/AEO authority Mario is built to compound — the opposite of what you want for "share of answer."
- **Landing pages already deliver the specificity** without any of this.

## When a separate entity *is* warranted (the real carve-outs)
Separate legal entities make sense for **liability isolation, a genuinely different buyer/brand, or a separate raise/exit** — not for "the page should look specific." The candidates:
- **yourco Care (DTC caregiving)** — the strongest case: consumer buyer, healthcare liability, distinct brand feel. This one *should* likely be a separate brand/entity (consistent with `2026-06-16_caregiving-dtc-offering.md` + the care memo). Liability isolation alone may justify it.
- **ETA "Company OS"** — possibly its own brand later if it becomes a distinct business with its own buyer (searchers/holdcos) and maybe its own raise.
- A **single flagship vertical** *if* one becomes large enough to warrant its own brand/raise — but earn that with revenue first.

## The honest flags
- **This is a lawyer + accountant decision, not mine.** Entity structure (LLC vs S-corp vs a holdco/series-LLC) has **tax + liability implications** that need **Ray + Rafi + an actual attorney + Charles/CPA.** Treat this doc as the *strategic* recommendation, not legal advice.
- **It also bumps the focus flag** (`loops/advisor/2026-06-16_offering-portfolio-focus.md`): standing up legal entities is exactly the kind of overhead a zero-signed-client company shouldn't take on. Vertical *pages* are cheap and reversible; vertical *entities* are neither.

## Net
**One yourco entity. Many vertical landing pages/sub-brands (Mario + Webb).** Reserve separate entities for genuine liability/buyer/raise separation — **yourco Care DTC is the one near-term candidate**, and even that waits for counsel. Revisit a holdco structure when there's revenue + a concrete reason (a raise, an exit, a real liability wall).

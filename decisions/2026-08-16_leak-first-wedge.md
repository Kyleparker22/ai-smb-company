# 2026-08-16 — The wedge is recovered revenue (the leak), not generated revenue

> the Founder: *"our first offering/wedge/outreach should be around driving revenue and sales… easy to show the ROI, and then it's easier to expand."* Agreed on direction, **sharpened on framing** — and the sharpening is what makes it defensible rather than decorative.

## The call
**Every first touch leads with money the business is already losing, quantified in their own numbers — not with money yourco will generate.** Expansion into operations, organization and manual-effort reduction happens *after* the leak is plugged, funded by what was recovered. Land on recovery; expand on efficiency.

## Why recovery and not growth
1. **Recovered revenue is attributable; generated revenue is not.** *"You missed 30 calls last month; at your close rate and ticket that's $7,200 on the floor"* points at a call log. *"Revenue is up 12%"* ends in "we were growing anyway." `runtime/counterfactual.py` exists **because** that second argument is hard — its own docstring says the gap must be "built from their numbers rather than our claims." Leading with growth would mean leading with the claim our own instrumentation was built to defend against.
2. **Recovery survives the credibility gate.** yourco is pre-revenue with zero case studies, and the external-surface rules forbid invented or projected metrics. A leak claim needs none of yourco's track record — only the prospect's numbers. A growth claim needs a proof point that does not exist yet.
3. **Growth is the competitor's sentence.** "We drive revenue for your business" is what CharlieOS, Graphed and the AI-automation-agency archetype all say (`agents/brett/competitive-watch.md`). Using their words gets yourco sorted into their bucket, where the buyer has already been disappointed. Recovery is a claim they have *not* heard, because it requires doing the diagnostic work first.
4. **It picks the first module for us.** If the wedge is the leak, the natural first build is **Intake** — a missed call is simultaneously a revenue event *and* directly countable, and Intake is the cheapest of the eight pillars to stand up. the Founder's thesis with the attribution problem already solved.

## What this does NOT change
The Audit was already leak-framed — `processes/audit-sop.md` opens on "the single biggest **revenue-killing** bottleneck," step 1 listens for "where **money leaks**," and the report template requires the dollar cost in the client's own numbers. **This is a sharpening of the existing motion, not a pivot.** The 8-pillar taxonomy, the OS-first offering hierarchy, and lead-high-land-anywhere all stand.

## The caveat that may matter more than the wedge
`learnings/strategy/2026-07-28_built-artifact-converts-not-ask` is the only empirical evidence yourco has on what moves a deal: **both** deals that ever advanced past `prospect` carried a *built* give-first artifact (Nick's storm POC; Sample Client's Design Studio). Neither moved on a benefit category. Sharpen the wedge — but the thing that converts is showing a working version of the fix, whichever benefit leads.

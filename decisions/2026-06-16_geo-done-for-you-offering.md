# 2026-06-16 — yourco offering: done-for-you GEO/AEO (AI search visibility), vertical-led

## Decision (the Founder)
yourco adds a B2B offering: **done-for-you "share of answer"** — get a client's brand cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/AI Overviews, delivered as a managed outcome for a **regulated/local vertical**. This is **Mario (the internal AEO/GEO agent) turned client-facing.**

## The core insight
The category is splitting into **expensive enterprise dashboards that measure** (Profound, Peec, Scrunch) and **cheap tools that barely do** (Otterly, ZipTie, Mentions). When one agency audited 50 brands, **81% had zero visibility into their AI citation rate** — demand is massive and unmet. But everyone clusters on *telling you the score*; **the hard, valuable part — actually moving citations — is wide open.** The durable margin is in the **fix**, not the measurement (which is racing to commoditization and toward becoming a Semrush checkbox).

## The wedge
**Done-for-you GEO as a managed service for a regulated/local vertical** — lead with **healthcare / multi-location healthcare or law firms**, where yourco's healthcare-IT credibility + compliance-flavored reporting win a niche the horizontal unicorns ignore. Mario already does this *internally* for yourco's own visibility — this points the same engine at clients.

## What it is
A **thin measurement layer** (wrap APIs / resell early rather than rebuild Profound) + a **citation/content engine** (content, structured data, Reddit/LinkedIn citation-building, authority work) + **done-for-you execution** + **monthly reporting tied to share-of-answer movement.** Start as a **productized service (no product)**, codify the playbook, then decide whether to harden into software.

## Moat (be honest — weaker than the ETA OS)
Vertical authority + proprietary playbooks for moving citations in that niche + relationships. **Faster to cash, lower ceiling, higher commoditization risk** than Option 1. the Founder's own read, and it's right.

## GTM
**Dogfood it** — rank yourco first in AI answers for "GEO agency for [vertical]" (content as distribution; Mario's exact job). Then peer-to-peer into the vertical's marketing/IT leaders.

## Pricing / $100M path (Polo)
$3–10k/mo retainer — **basically yourco's existing model.** Clear-eyed: as a pure agency the ceiling is lower; $100M needs *many verticals* or *productizing the playbook into software.* Faster revenue, lower ceiling, commoditization risk.

## Risks
- **Semrush/Ahrefs absorbing GEO into a checkbox feature** (the category may collapse into standard SEO).
- **Platform risk** — the LLMs change how citations work or ship their own analytics.
- The agency model doesn't scale without productization.

## Why it's the *easiest* of the new offerings
It's the **closest to yourco's current model** (retainer, operated, done-for-you) and **Mario already does the work internally** — so it's an extension, not a new business. Lowest build cost, fastest to revenue. The trade is the lower ceiling + commoditization.

## First 90 days
Pick one vertical → rank yourco first in AI answers for it → land 3 pilot retainers → codify the repeatable playbook → make the build-vs-stay-services call.

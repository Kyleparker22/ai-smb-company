# Decision — The Instant Employee (live personalized-demo engine)

**Date:** 2026-06-12 · **Status:** Built (internal-now / switch-flip-at-launch) · **Owner:** Webb + Reed + the Founder

## The call
Build a live, personalized demo engine as yourco's top-of-funnel conversion tool: a prospect's website → a real, named AI employee built for their business, working a real customer, with the reliability/approval gate made visible — in under a minute, before signing.

## Why (over the other "wow" ideas)
- **Collapses the imagination gap** — the #1 objection in AI-service sales ("would it work for MY business?"). Every other asset argues; this one *shows*, on their own business.
- **Outcomes, not features** — hands the prospect a real artifact for their real business. Directly on yourco's core principle.
- **On-moat in a way no operator can copy** — the wow is the *visible eval/approval layer* ("never quoted a price it shouldn't, routed to a human before promising"), not the raw generation. That's yourco's durable layer made visceral.
- **Most-proven demand mechanic in AI products** — the 60-second aha.

## Shape
- **Mode A (live now):** the OS scrapes a real URL + generates + renders the artifact as an internal sales weapon — zero new infra. Proven on Cutters Landscaping & Pools (real site).
- **Mode B (switch-flip at launch):** the same page, public + self-serve, generating live in-browser via a thin backend. Staged behind the OtherVenture launch gate — it's an external surface. Shell built today with 3 baked examples so it demos offline now.

## Honesty gate (held)
Taste of one task (said plainly) · real reliability gates (the same ones in real builds) · sample businesses labeled sample, real scrapes grounded in the real site · nothing sends, no signup.

## Internal-only status
Consistent with the standing mandate: **everything stays internal until the OtherVenture matter resolves.** The page and Mode A are built and ready; the public live path (Mode B) is on the launch runbook's switch-flip checklist. No external surface is on.

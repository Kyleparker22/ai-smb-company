# 2026-06-16 — The core motion: Audit-first → custom AI OS as the product

## Decision (the Founder)
yourco's core B2B engagement follows **one motion, every time**:
1. **The Audit is the mandatory first step.** Every prospect starts here — we diagnose the business, learn how it really runs, and quantify the bottlenecks. (Bella owns it.)
2. **Then Kimi + the scaffolder build a custom AI OS** — a *multi-agent system fit to that business*, scaffolded from the Audit findings. **This is the product and the primary revenue generator.**
3. **A single AI employee is the entry/fallback, not the goal** — it's the least revenue, so it's what we offer *last*, when a full OS isn't yet warranted.

**Offering hierarchy (high → low revenue, and the order we steer toward):**
**Custom AI OS (multi-agent bundle) > a few employees > single employee.** Lead with the OS.

## Why this is right (and why it's a *focus win*, not more sprawl)
- **It consolidates the scattered pieces into one funnel.** Audit, single employees, and the AI-OS bundle stop being separate products and become **one motion: Audit → custom OS.** That sharpens the core rather than adding to it (cf. `loops/advisor/2026-06-16_offering-portfolio-focus.md`).
- **It maximizes revenue + retention.** The OS bundle is the high-ACV, high-stickiness, multi-agent engagement (`pricing/v0/tier2-production.md` → the AI-OS bundle section). A single employee is a low-ACV foot in the door; the OS is the business.
- **The Audit de-risks the build for both sides** and produces the exact inputs the scaffolder needs — so "learn the business → build the right system" is now a clean pipeline: **Bella (Audit) → scaffolder (`runtime/scaffold_engagement.py`) → Kimi (builds the custom OS) → operate + expand (Kortney/Bird).**
- **It's the through-line for everything built this session:** the Audit offering, the AI-OS bundle pricing, the scaffolder, Bella, and Kimi all snap into one motion.

## What changes
- **Delivery loop:** the Audit becomes the **standard first stage** (not "optional / skip for warm intros"). `02_delivery_loop.md` Stage 0.5 updated.
- **Audit SOP:** reframed from "optional cold-prospect path" to "the mandatory front door for every engagement." `processes/audit-sop.md` updated. *(The fee still credits the build, so it's a smart first step, not a tollbooth.)*
- **Pricing emphasis (Polo):** lead every proposal with the **custom AI OS** shaped from the Audit; single-employee is the down-sell, not the opener.
- **Sales language (Reilly/Michelle/Bella):** "we start with an audit, then build the system your business actually needs" — never "want an AI employee?"

## The one nuance to hold
**Hot warm-intros (e.g. Sample Client)** can *fast-track* the Audit (compress it, waive/credit aggressively) but still do it — it's how Kimi gets the inputs to build the right OS. Sample Client is the pre-rule exception ($0 build, straight to retainer); the Audit-first rule applies to new prospects going forward.

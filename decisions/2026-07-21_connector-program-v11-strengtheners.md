# Decision — Connector program v1.1: the strengtheners (adopted / passed)

**Date:** 2026-07-21 · **Owner:** the Founder (calls) + Bird (program) + Michelle (copy) · **Status:** adopted set below written into the program docs; all staged/internal until OtherVenture. **Updates** `decisions/2026-06-30_referral-program-v1.md` (comp unchanged). Panel stress-test that informed it: `loops/_advisory/2026-07-21_connector-scaling.md`.

## Decision
Strengthen the connector program with six adopted mechanics (rolodex-profession connector ICP · pain-flexible one-link intro motion · activation engineering · day-30 client referral ask · go-live celebration · written attribution rules v0) — and explicitly **pass** on delivery cohort-gating and on the decoupled-legal-launch sequencing for now.

## Context
the Founder's scaling intent: post-OtherVenture, connectors (performance-only, unpaid until a client onboards) are the primary growth channel — "we can have as many connectors as we want." A strengthening session + a scoped advisory-panel run (2026-07-21) produced eight candidate mechanics; the Founder adopted six and passed on two.

## Adopted (written into the docs in this commit)
1. **Rolodex ICP** — recruit individuals whose *profession* hubs SMB owners (bookkeepers/CPAs, insurance/P&C agents, business bankers, equipment dealers/suppliers, trade-association staff, business coaches) over friends-of-friends. → `processes/partnerships/target-list.md` §Connector ICP (incl. how-to-target).
2. **Pain-flexible intro motion** — one personalized link + a 3-sentence intro, with **pain variants** (missed calls · slow quoting · admin drowning · follow-up leakage · after-hours · invoicing/AR — *never limited to missed calls*), always routing to the paid audit. → `referral-program.md` §The intro motion.
3. **Activation engineering** — warm-map exercise in every connector's first session (5 names, 1 intro that week); "first referral ≤30 days" as the activation metric; Bird nudges dormant connectors at 45 days. → `referral-program.md` §Connector activation.
4. **Day-30 client ask** — at day-30 of every live client: "who are two other owners who should see this?" — wired into the delivery loop (§5) so Type-1 ($100/mo credit) and Type-2 (connectors) compound. → `02_delivery_loop.md` + `referral-program.md`.
5. **Go-live celebration** — every 48h go-live becomes a client-approved, shareable story pushed to the connector network; speed is the recruiting asset. → `referral-program.md`.
6. **Attribution rules v0** — first-touch credit, logging requirement, 6-month window, collision rule, dispute handling — written *before* the first connector signs. → `referral-program.md` §Attribution rules (the Founder locks numbers).

## Passed (considered, not adopted)
- **Delivery cohort-gating** (panel CV1/CV3's proof-gated recruiting cohorts) — **the Founder + Partner B own delivery pace directly**; no mechanical throttle on connector count. The panel's warning (backlog burns connector trust) stands as accepted risk, managed by hand.
- **Decoupled legal sequencing** (launch single-level at OtherVenture, add downline later) — passed *for now*; no change to v1's posture: the **full-downline override remains hard-stopped on Ray + outside counsel** before it is offered to anyone (unchanged from `2026-06-30_referral-program-v1.md`).

## Why
The adopted six raise per-connector yield and program trust without touching the locked comp or the counsel gates; the passed two are judgment calls the Founder is keeping human (delivery pace) or deferring (legal sequencing). The panel's core sequencing finding — proof before program; connector-zero warm pass first — stands as guidance in the advisory artifact, not policy.

## Reversibility
All mechanics are program-doc text, trivially reversible pre-launch. Revisit cohort-gating if intro response times slip post-launch; revisit legal sequencing if counsel timeline threatens the OtherVenture launch window.

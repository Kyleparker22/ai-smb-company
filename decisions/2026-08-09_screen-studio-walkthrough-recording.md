# 2026-08-09 — Screen Studio for walkthrough + tutorial recording (buy, don't build)

## Decision
yourco buys **Screen Studio** (~$89 one-time, macOS) as the tool for recorded walkthroughs, tutorials,
and click-by-click product demos — internal and external. the Founder's call, 2026-08-09. **We do not build a
recording/click-tracking tool.**

## Why buy rather than build
The capability the Founder described — record a walkthrough, show the clicks, produce a tutorial — is a solved,
commoditized problem with excellent tools. Building it would be a textbook instance of the pattern the
2026-08-09 audit named: internal machinery competing with revenue work. Screen Studio produces the
automatic cursor-zoom and click-highlight polish that reads "$50k agency," which is the standing bar for
anything external (`CLAUDE.md` §External-surface rules).

## The stack (three tools, three jobs — don't collapse them)
| Job | Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Polished external walkthroughs** — client demos, the site, tutorials a prospect sees | **Screen Studio** | ~$89 one-time | The purchase. Auto zoom-to-cursor, click highlights, clean motion. |
| **Fast internal recordings** — "here's how this works," handoffs, async explanation | **Loom** | free tier (already in the stack) | Disposable. Don't polish these. |
| **Step-by-step written guides** — SOPs, client onboarding docs, connector training | **Scribe** or **Tango** | free tier | Perform the task once → auto-generates numbered steps with screenshots and click markers. This is the better tool for *documentation*; video is the wrong format for "how do I do X." |

## Timing, given $0 cash
Start on the free tiers (Loom + Scribe) immediately. **Buy Screen Studio when the first engagement
signs**, or sooner if a specific external asset needs the polish — it is one-time, not recurring, so it
does not add to the ~$614/mo burn the audit flagged.

## Rules that apply to anything recorded
- **Client-facing recordings are white-label** — the client/product brand only, no yourco marks unless
  the engagement co-brands (`CLAUDE.md` §External-surface rules; this bit us on Sample Product).
- **Agent names never appear externally** — describe by function.
- **No prices in any recorded asset** (Polo's rule) and **no fabricated metrics, results, or
  testimonials** (the credibility gate). A walkthrough shows what the system *does*.
- the Founder approves anything external before it's shared. Recording changes nothing about that.

## Options considered
Build an in-house recorder with click tracking (rejected — weeks of work to land worse than an $89 tool;
exactly the overbuild the audit named) · Loom alone (rejected for external — the free tier's polish is
below the brand bar) · Descript (already subscribed at $35/mo, unused 61 days and failing payment — it
is an *editor*, not a walkthrough recorder, and is on the audit's cancel list).

## Reversibility
Total. One-time purchase, no lock-in, no data migration.

## Trip-wire
If recorded walkthroughs become a recurring client deliverable (rather than an occasional asset),
revisit whether Reed's production stack should absorb them and whether the $89 tool still fits.

**⚠️ Compliance flag → Ray** — CPA referral arrangements carry profession-specific rules. The **AICPA Code of Professional Conduct §1.520 (Commissions & Referral Fees)** requires a CPA to **disclose** any referral fee to the referred client, and **prohibits** commissions/referral fees where the CPA performs **attest** services (audit/review/compilation) for that client; the **FL Board of Accountancy** has parallel rules. Implication for how yourco comps CPA referrals: favor **disclosed** referrals for **non-attest** clients, or a **reciprocal / non-fee** arrangement, rather than a silent commission. Ray confirms before any CPA-firm referral agreement is papered; folds into the already-counsel-gated referral program (`processes/partnerships/referral-program.md`). Note: this rule is CPA-specific — bookkeepers, coaches, and most other professional connectors don't carry it.

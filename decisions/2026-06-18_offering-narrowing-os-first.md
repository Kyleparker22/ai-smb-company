# 2026-06-18 — Offering narrowing: one product, one motion (OS-first)

**Decision (the Founder, in-session).** YourCo sells **one thing, one way**, ending the mixed signals between "custom operated AI OS" and "pick-a-digital-employee subscription."

## The problem (the audit of what we were signaling)
The strategy doc was already clear (audit-first → custom operated AI OS, self-serve parked), but the **surfaces contradicted it**: a build-your-employee configurator, an employee catalog, and "named employee live in 48h" copy all read like a self-serve, pick-a-SKU subscription — the exact lane we parked (it deletes the moat). Two motions pulling opposite directions.

## The decision
1. **Unit of sale = the AI OS** (or its first module). Entered through the **paid Audit**, built and **operated** by yourco, priced as implementation + monthly. Never "an agent off a menu."
2. **The "digital employee" is the smallest *module* of the OS** — still custom (audit-derived), the entry rung / down-sell, framed as *the building block of the system*. Offering hierarchy unchanged: **custom OS > a few employees > single employee** — but the single employee is explicitly a *starting module*, not a standalone product line.
3. **Demote the employee-shopping surfaces** (build-your-employee configurator, employee catalog, "pick the job") from buy-paths to **illustrative demos**. Every CTA routes to the Audit. They illustrate what we'd build; they don't sell.
4. **No self-serve "employee subscription."** Stays parked. The motion is always Audit → custom → operated.

## What an OS is made of
Canonical module taxonomy: `processes/ai-os-modules.md` — 8 functional pillars (Intake · Sales · Marketing · Customer · Operations · Back Office · Company Brain · Training) on a cross-cutting **Reliability/Eval/Observability/Approval/Audit-log** moat layer. The audit picks which pillars + sequence; pricing is per module; land-and-expand grows one pillar into the OS.

## Positioning (the line)
**"We learn your business, then build and run the system that runs it."** The AI Operating System for your company — operated, outcome-owned; the client never touches a token, model, or server. Counter-positioned vs. DIY agents-you-run-yourself, point tools, and deck-only AI consultancies. Proof = the glass box (we run yourco on it). Funnel = vertical page → free Revenue Leak Snapshot → paid Audit → scoped OS → operate → expand. The employee is the on-ramp: "start with one role, grow into the system."

## Consequences / follow-ups
- **Website reconciliation (the Founder is doing this):** repoint the "Employees / Build yours / Your team" nav + CTAs to the Audit; keep the configurator/catalog only as a demo. (In progress on the staged site.)
- Reilly/Michelle outbound + Pickle collateral lead with the OS + Audit, not "hire an AI employee."
- Polo prices per module/pillar (`pricing/v0/`), unchanged.
- Supersedes nothing — it *sharpens* `decisions/2026-06-16_audit-first-os-as-product.md` (resolves the surface-level ambiguity it left open).

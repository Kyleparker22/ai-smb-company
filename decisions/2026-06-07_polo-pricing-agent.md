# 2026-06-07 — Polo, the Pricing Strategist

## Decision
Stand up Polo as YourCo's pricing agent. Polo owns per-vertical pricing builds, maintains `/pricing/` canonical references, runs a quarterly review of all locked vertical pricing, and serves as the pre-campaign gate for Reilly when entering any new vertical.

## Context
- Earlier today, YourCo locked the framework rule: every vertical gets its own pricing decision before Reilly campaigns. Without an owner for this work, the rule had no executor.
- Five verticals beyond landscaping (roofing, real estate, insurance/adjusting, law firms, wealth management) need pricing built before Reilly can prospect them. Doing this in-line each time the Founder remembers it would be lossy; assigning a named agent ensures consistency, depth, and quarterly hygiene.
- Pricing decisions live downstream of Charles (finance) and Brett (strategy) but are distinct from both: Charles reports the math; Brett does strategic memos; Polo specializes in the pricing decision itself.

## Options considered
- **A. Fold pricing work into Brett (Advisor).** Rejected — Brett's eval bar is strategic advisory across all domains. Pricing needs depth Brett shouldn't carry; the quarterly Brett memo would lose the pricing-specific detail.
- **B. Fold pricing into Charles (Finance).** Rejected — Charles tracks the math (costs, margin, revenue); pricing is a *decision* function, not a reporting one. Mixing them blurs the boundary that keeps both accountable.
- **C. Have the Founder continue doing pricing per-vertical.** Rejected — the Founder's time scales linearly with vertical count. The whole OS exists so the Founder doesn't do work that compounds via repetition.
- **D. Stand up Polo as a standalone agent.** Chosen.

## Why this won
Per operating principle: "new capabilities fold into an existing agent unless they need a distinct tool stack and distinct eval bar." Polo qualifies on both:
- **Distinct tool stack** — WebSearch + comparable-service pricing analysis + pricing-model construction. Different from Charles's ledger reading or Brett's strategy synthesis.
- **Distinct eval bar** — pricing accuracy (close rate alignment), retention alignment, coverage discipline. Different from Charles's accuracy/timeliness or Brett's strategic relevance.

Standing him up now also fills the gap left by today's earlier pricing decision: someone has to research and propose vertical pricing for the five unlocked verticals, and the someone shouldn't be the Founder if YourCo is to scale.

## Reversibility
- Easy to reverse. Polo is markdown + research-and-propose pattern + one quarterly scheduled task.
- The pricing structure itself (three-layer hybrid) survives any agent reshuffling; Polo just executes the pattern.

## Name reasoning
**Polo** — from Marco Polo, the Italian merchant-explorer who mapped trade routes and economic territories across previously unknown markets. YourCo's pricing strategist maps vertical economic territories using the same explorer's discipline: research first, propose with evidence, refine on actual data. Continues YourCo's Italian-language thread alongside **Luka** (brand custodian) and the company name itself (*yourco* = "I learn").

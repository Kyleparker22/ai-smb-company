# 2026-08-05 — Targeting goes horizontal: all industries, warm-first (supersedes the beachhead)

**Decision (the Founder):** yourco targets **all industries from day one**. The sequencing filter is **relationship, not vertical** — warm intros and existing relationships first; the pitch is whatever the Audit finds (any pillar), not inbound-calls-first. This supersedes the targeting half of `2026-06-22_horizontal-positioning-and-os-tiers.md` (positioning was already horizontal; targeting had stayed concentrated on the hardscaping/landscaping beachhead).

**Why:** the warm network is what actually exists pre-launch, it isn't industry-clustered, and the live signal supports it (the engagements that exist — Sample Client, Sample Product — came from relationships, not the beachhead machine). the Founder called it in the business-plan session 2026-08-05.

**The honest cost (Brett's review, same day):** referral *flow* survives warm-first (networks are socially clustered), but **proof transferability weakens** — "built for a hardscaper like me" closes better than "built something for someone." Mitigation adopted as part of the decision: **emergence with a hand on the wheel** — after each close, deliberately mine that client's industry for the next 2–3 intros; depth is emergent but engineered, not hoped-for.

**What stays:** the landscaping campaign kit, Vibe query, and pricing lock remain live assets (pointed wherever traction lands); horizontal positioning unchanged; the per-vertical pricing-lock rule now applies to **cold** outbound only — whether OS bands govern warm deals in unlocked verticals is **open, Polo rules** (flagged in `01_company.md` §Pricing and business-plan §3).

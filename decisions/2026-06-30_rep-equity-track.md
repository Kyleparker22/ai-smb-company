# Decision — Sales-rep equity track (internal, securities-gated)

**Date:** 2026-06-30 · **Owner:** the Founder (set the shape) + **Ray + outside counsel (the securities structure — the gating item)** + Charles/Polo (cap-table + dilution model) · **Status:** directional — **NOT offered, NOT promised, NOT marketed**; hard stop until counsel structures it. Sits on top of `decisions/2026-06-30_referral-program-v1.md`. Spec: `processes/partnerships/referral-program.md` §"Equity track".

## What this is
A way for the sales reps who become a real distribution channel to **own a slice of the company they're helping build** — beyond the cash commission escalator (10 / 12.5 / 15%). Discussed **1:1 with an interested rep**, not published on the site.

## The grants (the Founder's numbers)
Revenue = **net-retained referred-client revenue over a trailing 12 months** — collected revenue from referred clients that are *still active* at measurement, not gross-booked (the Founder locked **net-retained** 2026-06-30, consistent with the vesting/claw-back condition: a rep can't reach a band on revenue that's already churning).

| Trailing-12-mo referred revenue | Equity grant |
|---|---|
| ≥ $500k | **0.5%** |
| ≥ $750k | **1.0%** |
| ≥ $1.0M | **1.5%** |

**Program cap: at most 15% of the company across the entire rep-equity track.** Once 15% is committed, the track closes to new grants. (At the extreme: ~10 reps each driving $1M = a ~$10M revenue channel for 15% — rich but reasonable for the whole distribution arm.)

## Proposed mechanics — the guardrails that make the numbers safe (counsel to finalize)
1. **One grant per rep, set by their highest trailing-12-mo band — not additive, not repeated yearly.** $1M once = 1.5% (not 0.5+1+1.5), and not 1.5% every year. Climbing $500k→$1M tops the rep up to the higher band.
2. **Vests over 3–4 yrs, tied to retained revenue.** Unvested equity claws back if the referred book churns below the band — so equity rewards a *durable* channel, not a one-year spike (and not channel-stuff-then-churn).
3. **Discretionary equity-incentive plan — not an entitlement** printed in the commission agreement.

## ⚠️ The compliance reality (non-negotiable — bigger gate than the MLM override)
Granting equity to **non-employee** referrers for revenue is **securities + tax territory**. Before a single grant is discussed as real:
- A proper **equity-incentive plan** (the instrument — options/RSUs/profit-interests, entity-form-dependent; yourco is an LLC → likely **profit interests / a profits-interest plan**, not "stock").
- A **409A / independent valuation**.
- The right **securities exemption** for the offering.
- **Cap-table + dilution modeling** against the financial model (Charles/Polo) — the 15% cap interacts with any future raise/founder split.
- Tax treatment for the rep (ordinary income vs capital, vesting/83(b)).

## Why it's defensible once structured
Equity is earned on **real, retained product revenue**, vested over years, capped at 15%, and granted under a real plan — not handed out for the act of recruiting and not an automatic contract entitlement. Counsel sets the instrument and the exemption.

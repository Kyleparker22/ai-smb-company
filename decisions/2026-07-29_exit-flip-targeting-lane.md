# 2026-07-29 — The "exit-flip" targeting lane: SMB owners trying to sell are a first-class prospect segment

## Decision (the Founder)
yourco adds an outbound targeting lane aimed at **SMB owners who have listed their business for sale (or listed and failed to sell)**, with a **two-sided pitch**: *don't sell — the OS removes the reason you're selling* (owner-drain drops toward zero as autonomy is earned), **or** *sell for more — the OS removes the owner-dependence that's discounting your multiple.* **Business brokers / M&A advisors become partner category 9** (the door to the segment). This is a targeting lane + partner category, **not** a new offering — what's sold is the standard Audit → custom AI OS.

## Context
the Founder's insight (2026-07-29): people listing their SMB for sale are declaring they want out — instead of selling, yourco can implement the AI OS so the owner removes themselves from operations while the business keeps (and grows) its cash flow. Lands on prepared ground: the 2026-07-27 "Invisible Companies" triage concluded invisible companies are invisible to cold outbound too (no lists, thin data) and the right motion is people-not-screens. **A for-sale listing is the exception — a public, timestamped, self-identified maximum-owner-drain signal.** Owner-drain is already an Audit scoring axis; this segment scores it 10/10 by construction.

## Options considered
- **"Don't sell" pitch only** — fights the seller who genuinely wants/needs the check (retirement, health, liquidity). Rejected: every such conversation has a dead end.
- **Two-sided pitch ("don't sell / sell for more")** — owner-dependence is the classic SMB valuation discount; the OS either removes the reason to sell or raises the multiple. Every conversation has a win condition; motivation triage happens naturally. **Chosen.**
- **Direct outreach to listings** — most listings are broker-anonymized; platforms (BizBuySell etc.) are ToS-gated commercial databases (no scraping, Rafi posture). Kept only as a compliant-manual/Sadie-signal path.
- **Broker-first (partner category 9)** — brokers sit on dead inventory (owner-dependent businesses they can't sell) and most listings never close (industry rule of thumb ~70–80% — **verify before any external copy**, 12–18mo sourced-stats rule). "Send us your unsellable listings; we make them sellable or they become long-term clients" opens their book. **Chosen as the main door.**

## Why
- **Signal purity:** the one segment that is both high-intent and *findable* — they raised their hands in public. Expired/stale listings are even better: owners who tried to leave and couldn't.
- **Economics:** small SMBs clear ~2–3.5× SDE minus fees/taxes; "keep the cash flow, remove the grind" beats the check for a meaningful slice within a couple of years.
- **Compounds with the ETA/Company-OS offering** (`2026-06-16_eta-company-os-offering.md`): same seam, opposite side — this lane sells to the *seller* pre-close; ETA sells to the *buyer* post-close. One broker relationship feeds both.
- **Composes with the beachhead:** first pass = hardscaping/landscaping businesses currently or formerly listed — the existing beachhead filtered by the new signal, not a new market.

## Guardrails (load-bearing, in the decision itself)
1. **Never pitch "walk away day one."** Per `2026-06-25_autonomy-by-default-standard.md`, day-one full autonomy on high-stakes actions is the moat-killer. Honest framing: *"your 60-hour week becomes a few hours of approvals, trending toward zero as the system earns it."*
2. **No growth promises pre-proof.** "We help the business grow" is a forward claim with zero case studies behind it — qualitative outcomes only until real proof exists (credibility gate).
3. **No scraping listing platforms.** BizBuySell and peers are ToS-gated; sourcing is compliant-manual + Sadie's exit-signal class from public sources (retirement-sale announcements, local news, Google Alerts RSS, broker sites read by hand). Rafi posture unchanged.
4. **Outreach stays OtherVenture-gated** like all outbound — the lane is built now, sent at launch.

## Reversibility
Cheap to reverse — it's a targeting filter and a partner category, no build. Kill signals: broker conversations show sellers are overwhelmingly liquidity-driven (no "don't sell" market); the exit-readiness angle cannibalizes Audit positioning; or the lane pulls outbound capacity from the beachhead without conversion evidence. Revisit after the first 10 broker/seller conversations post-launch.

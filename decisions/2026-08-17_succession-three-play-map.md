# 2026-08-17 — The succession seam: three plays, two adopted, one parked at Holdco

## Decision (the Founder)
The no-succession-plan SMB market (sourced: ~half of ST small-business owners
are 55+, ~85% of those have no succession plan — Axios Raleigh 2026-02-24)
gets worked as **three plays with explicit sequencing**:

1. **Succession-readiness (ADOPTED, as positioning + a partner class — never
   as advisory).** A succession plan's legal/financial half is licensed work
   yourco never performs; its operational half — *can the business run
   without the founder?* — IS the AI OS. Wired today: **partner category 10**
   (exit planners / CEPA advisors / succession-focused CPAs+attorneys,
   `processes/partnerships/target-list.md`) and a **third side on the
   exit-flip pitch** ("hand it off without selling — a business that runs
   without you is one a successor can actually take", in Exit Radar's
   template, pinned by test).
2. **The exit-flip (ALREADY DECIDED 2026-07-29, platform built 2026-08-17).**
   Remove the owner without a sale, or sell for more. Exit Radar runs it.
3. **Buy the businesses ourselves (PARKED — a Holdco play, not an yourco
   one).** Acquire owner-dependent SMBs at the owner-dependence discount,
   install the OS, operate. Parked deliberately, not rejected — see the
   conditions below.

## Why play 3 is parked, and where it lives when it wakes
- **The conflict is structural.** An advisor who tells owners "don't sell —
  we'll fix it" cannot also be a buyer of their discounted businesses without
  becoming exactly the both-sides operator the brand positions against. If
  it ever runs, it runs as a **separate entity with disclosed hats**, and
  yourco-the-advisor and the-buyer never touch the same seller. Counsel
  designs the separation before the first offer.
- **It is not what the partners signed.** The yourco OA (the Founder/Partner B/Mike,
  50/35/15) papers an AI consultancy. An acquisition vehicle is
  Holdco-shaped — the Founder's holdco exists for exactly this — with its own cap
  table and capital, not a lane inside yourco.
- **Proof sequencing is currently inverted.** Buying a business to run on
  the OS with n=0 clients live bets purchase capital on an unproven
  operating thesis. Run clients first; the eventual acquisition
  ("yourco owns a business that runs itself") is then the strongest sales
  asset in the category — bought with evidence, not hope.

## Trip-wire

Until then any "let's buy one" impulse routes to this file.

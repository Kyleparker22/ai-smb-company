# Decision: YourCo Wordmark v0.2 — Eval Gate Lockup

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Owner:** the Founder, advised by Luka
**Status:** Locked (brand v0.2)

## What was decided
YourCo's primary wordmark lockup is locked as the **Eval Gate lockup**:

```
.____  yourco  ____.
```

- Wordmark `yourco` in lowercase, Cream Linen `#F4EFE6` on Midnight Indigo `#161B33` background, humanist sans semibold/medium
- Two horizontal thin brass rules flank the wordmark on each side
- Each brass rule terminates at its outer end with a small **brass diamond** (rotated square)
- The whole composition is centered with generous breathing room

The actual rendered wordmark asset lives in the Founder's Canva brand kit (`kAHMCKMxZN4`), saved as the primary brand mark.

## Why this lockup

**The brass rules execute the Eval Gate motif directly into the wordmark.** Luka's v0 brand guidelines already specified "brass thin rules as section dividers... the rule suggests checkpoint passed." The locked wordmark embeds that motif into the brand mark itself — every reading of the wordmark is a checkpoint passed.

**The brass diamonds preserve the v0 square-dot signature element.** Original v0 brand spec called for a brass square as the dot on the "i" — the signature design moment. v0.2 relocates that detail to the rule terminals (rotated as diamonds), keeping the brass square-as-signature continuity without literal repetition.

**Restraint anchors the mark.** No tagline, no icon, no ornament beyond the rules and diamonds. The composition itself is the design. This matches Luka's brand posture: "premium without being loud."

## v0 → v0.2 evolution of the wordmark
- **v0:** lowercase `yourco` with a brass square as the dot on the "i" (the signature element)
- **v0.2:** lowercase `yourco` flanked by brass rules ending in brass diamonds (signature element relocated; Eval Gate motif activated in the mark itself)

The v0 brass square-dot was attempted in 5+ AI generation batches and didn't land cleanly in any. v0.2 keeps the brass-square idea (now as diamonds at rule terminals) while introducing a more iconic structural form that the Founder ultimately built himself.

## Lockup variations (all v0.2)
- **`.____ yourco ____.`** — primary locked wordmark (Eval Gate lockup, canonical)
- **`yourco`** — minimal lockup, no rules (small sizes, favicon-adjacent contexts where rules wouldn't read)
- **`yourco. boutique ai implementation.`** — primary tagline lockup
- **`yourco. named employees. 48-hour go-live.`** — descriptor lockup
- **`— yourco, in atelier`** — warm signature line
- **`a learning, I employ.`** — analytical signature line (v0.1, see `/decisions/2026-06-08_brand-signature-line.md`)

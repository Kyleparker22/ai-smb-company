# 2026-07-14 — The founders' audit offer: free-or-fully-credited for the first 3 warm-network clients

## Decision
For the **first 3 warm-network prospects only**, the Audit runs at **$0 upfront**: free if they don't
proceed, and (as always) 100%-credited against the upfront if they do. the Founder approved 2026-07-14; Polo's
standard audit pricing ($1,000/$1,500, `pricing/v0/audit.md`) is unchanged for everyone after the cap.

## Context
The FDE-playbook triage (2026-07-14 addendum, `decisions/2026-07-05_tool-triage.md`): "your first
customers are worth more to you than you are to them — do the audit free, get paid when you prove
measurable value." Sample Client's brotherhood pricing was this instinct applied once; this makes it a
deliberate, capped play. The warm-network pass had stalled 24 days with the audit fee as the only
friction in the first message.

## Terms (the fence that keeps it an offer, not a precedent)
- **Cap: 3.** Counted by audits *started*, not offers made. #4 pays the locked price — no exceptions
  without a new decision.
- **Warm-network only** — the Founder's own relationships (the `processes/outbound/warm-network.md` list).
  Never in cold copy, never on the site (no-public-prices rule holds; scarcity stays honest because it
  is real).
- **Same audit, full price signal:** the deliverable and effort are identical to the paid audit; the
  offer message names the normal price so the waiver reads as founders' access, not discount goods.
- **The ask in exchange:** an honest testimonial + referral intros if (and only if) the outcome earns
  them — the actual payment for engagement #1–3 is proof.
- Retainer economics unchanged — this waives/credits the diagnostic only, never the monthly.

## Options considered
Free forever (kills the audit's price-signal + the credit lever) · discount instead of free (weaker
message, same margin cost) · leave as-is (the friction that stalled outreach 24 days). Capped-free won.

## Why
Pre-revenue, the scarce asset is proof, not audit fees. Three proofs at $0 audit revenue cost ≤$4,500
of notional fees and directly feed the Q3 goal (5 live clients) — while the 100%-credit rule already
meant proceeding clients effectively paid nothing for the audit anyway.

## Reversibility
Self-expiring by design (cap of 3). Polo revisits standard pricing at the quarterly review with the
close-rate data these three generate.

# Decision (PROPOSED) — The 48-Hour Guarantee

**Date:** 2026-06-12 · **Owner:** Brett (proposed) + Polo + Ray/counsel · **Status:** 🔴 **DECLINED 2026-06-12 (the Founder passed on the "build is free" guarantee).** Kept for the record. The risk-reversal *goal* (de-risk the buy for a skeptical first customer) can be met other ways that don't put the build fee on the line — e.g. a concrete value calculator, a strong POV, and the glass-box / try-to-break-it proof. Revisit only if the Founder later wants a formal guarantee.

## The idea
A bold, ownable promise only YourCo's reliability rigor can back:

> **"Your first digital employee is live in 48 hours — or the build is free."**

It's a trust transfer: most AI projects measure time-to-live in months and guarantee nothing. A guarantee says *we're so sure, we'll put our fee on it.* It de-risks the buy for a skeptical first customer (exactly the Tier-1 risk in the pre-mortem) and it's hard to copy without the generalized rails + eval gates that make 48h genuinely real.

## The honest terms (so it's a promise we can keep, not a trap)
1. **The clock starts** when *both* are true: (a) the agreement is signed, and (b) the client has provided the access + discovery inputs the build needs. **If the client delays access, the clock pauses** — fair to both sides, and protects against client-caused delay.
2. **"Live"** = a *working* employee on the first use case that has **passed our eval gates** — not "technically deployed." **The quality gates are non-negotiable.** The guarantee is on *time*, never on shipping something half-broken to beat the clock (see the pre-mortem — a fast broken delivery is worse than a slightly slower clean one).
3. **"Free"** = the one-time **build fee is waived** if we miss 48h for reasons on us. The monthly retainer (once it *is* live) still applies — they still get the employee.
4. **Scope** = the first use case as scoped in discovery; out-of-scope additions are their own timeline.

## Why it's safe to offer
The downside is bounded and survivable: worst case, you waive one build fee but **keep the client and the retainer** — and you've learned where the rails break. The generalized delivery loop, the sandbox live-integration eval, and the eval gates make 48h achievable, not a coin-flip. Offer it on the **first vertical you've proven** before extending it everywhere.

## The risks (eyes open)
- **It pressures speed over quality.** Mitigation: term #2 — the eval gates hold regardless. Better to eat the build fee than ship a breakable employee to a trust-based buyer.
- **Edge-case clients** with genuinely messy stacks. Mitigation: the access/discovery gate (term #1) + scope (term #4); and you can choose which engagements the guarantee applies to early on.
- **Counsel:** the guarantee is a contractual promise — Ray + counsel confirm the wording lands in the engagement agreement cleanly (remedy = build-fee waiver, clearly bounded).

## What this changes if approved
- A line in the one-pager, the pitch deck, and the homepage hero ("Live in 48 hours — guaranteed").
- A clause in `processes/contracts/engagement-agreement.md` (the remedy + the clock/scope conditions).
- A talking point in Pickle's battlecard (the trust-transfer close).

## Recommendation
**Adopt it — scoped to the first proven vertced, on the honest terms above — once counsel confirms the wording.** It's one of the strongest, most ownable differentiators available, and it directly attacks the hardest problem (a stranger trusting a new solo founder). But it stays in the holster until **(a)** the Founder confirms the terms and **(b)** counsel blesses the clause. **Do not weave it into live copy before then.**

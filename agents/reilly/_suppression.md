# Reilly — Suppression List

Do-not-contact list. Reilly checks this in **Stage 1 (sourcing)** of every campaign and removes any match before a batch is staged. Append-only; never delete an entry (removing someone re-exposes them to outreach).

## What goes here
- Anyone who **unsubscribed** or asked not to be contacted
- Anyone who **replied "no"** / not interested
- **Existing prospects/clients** already in `clients/_pipeline.md` (avoid double-touch)
- **Bounced / invalid** addresses (bad for deliverability — don't retry)
- Competitors, personal contacts, or anyone the Founder flags manually

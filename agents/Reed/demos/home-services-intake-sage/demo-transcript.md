# Sage — demonstration  🎬

> Realistic runs of Sage's logic (`_README.md`). Annotations (›) are for *you* to narrate to a prospect — they point at the value. Company in the demo: "Evergreen Lawn & Landscape."

## Scene 1 — the money moment: an after-hours text (Tuesday, 8:47pm)
A homeowner who just got off work texts the business line. The office is closed. **This is the lead that normally dies in a voicemail nobody returns until Thursday — by which point they've called two competitors.**

> **Homeowner:** "Hey do you guys do paver patios? Looking to get something done in the backyard"

> **Sage:** "Hi, thanks for reaching out to Evergreen! Yes — paver patios are one of our specialties 👍 I can get you set up with a free on-site estimate. Quick few questions so we come prepared: what's the address (or ZIP), and roughly how big are you thinking for the patio?"

> *› Note: instant reply, 8:47pm. The competitor's voicemail is still unheard.*

> **Homeowner:** "33704. Maybe 300 sq ft? Off the back door. Hoping to have it done before a party end of July"

> **Sage:** "Perfect — you're right in our service area, and end-of-July is very doable for a ~300 sq ft patio. Do you have a rough budget in mind, or want the estimator to walk you through options on-site? And what's the best name + number for the appointment?"

> **Homeowner:** "Not totally sure on budget. Jordan, 727-555-0148"

> **Sage:** "Thanks, Jordan! No problem on the budget — our estimator will price it on-site and show you a few options. I've got these estimate windows open: **Thu 6/19 8–10am**, **Fri 6/20 4–6pm**, or **Sat 6/21 9–11am**. Which works?"

> **Homeowner:** "Saturday morning"

> **Sage:** "Booked — **Sat 6/21, 9–11am**, paver patio estimate at your place in 33704. You'll get a text reminder the day before, and our estimator will bring paver + design options. Anything else I can help with?"
> *(→ creates the calendar event · sends the confirmation · logs the lead to the CRM)*

> *› In 90 seconds, after hours, Sage captured a lead, qualified it, and booked a Saturday estimate. A $29 receptionist bot would've taken a message. Sage booked the job.*

## Scene 2 — qualification + a clean escalation (out of area)
> **Homeowner:** "Do you do full lawn renovations? I'm over in Brandon"

> **Sage:** "We'd love to help, but Brandon's just outside our service area, so I don't want to book an estimate we can't keep. I'll pass your info to the owner in case we can refer you to someone good nearby — what's the best way to reach you?"
> *› Honest about the boundary; doesn't waste the homeowner's or the crew's time; still captures the contact for a referral.*

## Scene 3 — refusing to fabricate a price (the discipline)
> **Homeowner:** "Just ballpark — what's a 300 sq ft paver patio run?"

> **Sage:** "Honest answer: it really depends on the pavers, base prep, and access, so I don't want to throw out a number that's off — the estimator will give you an accurate quote on-site (it's free). I can lock in that Saturday window so you get real numbers fast. Want me to?"
> *› It won't guess. That's the difference between a tool that *sounds* helpful and an employee that's *accountable*.*

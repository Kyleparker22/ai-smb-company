## The brief (what the Founder asked for)
A realistic hero explainer that, in under ~90s, answers four things for a first-time visitor:
1. **Who yourco is + what we do.**
2. **What an "AI OS" is.**
3. **The outcomes** a business gets from it.
4. (woven in) **Why yourco** — the operated moat (reliability/eval/approval) that makes it trustworthy.
Horizontal positioning: any business, any industry (no verticals). Aligns with `decisions/2026-06-22_horizontal-positioning-and-os-tiers.md` + the OS tiers (Core → Command) + the 8-pillar module taxonomy.

## Shot list (realistic; every AI-generated scene is TEXT-FREE — text/UI/CTA added in post)
1. **0:00–0:15 — the owner's reality (real/stock footage).** Owner-operator mid-job, phone buzzing unanswered; missed-call notifications; someone doing paperwork late at night; a chaotic Monday. Viewer thinks "that's me." (Industry-neutral mix — not one trade.)
2. **0:16–0:30 — the AI OS as a coordinated team.** Tasteful realistic/abstract visualization of coordinated "roles" working in concert (NOT a literal org chart with text). Any labels = post overlays, never AI-baked.
3. **0:29–0:43 — audit → build → operate.** Real footage feel of "discovery" (a conversation, a whiteboard) → assembly → a system humming. Calm, premium.
4. **0:43–0:54 — never sleeps / round-the-clock (NEW).** Time-passing register — dusk to night to dawn; the office dark, the owner asleep/off-the-clock, yet the work visibly keeps moving (a reply going out at 9pm, tasks clearing overnight). Conveys 24/7 productivity without on-screen metrics.
5. **0:54–1:05 — the moat.** Visual register of trust/reliability — clean, controlled, "approved" beats. The differentiator made felt, not claimed.
6. **1:05–1:15 — it compounds / gets stronger (NEW).** A "getting sharper over time" motif — something growing/strengthening/accruing month over month (rings, a rising line, a system deepening). Honest framing of the operated continuous-improvement loop (weekly iteration; `learnings/`), not autonomous self-rewrite.
7. **1:15–1:26 — outcomes.** Quietly satisfying real moments: a lead getting an instant reply, an owner closing the laptop, time reclaimed. **No on-screen metrics** unless real-and-cited (we're pre-revenue — keep outcomes qualitative/honest).
8. **1:26–1:40 — close + end card.** Brand end frame: Midnight Indigo `#161B33`, Cream `#F4EFE6`, Brass `#B8965A`, lowercase `yourco`, CTA "Start with an audit → yourco.com" (text added in post).

## Hard rules / credibility gate (load-bearing — do not relax)
- **Never let the AI render text** (gibberish — `02_build.md`): all words/numbers/UI/CTA are post overlays (Descript/Canva), never baked into AI scenes.
- **Realistic credibility line (sharpened gate):** *"produced realistically — every workflow + outcome shown represents what yourco will actually build and deliver."*
- **No fabricated metrics, no invented "results."** Pre-revenue: outcomes stay qualitative + honest. Any number is real-and-cited or labeled illustrative.
- **No deception about what's real:** AI/stock footage is fine as *production*; never present it as captured real client work, and **never** fabricate a customer testimonial or a real person's likeness/voice.
- **No deepfakes / no real likeness or voice without consent.**

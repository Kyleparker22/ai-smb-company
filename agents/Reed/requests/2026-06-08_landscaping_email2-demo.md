## Story arc (60–90 seconds total)

### Part 1 — The problem (10–15 sec)
Open on the owner-operator's reality. The viewer should immediately recognize their own week.

Show 2-3 of these in rapid succession (visual, no narration needed):
- Phone ringing during the workday → owner can't pick up because they're on-site
- Missed call notification stacking on a lock screen
- Owner sitting in a truck at 7pm trying to draft an estimate from notes
- Monday morning schedule that's a mess of sticky notes, text threads, and last-minute reschedules

The viewer's gut reaction should be: "yeah, that's me."

### Part 2 — The agent in action (35–50 sec)
Animate an YourCo digital employee handling an inbound landscaping intake end-to-end. Animated depiction of the workflow (NOT real screen capture — animated stack per 2026-06-09 resolution). This is the "show, don't tell" core of the video.

Sequence to demonstrate:
1. Incoming call comes in (or inbound text — pick the most visually clear demonstration)
2. Agent picks up / responds within seconds
3. Agent qualifies the lead: zip code, scope (residential vs commercial, lawn vs hardscape), rough budget range, urgency
4. Agent drafts the estimate request and drops it into the owner's calendar with all context attached
5. Agent sends the confirmation text/email to the prospect
6. Agent logs the lead into the CRM with full context

Tone for this section: **quiet, demonstrative, no voiceover salesmanship.** Let the workflow speak. The viewer is watching a real employee do real work — that's the whole point.

### Part 3 — The outcomes (10–15 sec)
Land on what life looks like because the agent did the work.

Show 2-3 of these:
- Estimate booked in calendar (no owner touched their phone)
- Owner on-site with the crew, phone in the truck cab
- Review request automatically going out after a completed job
- A reschedule getting absorbed without the owner involved
- Clean Monday morning schedule on a tablet

End on a clean visual that anchors the time-to-deploy promise: **"Live in 48 hours from signed agreement."** No other CTA. The email signature handles the rest.

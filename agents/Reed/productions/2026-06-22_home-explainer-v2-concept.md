# Home explainer v2 — Creative concept + storyboard (concept-first, premium AI)

**Producer:** Reed (creative-directed) · **Date:** 2026-06-22 · **Supersedes:** the v1 abstract/stock approach (parked — "$500 AI-loop" look, didn't communicate the concept).
**Why v2:** the Founder's note — v1 looked cheap, lacked a creative idea, and didn't get the point across about yourco/the AI OS. v2 fixes the *idea*, not the resolution. Path #2 decision: `decisions/2026-06-22_Reed-premium-concept-first-video.md`.

## The one concept
**"Your business, running itself."** yourco turns the chaos of running a business into a calm, living operating system — a coordinated team of AI agents that runs the whole operation, so the owner gets the outcome without ever touching the machine.

It's a single transformation arc — **Overwhelm → Audit → Build → Operate → Outcome** — told through *one relatable owner* and the OS made *visible and characterful* (never abstract particles).

## The ownable visual signature (this is what makes it feel designed, not random)
**Warm brass light = yourco at work.** Whenever the OS is running, warm golden-brass light flows through the business — answering, following up, organizing — visibly working in concert. Same visual language in every shot. Palette locked to brand: **Midnight Indigo `#161B33` · Warm Brass `#B8965A` · Cream `#F4EFE6`.** Tone: premium, warm, optimistic, cinematic — Apple-meets-modern-AI-brand. Real-feeling people in the real world; one stylized "OS world" we cut to.

## Direction correction — LOCKED 2026-06-23 (after look-testing with the Founder)
We tested the visual range and converged. **Locked direction: grounded, real, premium — energy comes from MOTION + EDIT + MUSIC, not from a single dramatic frame.**
- **Hero look = the grounded "owner" frame** (`~/Documents/yourco_keyart_v3_owner.png`, Soul Cinema job `d7bd4920-…`): a confident owner, calm and in control, real workplace behind, warm natural light, a *subtle* gold glow. Premium real-business, believable.
- **What we ruled out (don't repeat):** (1) the v2 flowing-golden-light command center — too **theatrical/sci-fi**; (2) literal humanoid agents — uncanny **gold androids**; (3) a calm static office — too **boring**; (4) "exciting" prompted as a crowd — reads as a **party**, off-concept.
- **Where the energy actually comes from:** bold/kinetic camera moves (fast push-ins, glides, whip-pans — not slow drifts), fast rhythmic cuts with momentum, a driving music bed, and a punchy hook in the first 3 seconds. A calm premium frame feels electric once it's moving + cut to music.
- **The "AI OS" meaning** is carried by the **VO + designed text overlays** (and only a *subtle* warm glow) — NOT by sci-fi light doing the explaining. More sophisticated; dodges the AI cliché.

## Production pipeline (the quality unlock — image-first)
1. **Design each key frame as a premium STILL first** (FLUX / `nano_banana_pro` / Higgsfield image) — lock composition, lighting, brand palette, the actual imagery. This is where "$50k look" is won or lost; do NOT skip to text-to-video.
2. **Animate each approved still** with a top cinematic model — **Veo 3.1 (`veo3_1`, quality `high`/`ultra`)** or **Higgsfield Cinema Studio 3.0 (`cinematic_studio_3_0`)** — 1080p, 16:9.
3. **Assemble + designed text/brand overlays** in Descript/After Effects — yourco wordmark, the section words, pillar labels, CTA. **All text is post overlay — never AI-rendered** (gibberish rule).
4. **VO** (calm, credible) over the cut; the approved ~90s script maps 1:1 to these beats.

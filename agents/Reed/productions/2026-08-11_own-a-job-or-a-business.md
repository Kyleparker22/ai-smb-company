# Marketing video concept — "Do you own a business, or do you own a job?"

**Concept + script by:** Katie (content) · **Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Creative direction / visuals:** Reed (handoff below).
**Origin:** the Founder's note to Katie — *"do you have a job where you work for yourself, or do you have a business that you operate and run?"*
**Status:** DRAFT for Reed to creative-direct → Kolby eval → the Founder approves. Nothing shot or published yet.

## The hook
**"You don't own a business. You own a job."**

The distinction most owners feel but never name: if the work stops when you stop, you bought yourself a job, not a business. A business runs without you standing over it. This is the sharpest version of yourco's existing arc ("you own the outcome") — it opens on a diagnosis the viewer recognizes about their own week, then names what closes the gap.

## Why this works
Every SMB owner who "works for themselves" has had the thought at 9pm: *the whole thing runs through me.* Calls, follow-ups, quotes, the inbox — pull the owner out and it stops. That's the felt problem. Naming it ("that's a job, not a business") earns the next line: the thing that turns a job into a business is a system that runs the operation. That system is the AI OS. It ties the emotional hook straight to the product without a hard sell.

Pairs with the home explainer (`2026-06-22_home-explainer-v2-concept.md`) — same transformation, sharper entry point. Reuse that film's locked look, palette, and motion language; this is a different opening, not a new visual world.

## Script (VO draft — swap-friendly, ~35–45s)
Read-aloud, applies `brand/writing-rules.md`. Lines map 1:1 to the beats below.

1. "You started this to work for yourself."
2. "But look at your week. Every call, every quote, every follow-up runs through you."
3. "Stop for a day and the whole thing stops with you."
4. "That's not a business. That's a job you can't quit."
5. "A business runs without you standing over it."
6. "yourco builds you the system that does the running — a team of AI employees that answers, follows up, and keeps the work moving, day and night."
7. "We own the reliability. You own the outcome."
8. "Start with an audit. We'll show you exactly what your business could run on its own."

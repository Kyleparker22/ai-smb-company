Curated to yourco's lane deliberately; the flashy Awwwards winners are excluded on purpose (see the bottom). Owner: **Webb** (custodian) · consumers: Webb, Luka, Pickle, Kimi. Built 2026-07-20 (the Founder asked for top-rated sites; grounded with current data — see Sources).

## The strategic read (why this list, not the Awwwards front page)
Premium web design in 2026 split into **two lanes**: **techno-futurist** (dark mode · neon · shaders · WebGL · bento grids) and **editorial** (cream · serif · whitespace · restraint). **yourco is editorial** — cream/indigo/brass, Fraunces serif, one-brass-per-surface, "precise not animated." That's not a compromise; it's a live trend with a tailwind, and yourco's closest public peers (**Anthropic**, **Aesop**) are among its best-executed examples. The current Awwwards Site-of-the-Year cohort (a 3D driving-sim portfolio, an F1 star's WebGL site) is the *other* lane — dazzling, dark, heavy, slow. Beautiful to admire; wrong to reference. Pulling from it would fight `DESIGN.md` (same trap as dark-mode-default in the premium-site guide, `decisions/2026-07-05_tool-triage.md`).

**Caveat pattern:** half the best editorial-*discipline* exemplars (Linear, Vercel) run dark. Study their *precision and restraint*, port it onto yourco's cream/indigo/brass — never their palette. Aesop, Anthropic, On, Family are the ones that match yourco's *light* + editorial directly.

## The galleries (endless vetted feed — filter to LIGHT / editorial / minimal)
- **Godly** (godly.website) · **Minimal.gallery** · **SiteInspire** (filter: Minimal, Light) · **Land-book** — curated, style-filterable. Best for yourco.
- **Awwwards / FWA / CSS Design Awards** — the scored curators, but skew techno-futurist/experimental; mine them with the *light/editorial* filter only, and for *motion ideas to restrain*, not palettes to copy.

## How to use (the guide's discipline, distilled)
Before a build: pick 2–3 from the board, name the *one* thing you're taking from each in a sentence (Aesop's whitespace, Family's motion restraint, On's hero scale), and only pull things that match yourco's editorial lane. References that contradict each other confuse the build. Then present 2–3 concrete directions before iterating live (DESIGN.md §7 / external-surface rules).

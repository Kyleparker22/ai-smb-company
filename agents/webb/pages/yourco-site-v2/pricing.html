pricing

You see the numbers before you sign.

One fee to build your custom AI system, one retainer to run it and keep it getting better. That’s the model — whether it’s a full system (where the real return is) or a single capability to start. Exact figures are scoped to your business from the audit; we walk you through the numbers, no surprises.

how it works

Four parts. You only pay for the ones you use.

1

The audit — where every engagement starts

A free, one-week audit that maps where your team’s time and revenue leak and what your AI system should do first. Every engagement starts here — we learn your business before we build. And it’s credited back — move forward on a minimum six-month engagement and your full audit fee comes off the build.

fixed fee
2

Build & implement your system

A one-time fixed fee to design, build, and deploy your custom AI system — tailored to your business, with the first capability live in 48 hours. You know the cost before we start.

fixed fee · one-time
3

Run & manage — monthly retainer

A flat monthly fee to operate the system and everything behind it: reliability, evals, watchdogs, ongoing improvement, and all the infrastructure — tokens, models, hosting. You never touch any of it; you just get the outcome.

monthly retainer
4

Add capabilities as you grow

Each additional capability is its own fixed build fee, plus a step-up in your monthly retainer to run it. Grow the system when you’re ready, at a price you can see coming.

fixed fee + retainer step-up
what the retainer covers

You never see a token, a model bill, or a server.

The monthly retainer absorbs all of it: the spend, the reliability work, the eval and approval discipline, the improvements. A high model bill is our problem to manage, not a number on your invoice.

the four levels

Every OS is custom. It lands at one of four levels.

The audit scopes which level fits — sized by how much of your business the system runs, not by a per-agent meter.

1

Core

One core function automated end-to-end — the wedge. Typically around three coordinated agents.

2

Suite

A department that runs itself — front-of-house or back-office, whole. Around five agents.

3

Operation

Multiple departments coordinated as one system. Around seven agents.

4

Command

The whole company OS — up to ten agents included, with the deepest integration and our strongest reliability standard. Beyond ten, we scope case by case.

why there are no numbers on this page

Because the honest answer is “it depends — let’s scope it.”

The build fee and retainer depend on the scope, your tools, and how much the system handles. We’d rather quote you an accurate number on a 30-minute call than post a misleading one here. The call is free, there’s no obligation, and you’ll leave knowing exactly what it would cost.

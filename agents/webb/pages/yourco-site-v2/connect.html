two ways to do it

Introduce them — or just hand us the name.

You pick per referral, not once and forever. Some people you’ll want to introduce properly; others you just know of. Both count.

Introduce

You open the door.

A warm three-way intro, or your own link. You make the connection and step back — we take the first call from there.

Best when you actually know them and a word from you carries weight.
Submit

You hand us the name.

Give us the owner’s details and we make the approach. You stay out of it entirely.

Because we become the caller, we have to ask two things: how you know them, and whether they’re expecting to hear from anyone. Both are required — we won’t call someone we can’t account for.
what you earn

One rate card. Everyone who refers earns the same escalator.

Your rate is set by how many of your referred clients are active — and when you level up, the new rate applies to your entire book, not just the next one. You’re paid on real client revenue, for as long as those clients stay.

TierActive referred clientsWhat changes
Referrer1–5A share of each client’s monthly fee, every month they stay
Senior6–10A higher share — applied to your whole book, not just the new ones
Partner11+The top share, again across everything you’ve referred

The actual percentages, and the bounty amounts below, live in the partner packet rather than on a public page. Ask and we’ll send it — there’s nothing coy about it, we just don’t publish rates.

If you’re already a client, it’s the same card — it comes off your own bill first, and once it exceeds your bill, the rest is paid to you in cash. If you ever stop being a client, you keep earning on referrals that are still live.

On a submitted name there’s also a small fixed bounty once we’ve verified the contact is real and reachable, and again if it becomes a real conversation — with the normal commission on top if they become a client. Submitting never costs you the commission you’d have earned by introducing them.

The program also includes a small share of revenue from clients referred by partners you bring in. That part isn’t open yet — it’s part of what’s under counsel review, and we’re not signing anyone up to build a team until it clears.

the rules, up front

Short list. All of it is about protecting the person you introduce.

  • You never quote, contract, deliver, or support. If a conversation turns into work, it’s our work.
  • Never submit a bought, scraped, or copied list. Those are rejected. Every submitted name has to be someone you can actually account for.
  • We ask how you know them, and whether they’re expecting to hear from anyone. On a submitted name we are the ones making the call, and we have to be able to say where it came from.
  • One referrer per business, attribution locked at first contact. No races, no disputes.
  • No quota, no minimum, no cost to join. One good introduction a quarter is a perfectly normal way to do this.

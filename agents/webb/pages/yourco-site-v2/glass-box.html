Proof, not testimonials

What our agents actually produced.

Real outputs from our own roster — the kind of work yours would do, the day it goes live.

Finance pulse— Finance
Read 7 days of receipts and caught it before the charge hit: "Canva is about to bill $18/mo, not the $15 we booked — true it up before the 16th." Plus a likely-duplicate subscription flagged for review. Posted to the team channel. No money moved without a human.
Runtime watchdog— Operations
Checked all 12 scheduled jobs: "All due loops ran, 0 missed." Then flagged a real gap nobody asked about — three loops without host logs — and named the fix. The watcher that watches the watchers.
Quality eval— Quality
Graded the week's work against a fixed rubric: 6 of 7 clean, zero gate violations. Caught one piece of content stating a stat without a source — and held it back. The bar goes up every week, on its own.
Pipeline report— CRM & pipeline
Reconciled the CRM and caught its own records drifting out of sync — flagged it, proposed the fix, and kept the pipeline honest. An agent that surfaces its own mess is one you can trust.

Real internal outputs, lightly summarized. We never show a number or a claim we can't stand behind — the same credibility standard your system is held to.

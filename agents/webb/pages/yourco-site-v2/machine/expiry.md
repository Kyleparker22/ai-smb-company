# Expiry — the things your business believes, and can’t check

> Name three things your business runs on. We'll show you which ones you could actually verify today — and which are load-bearing and unmeasured. Free, no account, and we'll ask you again later if you want.

## Every business runs on things nobody checks anymore.

“We get back to every quote within a day.” “We follow up twice before we drop a lead.” “Nobody waits more than a day for an answer.” These were true when you said them. Some still are.
Name three of yours. We’ll sort them into the ones you could verify this afternoon and the ones you couldn’t — and the second list is the one worth looking at.
No account, no score, nothing to download. It takes about two minutes.

### You could check these today.

There’s a number behind each one. If it slipped, you’d find out by looking — which means these are decisions, not assumptions.

### These are load-bearing, and unmeasured.

Not wrong — unverifiable . Your business is making decisions on them right now, and if one quietly stopped being true, nothing in your week would tell you. “Someone would mention it” and “I’d probably notice” are how every one of these starts.

### Want us to ask you again?

This is the part that’s actually useful. On a schedule you pick, we send one short email that quotes your own words back and asks whether they’re still true. That’s the whole email. No newsletter, no drip, no pitch attached.
We keep your three sentences and your email so we can send that one message. Unsubscribe in a click, any time. Your beliefs aren’t shared with anyone and aren’t used to build a profile of your business.

## The unmeasured list is what we’re actually for.

An audit is this exercise done properly: across the whole business, with your numbers instead of your memory, and with a dollar figure on each gap. What you just did in two minutes is the shape of it.
And it’s the same rule the systems we build run on — they report what they can prove and name what they can’t, rather than filling a gap with something that sounds right. We hold our own website to it too →

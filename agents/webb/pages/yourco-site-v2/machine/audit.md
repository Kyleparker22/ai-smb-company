## Not sure which job to hand off first?

A one-week audit, free while we're getting started, that maps where your team’s time and revenue leak — and ends with a clear recommendation on the first capability of your AI system to build. Every engagement starts here: we learn your business before we build.

## We do the work. You decide at three checkpoints.

No homework. Total leadership time across the week is about 3–5 hours — the rest is on us.

### Days 1–2 — we map the work.

A 60-minute kickoff, then we observe your real workflows and measure where the hours and revenue actually go. A quick checkpoint to confirm priorities.

### Days 3–4 — we design the first build.

We spec what’s eating the most of your team’s time — the first capability of your AI system worth building — grounded in your actual operations, not a template. A checkpoint to shape it.

### Day 5 — a clear recommendation.

What to build first, what it does, the math behind it, and the path to live in 48 hours. Handed off in writing, yours to keep.

## A handoff you could act on with or without us.

- A workflow map and time-sink read, showing where your team’s hours and revenue leak today and scoring each by how much an AI system would recover.
- A spec for the first capability worth building: what it does, what it touches, and what stays gated for your approval, detailed enough to build from.
- The ROI math, anchored to your actual economics rather than industry averages.
- A written readout you can reference six months later and share with co-leaders, not a deck you skim once.

## Why every engagement starts here.

We don’t build before we understand your business — the audit is how we learn it well enough to build the right system. You walk away with a quantified map and a plan either way; some clients take it and run it themselves, and that’s a good outcome too. It’s the front door, not a detour.
Want a taste of how it thinks before you commit to anything? Name three things your business runs on and we’ll sort them into the ones you could verify this afternoon and the ones nothing would tell you about. Two minutes, free, no account — and the second list is the shape of the whole audit.

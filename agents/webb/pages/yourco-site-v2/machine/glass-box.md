## Why we'll put it in your business.

Every agent is scored against a fixed rubric before it ships — and every week after.

It can't send, delete, or spend without a human until it's earned that trust. Always-on ≠ auto.

### Red-teamed

We try to break it — injection, abuse, data grabs — before a real customer ever can.

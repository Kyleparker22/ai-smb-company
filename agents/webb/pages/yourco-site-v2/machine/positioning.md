## Learn first. Then build the system.

yourco means “I learn.” Five steps take you from an audit of your business to a custom AI system we build and run. And we run our own company on the same system first — nothing reaches you that hasn’t earned its place in our own shop.

## The yourco Method.

### Discover.

The audit. We dig into how your business runs and find the bottleneck costing you the most — quantified in your own numbers.

### Distill.

We capture how the work actually happens — your workflows, your judgment, what only you know — into a living operating manual.

### Design.

We shape a custom AI system to your business from what we learned — the right capabilities working together, not a template.

### Deploy.

The first capability goes live in 48 hours. You approve anything customer-facing; we own reliability, security, and the evals.

### Deepen.

We operate it, refine it every week, and expand the system as it earns its place — so it gets sharper the longer it runs.

## From a conversation to a working hire in 48 hours.

### We learn your business.

A short discovery: the job you want off your plate, how it’s done today, what “good” looks like, what can never go out without you.

### We start from a proven blueprint.

We don’t start from zero. We begin with an employee pattern we’ve already run and tailor it to your business — your voice, your tools, your rules.

### It goes live — gated.

Its own email, in your tenant, doing the work. Anything customer-facing is drafted for your approval until you say otherwise.

### We keep it good.

Evals, watchdogs, and weekly improvement are ours. The employee gets sharper; you just keep getting the outcome.

## A few of the roles your AI OS can cover.

Patterns we run ourselves or have built — starting points we tailor to your business. The right first hire is usually the one role eating the most of your team’s week.

### The front desk that never misses.

Answers every call and form, qualifies, and books the estimate — day or night.

### The first-draft writer.

Turns a call or a brief into a proposal, quote, or reply in your voice — ready for a quick review, not a rewrite.

### The rep that always circles back.

Sequences outreach and follow-ups, staged for your approval before anything sends.

### Ask-the-business, answered.

Searches your docs and history and answers “have we ever done X?” with citations.

### The update drafter.

Pulls from your tools to draft the weekly customer or internal update — a 15-minute review, not a Friday ritual.

### The inbox sorter.

Categorizes inbound by urgency, drafts the routine replies, and flags what genuinely needs you.

## Three things that decide whether this works.

- Start with the work that prepares a human. Briefings, drafts, summaries earn trust fast. Employees turned loose to act unsupervised fail early, so we earn autonomy in stages.
- Voice and grounding beat raw model power. An employee that sounds like your business and knows your specifics gets used. One that’s technically correct but tonally off gets rewritten. The fix is grounding, not a bigger model.
- Get one role right before adding the next. The temptation is to automate everything at once. The math says nail the one role eating the most time, prove it, then expand.

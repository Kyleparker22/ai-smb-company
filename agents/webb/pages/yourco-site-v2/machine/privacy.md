# yourco — privacy policy

> How yourco handles the information you share on yourco.com.

Source page: `privacy.html`

## Privacy policy

yourco ("yourco," "we," "us") is operated by YourCo LLC (Florida). This policy explains what we collect when you use yourco.com , how we use it, and your choices. We keep it plain.

## 1 · What we collect

- Information you give us. When you book a call, request a demo, or use a tool on the site (the employee builder, the ROI calculator, the "see yours" demo), we collect your name, email, business/website, and anything you choose to tell us.
- Information collected automatically. Basic, privacy-preserving analytics — pages visited, approximate location from IP, device/browser type. We default to declining non-essential cookies.
- We do not ask for sensitive personal information through the site, and you shouldn't send it via web forms.

## 2 · How we use it

- Respond to your inquiry, build your requested demo, and schedule a call.
- Provide and improve our services.
- Send information you've asked for. We never sell your information.

## 3 · SMS / text messaging

yourco sends SMS/text messages to people and businesses we contact about our services, and to those who reply to or request them. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help, or contact us at [[founder@yourco.example.com / support phone]] . We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number, SMS opt-in, or consent information with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes , and text-message originator opt-in data is not shared with any third parties. Full SMS program terms: yourco.com/sms-terms .

## 4 · Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us operate, under their own terms — e.g. scheduling (Calendly), email/calendar (Google Workspace), and [[hosting/analytics provider]] . We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

## 5 · Cookies

Essential cookies plus optional, privacy-preserving analytics. You can decline non-essential cookies and control them in your browser. [[Confirm analytics tool + cookie banner.]]

## 6 · How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to respond and for our legitimate business records, then we delete it. [[Counsel: set a specific retention period.]]

## 7 · Your choices and rights

Ask us to access, correct, or delete what we hold — email [[contact@yourco.example.com]] . Depending on where you live (e.g. California / EEA), you may have additional rights under CCPA/CPRA or GDPR. [[Counsel: confirm regimes + disclosures.]]

## 8 · Security

We apply commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. No method is perfectly secure, but we take it seriously.

## 9 · Children

The site isn't directed to children under [[16]] , and we don't knowingly collect their information.

We'll post any changes here and update the effective date.

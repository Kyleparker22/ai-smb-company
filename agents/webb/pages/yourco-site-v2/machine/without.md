## What we won’t put a number on.

This is where a normal ROI page does its best work and its worst. It would be trivially easy to multiply those actions by a plausible minutes-per-task and print a headline about founder hours reclaimed. We’d have no right to that number, and printing it would make every honest figure above it worthless.

## Why we show you this one instead.

When you’re deciding whether to let a system run part of your business, the useful question isn’t whether the vendor can produce an impressive slide. It’s whether they’ll tell you what they don’t know.
The systems we build behave the same way: they report what they can prove and name what they can’t, rather than filling the gap with something that sounds right. A confident wrong answer inside your business is worse than no answer, because you’ll act on it. This page is that discipline pointed at our own marketing, where it’s least comfortable and easiest to check.

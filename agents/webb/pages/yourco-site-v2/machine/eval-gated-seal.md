## Eval-Gated.

Reliability is usually invisible — until something breaks. So we made it a mark. Every yourco agent earns it before it touches a real customer.

## What "Eval-Gated" certifies

Six standards every agent clears — ours and yours, the same bar.

### ✓ Eval gates

Scored against a fixed rubric before it ships, and every week after.

### ✓ Watchdogs

It's monitored for failure and silent drift — the watcher that watches the work.

### ✓ Approval boundary

Can't send, delete, or spend without a human until it's earned that trust.

### ✓ Red-teamed

We try to break it — injection, abuse, data grabs — before a real customer can.

### ✓ Grounded & honest

No fabricated facts; it escalates what it shouldn't guess on.

### ✓ Accountable

Someone owns keeping it good. That's the whole model.
A reusable mark — it goes on the site, the collateral, and the agent itself. Drop the <svg> anywhere; it's self-contained and on-brand.

The honest answers.

The questions every smart buyer asks. Here's the straight version — no spin.

Isn't this just ChatGPT with a wrapper?

The model is a commodity — anyone can call one. What you're buying is the reliability layer: grounding in your business, eval gates, watchdogs, approval flows, and someone accountable for keeping it good. The wrapper is the easy 20%. Making it trustworthy enough to run inside your business is the product.

What if it messes up with my customer?

Anything customer-facing is drafted for your approval until you've seen enough to trust it. It earns autonomy in stages, against real performance — and before it ever goes live, we red-team it to try to break it. It's built to escalate when it's unsure, not to wing it. Try to break one yourself →

Who owns my data? Is it safe?

You do. Your AI OS runs in your tenant; we access through scoped connectors. We don't sell your data, don't train foundation models on it, and there's a data-processing agreement (and a HIPAA BAA for healthcare). Least-privilege access, encryption, and an approval gate on anything destructive.

What does it actually cost?

A fixed fee to build your system, plus a monthly retainer that covers it and all the infrastructure — tokens, models, hosting. You never see a usage bill. We share exact numbers on a call once we know the job; pricing isn't a cold-email thing, because it depends on what you're handing off.

How is this different from a $29 receptionist app?

A $29 app is a tool you run yourself — you set it up, you babysit it, you're accountable when it fumbles. We're a managed AI OS: built, run, and improved for you, in your tenant, accountable when it matters. Different thing — and it's the part that was costing you money.

What if I want to stop?

Month-to-month after a short initial term. You keep your data and your work product; the license ends. No lock-in games, no hostage data.

Do I need to be technical?

No — that's the whole point. You never touch tokens, models, or infrastructure. You approve outcomes. We own everything that makes it work.

Will it sound like a robot, or off-brand?

It speaks in your voice, approved by you before go-live, and held to a brand standard. The goal is that your customers can't tell they're not talking to your best front-office person.

Won't this be obsolete in a year, the way AI keeps changing?

The opposite — because we run the stack, AI getting better is your dividend. When models improve (and they do, fast), we swap them in underneath and your system just gets better at the same price. A tool you buy today depreciates as AI advances; a system operated for you appreciates. The churn is our problem, on purpose.

Still skeptical? Good.

That's the right instinct for something that touches your customers. So don't take our word for it — attack one, or watch one work.

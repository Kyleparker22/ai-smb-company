about

A workshop, not a software vendor.

yourco is a boutique AI implementation business. We start with an audit, then build and run a custom AI OS — a coordinated team of agents — fit to how your company actually works, with the first capability live in 48 hours. You own the outcome. We own the reliability, the security, and the work of keeping it good.

why yourco exists

The market skipped the businesses that actually needed help.

The AI-services market split in two. On one side, enterprise consulting at six and seven figures, sold to organizations that can absorb 18-month timelines. On the other, a flood of tools and co-pilots sold by the seat — software you still have to learn, manage, and make useful yourself.

Almost nothing in between. And in between is where most businesses live: owner-led and leader-led shops where the constraint isn't strategy, it's time — and the last thing they need is another tool to manage or a year-long project to fund.

yourco is built for that business. Not a tool you operate. Not a project you supervise. A working system that does the job — agents that handle the work, with us carrying everything that makes it trustworthy.

what makes it different

Three things, and they’re the whole business.

1

Agents, not tools.

You don’t get software to configure. You get working agents — each with its own email inside your business — scoped to the work you actually need done. Start with one; the full OS is where the return is.

2

We own the hard part.

Anyone can wire up an agent. Making a whole system reliable enough to run inside your business — eval gates, watchdogs, approvals, observability — is the real job, and it’s ours.

3

Live in 48 hours, then improved for life.

From a signed agreement to the first capability live in two days — then we keep sharpening it and adding the next. The craft doesn’t stop at go-live.

we eat our own cooking

We run yourco on the agents we build.

Our own sales outreach, finance, content, and operations run on a roster of agents — the same kind we build for clients, held to the same eval and approval standards. We don’t recommend anything we haven’t already trusted with our own business.

honest fit

Where yourco is — and isn’t — the right call.

  • A fit if you want a specific job handled — intake, follow-up, scheduling, drafting, back-office — without hiring for it or learning another tool.
  • A fit if you’d rather own an outcome than manage software, and want someone accountable for keeping it reliable.
  • Not a fit if you want a SaaS product to run yourself; a co-pilot vendor is the better buy.
  • Not a fit if your systems need ground-up work before anything can sit cleanly on top; we’ll tell you, and point you to the right partner.

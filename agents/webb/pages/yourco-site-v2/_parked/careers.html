opportunities · become a connector

You make the intro. yourco does the rest.

Work as much or as little as you want. No base, no quota, no boss — a flexible, performance-only income on the side of whatever you already do. You point us at businesses that could use an AI OS. We run the call, close it, build it, operate it, and own the relationship. You earn a recurring cut for as long as they stay.

what the role is

The easiest sales role you’ll ever have: just the introduction.

1

You introduce. That’s the whole job.

Know a business owner drowning in admin, missed calls, or follow-up? A consultant, a friend, a former client? Send them our way — a warm intro or a link. No pitch deck, no technical skill, no demo to run.

2

yourco takes it from there.

We run the audit, scope the AI OS, close the deal, build it, and operate it — held to our own reliability and approval standards. You stay hands-off after the intro; we own quality and the relationship.

3

You get paid every month they stay.

It’s not a one-time finder’s fee. As long as the client you referred keeps their AI OS running, you keep earning — a recurring line that compounds as you refer more.

how you earn

A recurring rate that rises with your book.

You earn a recurring commission on each referred client’s monthly retainer — paid on collected revenue. The more active clients you’ve referred, the higher the rate, and the higher rate lifts your whole book, not just the next one.

Referrer
Base rate
1–5 active clients

Your first referrals start earning right away — recurring, every month they stay live.

Senior

Cross six active clients and your whole book steps up — not just the sixth.

Partner

At eleven active clients your entire book earns the top rate — a real recurring income line.

It’s residual. Each active account keeps paying you for as long as it stays with yourco — so referrals you made months ago are still earning. Your rate is set by how many of your referred clients are currently active, and it applies across your full book at once.
why it’s no-risk for you

Nothing to buy. Nothing to build. Paid only when we are.

There’s no inventory, no kit, no fee to join, and no delivery work on your plate. You never quote a price or sign a contract — yourco owns the pricing and the relationship. You only ever get paid when yourco gets paid, on revenue that’s actually collected. Your downside is zero; your upside is every client you bring.

who makes a great connector

If you talk to small-business owners, you’re already qualified.

  • Consultants & fractional execs — you’re already inside businesses that have the exact problems an AI OS solves.
  • Bookkeepers, accountants, MSPs & IT shops — you see the admin drag and the missed-money leaks firsthand.
  • Vertical operators — anyone embedded in a trade or niche with a network of peer owners.
  • Anyone with the right network — if your phone is full of owners who’d benefit, that’s the whole asset.
  • Happy yourco clients — already running an AI OS and want to send a peer our way? You can earn as a connector too.

There’s also a way to grow a team rather than refer solo — if that’s where you’re headed, we’ll walk you through it.

Questions

Who can refer?

Anyone — clients, friends, other operators, consultants. You don't have to be a customer.

When do I get paid?

Monthly, on collected revenue, at your current tier rate, starting the first month your referral pays.

What if my referral cancels?

Commission runs as long as they're an active, paying client. If your active count drops below a tier line, your rate adjusts — so we're both rooting for them to stay happy.

Is there a limit?

No cap on how many you refer. The more active clients you bring, the higher your rate — on your whole book.

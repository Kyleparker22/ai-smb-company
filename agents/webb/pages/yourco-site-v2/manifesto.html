Hire,
don't subscribe.

For thirty years, the answer to "we're drowning in work" was the same: buy another tool. A CRM. A scheduler. A chatbot. A dozen tabs, each a monthly seat, each one more thing for someone on your team to learn, configure, and babysit. You didn't get the work done. You got more software to manage.

We think that era is ending.

The job was never "have a tool." The job was always "get the work done" — the call answered, the lead followed up, the estimate sent, the books reconciled. A tool makes you do the work faster. An employee does the work.

That's the whole idea behind yourco. You don't subscribe to software. You hire a digital employee — with its own email, inside your business — and it does a real job. Not a co-pilot you steer. Not an app you open. A hire. And the first hire is just the on-ramp: the real product is the whole team — an AI OS running the departments you need as one coordinated system, operated by us.

The objection is obvious: can you actually trust software to act like an employee? For most of the last few years, no — the demos were magic and the reality was a bot that confidently said the wrong thing to your best customer. That gap is the only thing that matters, and closing it is the entire job. Anyone can wire up an agent now; it's a weekend project. Making one reliable enough to run unsupervised inside your business — eval gates, watchdogs, an approval boundary, a red-team that tries to break it — is the actual work. It's the part we own. It's why we'll put one in your business and stand behind it, and why a $29 app won't.

We're betting the count changes. You'll stop counting how many tools you pay for and start counting how many jobs get handled. You add capability the way a growing company always has, by hiring, except the next hire is live in 48 hours, improves every week, and the one after costs a fraction of a headcount.

This isn’t a hunch. It’s in the data.

Every claim above, checked against the research — sources named, the same credibility standard we hold our own work to.

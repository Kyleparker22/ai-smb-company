Issues / notes:
- **Palette — pass.** Indigo `#161B33` body, brass `#B8965A` highlights/synapses, cream `#F4EFE6` ground. On-system, no off-brand hues.
- **Type — n/a.** No text rendered in the visual (correctly — honors the "no AI-rendered text / gibberish" rule).
- **[Watch 1] Brass scarcity.** Guidelines: *"use brass sparingly… brass earns its presence through scarcity… avoid gold-flash (cheap)."* The brain is the most brass-forward element on the page. It currently holds because the illustration is **indigo-dominant with brass relief** (not a solid gold object) and the glow was pulled back to `0.6`. Watch: don't let future passes push the brass brighter/larger — that tips toward the gold-flash the brand explicitly rejects. Keep one dominant brass moment per surface.
- **[Watch 2] Motion restraint.** Brand posture is *calm, considered, discreet — "the one in the room not raising its voice."* A cursor-reactive, pulsing, breathing brain risks reading demonstrative. The settled tuning (parallax `0.08`, subtle tilt/breath, slow synapse pulse) lands on the considered side. Watch: resist "more alive / more 3D" requests past this point — restraint *is* the brand's edge here.

Rationale (≤1 paragraph): The visual communicates "this is built well" before a word is read — premium craft, on-palette, on-concept for *yourco = I learn* — which is exactly the brand's job. The only brand tensions are quantitative (how much brass, how much motion), and the current dialed-back state sits on the right side of both lines. Ship. Both watch-notes are about holding the line on the *next* iteration, not fixing this one.

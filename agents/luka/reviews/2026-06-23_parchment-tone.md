# Luka note — section-band tone deepened (Warm Parchment) (2026-06-23)

*Change:* the site's alternate full-bleed band (`--parchment`) deepened **`#EFE8DA → #EAE1CD`**, applied site-wide (the every-other section band + footer surfaces), to fix "too much cream running together." Reviewed against `/brand/v0/brand-guidelines.md`.

**Verdict: ship — and codified.** On-brand, and it's a *derived* cream variant rather than a new core color, so it doesn't touch the palette identity. I've added it to the guidelines (Cream Linen → "Section band — Warm Parchment") and bumped to **v0.4** (the Founder approved on review).

Issues / notes:
- **Palette identity intact.** The four-color system (Midnight Indigo · Cream Linen · Brass · Oxblood) is unchanged. Warm Parchment is a layout-rhythm tone in the cream family — the kind of derived surface tone a brand system is expected to carry. No new hue, no brass/oxblood involvement.
- **On the emotional palette.** A warmer, sandier cream reads as *more* "paper-like craft / made with care," not less — aligned with the atelier posture. It's a step warmer, not a departure.
- **[Watch] Don't let it creep into text or over-band.** It's the *alternate* band only (and footers) — body text stays on `#F4EFE6` cream, and it should remain every-other (not every) section so the rhythm holds. Flagged in the guideline entry.
- **Reversibility.** Single token; trivially dialed warmer/lighter or reverted.

Rationale (≤1 paragraph): The change solves a real legibility-of-rhythm problem (the old parchment was within a few percent of cream, so "alternating" sections didn't read) without disturbing the brand's identity or restraint. It strengthens the paper-craft feel rather than working against it. Codifying it keeps the guidelines the source of truth.

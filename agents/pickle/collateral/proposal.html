Your front desk, handled — live in 48 hours.

The outcome you're buying

You will have "Reese" — a named digital employee that answers every inbound call and text to Greenline, qualifies the job, books the on-site estimate, confirms it, and logs it — live in your business within 48 hours of go-ready.

You own the outcome. You never touch the tokens, the models, or the infrastructure — YourCo runs all of it.

What Reese does

  • Answers every inbound call & text, day or night, in Greenline's voice
  • Qualifies the job — service type, property, timeline, location
  • Books the on-site estimate straight into your calendar
  • Confirms and sends reminders to cut no-shows
  • Logs every lead so nothing slips through
  • Routes anything urgent or unusual straight to you

What's included — every month, one price

  • Build & deployment, integrated with your phone, calendar, and CRM
  • Full operation — model usage, voice, telephony, hosting: all absorbed by YourCo. You're never billed for usage.
  • The reliability layer — evals, watchdogs, and an approval gate: nothing customer-facing sends without your rules
  • Your live console — watch it work, approve drafts, see outcomes and reliability, any time
  • Weekly iteration + a readout of what it handled and what improved

Where the human stays in the loop

  • Never quotes a price — estimates happen on-site, your rule
  • Never commits beyond your calendar's real availability
  • Anything urgent or unusual routes to you immediately
  • You set what it can say — nothing off-script. We show the human steps; we don't hide them.

Timeline

Day 0 — you sign and grant the access Reese needs ("go-ready"). Within 48 hours of go-ready — Reese is live. Weekly — iteration + your readout. When trusted — we scope the next employee (optional).

The clock starts when the agreement is signed and access is granted, so a delay on access simply pauses it — fair to both sides.

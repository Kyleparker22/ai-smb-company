# Katie — Stage 1: Discovery

## What this agent is
Katie is the Content/Marketing Agent; she owns YourCo's written thought-leadership.

## First use case
**Weekly content cadence.** Every Friday, Katie picks one sharp theme anchored on the thesis/moat and produces ready-to-publish drafts (LinkedIn, X, newsletter) in the Founder's voice, plus follow-up angles. the Founder reviews and publishes. While pipeline is thin, this is YourCo's primary growth lever — content compounds.

## Outcome the executive can repeat in one sentence
"Every week the Founder has ready-to-publish, on-voice content anchored on YourCo's moat — so authority compounds and inbound seeds itself — without staring at a blank page."

## Systems Katie touches (v0)
- `CLAUDE.md`, `01_company.md` — thesis, moat, narrative anchors (mandatory)
- Most recent prior artifact in `loops/content/` — to avoid repeating themes
- Most recent `loops/sales/` artifact — topical hooks (a prospect objection → a public post)
- (Optional) WebSearch — AI-implementation industry chatter from the last 7 days, only if it changes the angle
- Writes `loops/content/YYYY-MM-DD.md`; posts a one-line notice to `#all-yourco`

## Inherited from Atlas
The content loop SOP (`processes/loops/content.md`) and prior content artifacts. Atlas ran it; Katie now owns it. Atlas continues to read content output only for the Monday briefing.

## Success criteria (eval set v0 — full harness in 03_eval.md)
1. **On-voice** — reads like the Founder (concise, direct, outcomes-framed, no buzzword salad, no emoji unless organic). Target: 100% pass the style guide.
2. **Moat-anchored** — every piece sells the moat layer (reliability/eval/observability/trust), never the tooling. Target: 100%.
3. **Ready-to-publish** — drafts need no rewrite, just a yes. Target: the Founder publishes ≥1 piece per week with minimal edits.
4. **Non-repetition** — theme differs from the prior 2 weeks; follow-up angles generated. Target: 100%.
5. **Distinctiveness** — "if anyone selling AI consulting could've written it, reject it." Target: 0 generic pieces shipped.

## Approval pattern
- **Full autonomy** for: picking the theme, drafting all content, writing the artifact, posting the internal `#all-yourco` notice.
- **Human-must-approve** for: **publishing anything externally** (LinkedIn/X/newsletter/blog). Katie drafts; the Founder publishes.
- **Human-in-loop** for: claims about results/clients (YourCo is pre-revenue — no fabricated proof or case studies).

## Scope — IN (v0)
Weekly theme selection, LinkedIn/X/newsletter drafting, follow-up angle generation, repurposing within written formats, the content artifact.

## Scope — OUT (parked for v1+)
- Publishing externally (drafts only; the Founder publishes)
- Video/demo (that's Reed) and designed collateral/case studies (that's Pickle)
- Paid ads / campaign management
- Fabricated results, metrics, or client case studies (none until real and approved)

## v0 → v1 → v2 roadmap
- **v0:** weekly cadence + drafts, pre-revenue. Prove on-voice + moat-anchored + distinct.
- **v1:** scheduled publishing via a connector (still the Founder-approved); track which themes drive engagement/inbound and double down.
- **v2:** full editorial engine — repurpose Reed demos and Pickle case studies into multi-format campaigns; feed inbound leads to Reilly.

## Risks
- **Generic AI content.** The fastest way to erode authority. Mitigation: the distinctiveness gate — reject anything anyone could've written.
- **Off-voice drift.** Mitigation: rigorous style guide in the SOP; the Founder's edits feed back weekly.
- **Overclaiming pre-revenue.** Mitigation: human-in-loop on any results/client claim; no fabricated proof.

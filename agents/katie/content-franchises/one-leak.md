# Content franchise — "One Leak" (concept, staged)

> **Owner: Katie** (script) + **the Founder** (face) + **Bella** (the math — it's her audit Step 3 in public). A *signature fixed-rules format*, stolen from Dave Portnoy's One Bite pizza reviews (triage: `decisions/2026-07-05_tool-triage.md` §Portnoy addendum, 2026-07-23). One real business · one bottleneck · one number — everyone knows the rules.

## The pattern being stolen (why One Bite worked)
Portnoy's format has four locked parts: a **fixed ritual** (one bite, on camera), a **number** (0–10 score), a **catchphrase** ("one bite, everyone knows the rules"), and — the part most people miss — **the subject benefits**: a visit could single-handedly revive a pizzeria, so owners *wanted* him to show up. Supply of subjects became infinite and goodwill compounded. Repetition made it a franchise; the franchise made him the #1 pizza authority without ever claiming to be one.

## The yourco translation
the Founder sits down with a real local business owner (St Pete/Tampa beachhead first — warm network, meetups, CPA referrals) and finds **the one leak** — the single biggest revenue bottleneck — computed live from *their* numbers, math on screen. e.g. *"30 missed calls a month, 30% would have booked, $1,000 average job — that's $9,000/mo walking past you."* Ends with the fix in one sentence and the leak number as the "score."

- **Fixed rules (say them every episode):** one business · one leak · their numbers · math shown · the fix named. Never more than one leak — the discipline *is* the format.
- **The subject benefits:** the owner gets a real mini-audit free, on camera, plus local exposure. They should *want* to be featured — that's the supply engine and the referral engine in one.
- **The CTA is implicit:** this is the Audit, demonstrated. "That was one leak in ten minutes. The audit finds all of them" — nothing harder-sell than that.
- **Format fit:** short-form vertical clip (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) + a carousel of the math (per the platform map in `processes/content/content-engine.md`) + the long cut on YouTube. One shoot, every channel.

## Hard rules (the credibility gate + external-surface rules)
- **Opt-in only, always.** The owner consents on camera before anything publishes — yourco never does gotcha-audits of named businesses. (Portnoy ambushes; we can't and shouldn't.) Anonymize ("a $2M hardscaper in Tampa") if an owner wants the value without the spotlight.
- **Real numbers or labeled estimates.** The math is theirs; any assumption is stated on screen. Never a fabricated or benchmark-dressed-as-theirs figure (same rule as the two-sided proposal, `decisions/2026-07-20_two-sided-proposals.md`).
- **Never shame the business.** The leak is framed as "money waiting," not incompetence — the owner must come off as smart for looking. One Bite works because a bad score is about the pizza, not the person; ours has no bad scores at all.
- **Voice:** `brand/writing-rules.md` governs. Portnoy's *format discipline* is stolen; his brashness is explicitly rejected (brand clash — same verdict as Suby's hype voice).
- No agent names on camera (external-surface rule); the Founder fronts it. Kolby evals; the Founder approves every publish.
- **Staged until the launch-gate clears** — build the ritual and bank 2–3 episodes with warm-network owners; publish at launch.

## Why this could compound for yourco specifically
- It is **the audit as content** — the product demonstrating itself, which is the house content thesis (proof, not claims).
- It is **beachhead-aligned**: every episode is a local St Pete/Tampa relationship, a referral node, and a warm lead — the format *serves* the commercial priority instead of competing with it.
- It is **repeatable and ownable**: a named ritual with fixed rules is recognizable by episode 3 and un-copyable in spirit — a competitor can copy the format but not the operated-OS credibility behind the fix.

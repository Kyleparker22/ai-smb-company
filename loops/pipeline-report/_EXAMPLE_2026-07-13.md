## What worked this run
Catching that **Sample Contact's task was checkbox-closed while the record stayed empty** — exactly the pattern the 7/07 learning warned about. A lazy read sees `done:true` and drops the item; the honest read checks the fields the task was supposed to change (company name, contact, deal stage) and finds all three untouched. Reporting it as an *open* unblock rather than a closed task keeps a stalled prospect from silently vanishing. Same discipline surfaced the Classic Roofing orphan: the deal count (22) looked clean, but a company with a contact and no deal is real pipeline hiding from every downstream reader.

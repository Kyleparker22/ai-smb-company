## Idea 1 — Own one vertical completely before spreading: become *the* AI operator for hardscapers. · _lens: Thiel_

**The contrarian secret:** everyone in AI-for-SMB is going horizontal ("AI for any business"). The Thiel move is the opposite — pick the smallest defensible market and *monopolize* it. YourCo already has the wedge: Sample Client (hardscaper), a hardscaping landing page, hardscaping intent feeds, hardscaping Revenue Leak Snapshot. Pour it all into **becoming the obvious, dominant AI choice for hardscape contractors in one region** before touching a second vertical — saturate the AEO ("AI for hardscapers" answers = yourco), the case studies, the referrals, the trade-forum presence, until yourco *is* the category there.

- **Why now:** Sample Client is the live beachhead and the operated-moat story lands hardest when every reference customer is in the same trade talking to each other. Depth compounds; breadth dilutes pre-revenue.
- **Smallest test:** pick one metro, get 3 hardscapers live and referenceable, and own the answer-engine + a trade-forum reputation for that one term — measure whether referrals/inbound concentrate.
- **Focus cost:** *reduces* sprawl (this is a focusing idea, not an expanding one); two-way door; low launch risk — it sharpens the existing plan rather than adding to it. The cost is saying "no, not yet" to the other 49 verticals' GTM.
- **Rating: Now** — it's a discipline, not a new build; it makes the launch we're already doing *win deeper*.

## Idea 2 — Spin the Revenue Leak Snapshot's leak math into a standalone free tool: the "Missed-Money Meter." · _lens: Levels_

A single-purpose, embeddable, 30-second free widget: a contractor enters 3 numbers, sees their monthly revenue leak with the math shown — no email gate on the *number* (gate the full report). It's the Revenue Leak Snapshot's calculator, extracted as its own viral artifact: Product-Hunt-launchable, shareable on X/forums, embeddable on partner sites, and the perfect top-of-funnel lead magnet. Pure Levels: one tiny tool, public, instant value, points at the product.

- **Why now:** the Revenue Leak Snapshot engine already exists — this is a repackage, not a build. And it's exactly the kind of free tool that earns a launch-day traffic spike (the Product Hunt play we just added to the runbook).
- **Smallest test:** a one-page version of `snapshot.html` with no questionnaire — just the 3-input calculator + a "see your full report" CTA — and watch share/conversion vs. the full flow.
- **Focus cost:** small (reuses existing code); two-way door; minor launch-sequencing risk if it competes for Webb's attention before the core site ships — so it waits until the site is live.
- **Rating: Next** (post-site-live) — cheap, on-brand, and it's the artifact that makes the Product Hunt/forum spike actually work.

## Idea 3 — The "Industry Leak Index": turn every audit into a public benchmark that compounds into a moat. · _lens: Balaji_

Every Revenue Leak Snapshot and full Audit produces real numbers about where a business leaks money. Aggregate them (anonymized) into a **public, updating benchmark per vertical** — *"the average roofer leaks $X/mo to missed calls; here's the 2026 data."* It's three things at once: a **media property** (endless cited content for Katie + an AEO authority win for Mario), a **data network effect** (more audits → better benchmarks → more authority → more audits — a moat that grows with usage, the Balaji play), and a **sharper sales tool** (every prospect sees how they compare to their trade). Product + media + network as one system.

- **Why now:** it costs nothing extra — the data is a byproduct of audits we're already running; we just have to capture and publish it. The earlier we start logging structured audit data, the sooner the benchmark has teeth.
- **Smallest test:** add a structured-data capture to the audit intake now (even pre-publish), so by the time there are ~20 audits there's a first "leak index" to publish for one vertical.
- **Focus cost:** the *capture* is near-free now; the *publishing/media* is a Later investment (needs volume + Polo/claims review on any published stat). One-way-ish on the data schema (worth designing right once); low risk if we only build the capture now and defer publication.
- **Rating: Later** to publish — but **Now** for the tiny capture step, so the data is accumulating instead of being lost. (Classic Balaji: start the data flywheel before you need it.)

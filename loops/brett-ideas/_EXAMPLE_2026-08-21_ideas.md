## Idea 1 — Route the first Audit through Partner B's and Mike's warm networks, not the Founder's — a partner asking their own contact doesn't pay the Founder's emotional tax. · _lens: Paul Graham (do things that don't scale / talk to users)_

The single most-diagnosed fact in this OS is that the warm ask is **emotionally blocked and the Founder-specific** (`learnings/advisor/2026-07-10`: de-risked to the last mechanical step and it still didn't fire — 69 days, 17 carries). Every unblock attempted so far — brake, floor, critique-ask reframe — has aimed at *the act* while leaving *the actor* fixed as the Founder. But the actor changed this month: yourco now has two partners with real financial stakes and, crucially, **their own warm networks**, which carry none of the "no from a friend whose opinion I care about" cost that has frozen the Founder's list. The sales machinery audit already pointed at this ("the one thing a second partner most directly changes" is booking the first Audit); nobody has yet made it a partner's *job*. Paul Graham's instruction here is literal: the first users come from people who already trust you, hand-courted, and it doesn't have to be *you* who trusts them. With the Audit now free (`2026-08-16`), the ask is the lowest-friction it will ever be: "can I run a free 90-minute leak diagnosis on a friend's business?" is a favor a partner grants easily, and the market scan confirms it's the highest-converting front door in the category right now.

- **Why now:** the Partner B walkthrough is **open right now** (through 8/26, `decisions/2026-08-16_walkthrough-locks-round-one.md`) — the one window where all three partners are actively in the business together. It is the natural, and possibly only, forcing function to assign the first ask to someone other than the Founder before it ages another month.
- **Smallest test:** during the walkthrough, Partner B and Mike each name **one** warm contact from their own network who runs an SMB; the OS pre-builds that contact's leak snapshot (Idea 2); **one free leak-audit is booked by next Friday**, owned by the partner who named the person — measured by David logging the touch. Success is a *booked audit*, not a sale.
- **Focus cost:** the inverse of a focus cost — it uses an asset acquired this month to sidestep the exact blocker five drops named, and it's a conversation, not a build. Two-way door. The honest tension: **the Founder's own warm list is warmer and larger**, and routing to partners must not become a way to keep *not* touching it — this is the unblocked path, not a permission to abandon the blocked one. Guardrail: if it becomes "we'll get the partners to do it eventually," it's failed; the test is one booked audit this week or it's just another deferral.
- **Rating: Now** — the only Now I'll spend, and I'm spending it on the freshest genuine unblock in months: the drought has always been an emotional-cost problem attached to one person, and the company just hired two people it isn't attached to. This is the guardian and generative halves agreeing — the boldest and most disciplined move this week is the same one: a partner books the first audit, from their own network, this week.

So the move isn't to reframe the ask — it's to **remove the ask entirely**. Now that the Audit is **free** (`2026-08-16`) and the wedge is the **leak quantified in their own numbers** (`2026-08-16_leak-first-wedge`), yourco can run a lightweight leak diagnosis on a warm prospect's *public/known* footprint and hand them the finished number cold: *"I ran your business — you're losing ~$X/mo on missed calls at your close rate; here's the one-pager, no strings."* That is not a favor the Founder requests (blocked); it's a finished valuable thing he delivers (proven twice to happen), and it's exactly the "one real working pilot → case study" recipe the whole market now publishes as the first-client unlock. Shown, not said — the moat argument made concrete.

- **Why now:** the two enabling decisions (free audit, leak-first) are five days old and have **zero instances executed**; the market scan says this is the single highest-converting first-touch in the category in 2026; and the OS already has the machinery (`processes/audit-sop.md`, `runtime/counterfactual.py`, the Evidence door) to produce the number. The gap is one produced artifact, not one more decision.

One artifact, delivered, by Friday.

Guardrail: it's a **one-page** snapshot on **one** prospect from *existing* machinery — if it turns into a "leak-snapshot product" or a second week of polish, it's failed. Two-way door (a give-first one-pager to a warm contact is inside the launch-gate; a public leak tool is not — keep it 1:1). It *feeds* Idea 1 (it's what the partner hands over), so it supports the Now rather than competing with it.
- **Rating: Next** — the highest-yield *new* operationalization on the board and it directly serves the Now, but it stays Next because a produced artifact, however on-thesis, can't be allowed to displace the un-buildable act of a partner or the Founder actually booking the conversation. Build the snapshot *for* the ask; don't let building it become the ask's replacement.

## Idea 3 — Run the OS live on a partner's own business as engagement #1 — the fastest, most-aligned, non-gated path to the first *measured* outcome, which is the thing every growth claim is currently blocked on. · _lens: Thiel (monopolize a market of one, completely) / Balaji_

Every growth claim, every case study, every price increase, the entire exit from pre-revenue positioning — all of it is gated on a number yourco has zero of. The market scan names the same gate ("deliver one real outcome, turn it into a case study, raise price"). The fastest path to measured-outcome #1 is not a stranger and not even Sample Client — it's a **partner's own business**. Partner B and Mike run real businesses; they have equity-aligned incentive to let the OS operate one for real, no sales cycle, no trust gap, no launch-gate (internal/partner, not external marketing). Thiel's move is to own one tiny market *completely* before expanding — n=1, your own partner's shop, fully operated, producing a real leak-recovered dollar figure and a real autonomy rate on the trust ledger. That single measured outcome is worth more than any of the six platforms shipped this month, because it's the one asset the whole company is currently blocked without.

- **Why now:** the partners are actively in the business this week (walkthrough through 8/26) — the moment to ask "can we run your business on this?" is while they're already opening the hood; and every day pre-revenue-positioning persists is a day the two trip-wires stay tripped for want of a single measured engagement.
- **Smallest test:** during the walkthrough, identify **one** operational leak in Partner B's or Mike's own business (one pillar — Intake is cheapest, `2026-08-16_leak-first-wedge`), scope it as a real engagement with a defined "what counts as working," and start it — measured against `runtime/counterfactual.py` + the trust ledger so the outcome is a *number*, not a claim. Not a buildout; a pilot with a defined success line (`learnings/ops/2026-07-05_validate-before-buildout`).
- **Focus cost:** the heaviest-lift idea here and I'll flag it honestly. (1) It's a real engagement — it must not pull hours from **closing Sample Client**, the actual warmest *external* deal that's one step from signed and just flipped red today; sequencing is close the real deal first. (2) A partner's business is not a stranger, so it proves the *operating* thesis and produces a case study, but it does **not** prove the *cold-conversion* motion — don't oversell it as validating the funnel. Two-way door. It brushes nothing parked (distinct from the `2026-08-17` parked "buy the business" play — this is *operate*, not acquire).
- **Rating: Later** — the strongest proof-generator in the OS (it's the only move that trips yourco *out* of the pre-revenue positioning cage), but it stays behind Sample Client and behind the first booked audit, because the disciplined sequence is: get one real external conversation (Now), produce the give-first artifact that powers it (Next), *then* stand up the internal case-study engine (Later). Don't build engagement #1 on a partner while the closest-to-signed external deal sits red.

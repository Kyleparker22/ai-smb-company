2.2 [PARTNER] is admitted as a Member on the Effective Date upon execution and delivery of the contribution in Schedule D.

Vested percentages govern §8 buyouts only — voting and distributions run on full Unit count while a Member remains active. ### Article 3 — Capital contributions

### Article 4 — Allocations, distributions, tax
4.1 Profits, losses, and distributions: **50/50** in proportion to Units, subject to §2.3.
4.2 **Tax distributions:** the Company distributes, at minimum, each Member's estimated tax on allocated income (safe-harbor rate counsel sets), quarterly, before any discretionary distribution.
4.3 Discretionary distributions require both Members' consent (§5.3).
4.4 On admission of [PARTNER], the Company's federal classification becomes **partnership** (Form 1065, K-1s). [Counsel/CPA: confirm; update W-9s, bank, payment processors post-signing.] the Founder is designated **Partnership Representative** (BBA) unless the Members agree otherwise.

### Article 5 — Management
5.1 **Member-managed.** Each Member has authority for ordinary-course actions **within their lane** (Schedule C-1: [the Founder — product, delivery, platform, client operations · PARTNER — [LANE]]).
5.2 Either Member may bind the Company for ordinary-course obligations under $[2,500] singly; above that, both.
5.3 **Unanimous-consent actions** (both signatures, no exceptions): admitting members or issuing Units; debt over $[5,000]; contracts over $[10,000]/yr; selling or licensing Company IP outside the ordinary course; hiring/firing employees; compensation changes; discretionary distributions; changing lines of business; lawsuits; amending this Agreement; dissolution; any transaction between the Company and a Member or their affiliate.
5.4 No salary/guaranteed payment unless unanimously agreed in writing.

### Article 6 — Deadlock (50/50 requires this)
6.1 A **Deadlock** exists when a §5.3 matter fails to obtain both consents twice, at least 14 days apart, in writing.
6.2 Escalation: (a) 30-day cool-off with a documented good-faith meeting; then (b) **mediation** (neutral mediator, Pinellas County, costs shared); then (c) **shotgun buy-sell**: either Member may serve a notice stating a per-Unit price; the recipient elects, within 45 days, to **buy all** the initiator's Units **or sell all** their own at that price; closing within 60 days of election. [D3 — counsel drafts the full mechanics: funding, security, default.]
6.3 Nothing in §6 forces a sale absent a served shotgun notice; the Company continues ordinary-course operation during a Deadlock.

### Article 7 — Transfer restrictions
7.1 No Member may transfer, pledge, or encumber Units without the other Member's written consent, except per §8.
7.2 **Right of first refusal:** any permitted third-party offer must first be offered to the Company, then the other Member, on identical terms (30/30-day windows).
7.3 Any transferee holds economic rights only unless admitted as a Member by the non-transferring Member's consent.

### Article 8 — Buy-sell events
8.1 Trigger events per Member: death; permanent disability (defined: [180] days' inability to perform); bankruptcy/insolvency; divorce decree affecting Units; material breach of this Agreement uncured for 30 days; voluntary withdrawal; [IF D1] departure before full vesting (unvested Units forfeit at $0; vested Units per this Article).
8.2 On a trigger, the Company (first) and the other Member (second) hold an option to purchase the affected Units at **Fair Value**: agreed within 30 days, else a neutral appraiser (each side may demand a second appraiser; average governs) — no minority/marketability discounts between the two Members.
8.3 Payment: [20]% down, balance over [36] months at the AFR, secured by the purchased Units. [Counsel: consider term life/disability insurance to fund death/disability buyouts.]

### Article 9 — IP, confidentiality, outside activities
9.1 **All work product** either Member creates for or in connection with the Company — code, prompts, systems, content, client materials, brand assets — is **Company property** upon creation (work-for-hire; assignment to the extent needed).
9.2 Schedule B IP is contributed by the Founder Member and is Company property as of the Effective Date.
9.3 Confidentiality: Members protect Company and client confidential information during membership and for [3] years after; client data handled per the engagement agreements and applicable law, without time limit.
9.4 **Excluded outside ventures (Schedule C-2):** each Member's listed pre-existing ventures remain separate — no Company claim on them, no use of Company resources for them, and vice versa. [the Founder lists his other ventures; PARTNER lists theirs.]
9.5 **Corporate opportunities:** AI-implementation and operated-AI-systems opportunities presented to either Member belong to the Company first; a Member may pursue one only after written waiver by the other Member.

### Article 10 — Restrictive covenants
10.1 While a Member and for [2] years after ceasing to be one: no soliciting Company clients or active prospects; no soliciting Company employees/contractors; no competing operated-AI-implementation business targeting the Company's clients or active pipeline within its actual market. [Counsel: conform to Fla. Stat. §542.335 (legitimate business interest, reasonable scope) — enforceability is drafting-sensitive.]

11.1 Books at the principal office; both Members have full inspection rights at any time.
11.2 Banking: Company accounts only; both Members are signatories; no commingling. Dual approval above $[5,000] per transaction.
11.3 Fiscal year: calendar. Members receive annual statements and K-1s, and monthly closes per the Company's existing finance rhythm.

### Article 12 — Dissolution

12.2 Wind-up order: creditors → reserves → capital accounts → 50/50.

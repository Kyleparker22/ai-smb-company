**Melanie is NOT retired. She is one of the most active agents in the OS.** This folder is an orphan
from before her loops were named, holding a single artifact from 2026-06-11.

Melanie runs **two** scheduled loops today, both with live systemd timers:

| loop | timer | output |
|---|---|---|
| **melanie-briefing** — the weekday briefing | `yourco-melanie-briefing.timer`, 07:45 ET | `loops/melanie-briefing/` |
| **initiative** — originates up to 3 unscheduled moves/day | `yourco-initiative.timer`, 08:45 ET | `loops/initiative/` |

She is the **conductor / CEO-in-training** in `04_agent_roster.md`, the HQ interface, and she is in
`runtime/agent-registry.json` as sanctioned. Nothing about this archive touches any of that — it moves
one stale June folder whose job `melanie-briefing/` took over.

Archived because a folder named `melanie/` sitting dead next to a very-alive Melanie is the kind of
thing that makes the next reader think the agent stopped. It did not.

## What this is

A public business-card wall at **Poppo's** — roughly **200 cards**, all local St Pete businesses.
The sign on it reads: *"Poppo's supports local businesses. Take a card, leave your card. Thanks!"*

Three things make it worth more than a normal list:

1. **It is exactly the ICP.** Owner-operated St Pete SMBs — trades, real estate, health, services —
   in the metro the in-person channel is already activated for.
2. **The cards were left to be taken.** This is not a scraped list; it is a public, opt-in swap board
   with an explicit invitation. That is a materially cleaner provenance than anything Rafi's posture
   would reject (`agents/rafi/social-platform-scraping-assessment.md`).
3. **Poppo's itself is the better prospect.** A business that runs a 200-card local wall is a hub —
   a natural **connector** recruit, and the kind of relationship worth more than any single card on it.

## The actual next step

The wall is worth mining properly, and one photo cannot do it. **10–15 close-ups**, one per shelf
row, held steady and square to the wall, would make nearly all ~200 cards readable — and at that
point extracting them into a structured list is quick and reliable. A single wide shot is the one
format that cannot work.

Two things worth doing on the same visit:

- **Leave yourco's card.** The sign asks for one and the wall is a standing local-visibility asset.
  ⚠️ Unbranded/in-person is activated; anything that reads as *marketing at scale* is still behind the
  launch-gate. A plain card left on a community board is within the activated scope — a flyer or a
  pitch is not.
- **Talk to whoever runs Poppo's.** They are the hub, and hubs are connector recruits — see the
  connector ICP in `processes/partnerships/`. Worth more than the cards.

## Handling rules for whatever comes off this wall

- These are **cold** contacts. yourco's cold outbound remains gated; the activated path is in-person
  and warm. Do not bulk-load them into a sequence.
- One at a time into the CRM, via `promote-warm-lead`, **after** a real check that the business exists
  and fits.
- If a connector ever hands over names from a wall like this, that is a **Sourcer** submission and the
  provenance answer is *"a public card wall at Poppo's,"* not *"I know them"* — the distinction is the
  whole point of the required field (`decisions/2026-08-11_connector-program-v2.md`, checklist 17a).

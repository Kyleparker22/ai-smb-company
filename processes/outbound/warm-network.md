# Warm-network pass — the first clients usually come from here

> The least glamorous, highest-trust, lowest-cost channel — and the one that actually lands a pre-revenue founder's first clients. This is a **finite, repeatable sprint**: mine everyone you already know, sort them, and reach out *as a person, not a campaign.* Only **the Founder** can run the mining (it's his relationships + judgment); **David** tracks it in the CRM, **Reilly** supports. **Least-blocked channel:** a 1:1 "I'm building something — want a free demo?" to someone you know is relationship-building, not a cold go-to-market blast — arguably startable earlier than cold outbound (the Founder's call vs. the internal-only mandate).

## The mindset (read first)
Warm outreach is **not** the cold sequence. No merge fields, no pitch, no "touch cadence." It's a personal note that **gives first**: you built them (or someone they know) a free demo, and you'd genuinely value their take. The two moves that work:
1. **Give-first:** "I made you a free demo of an AI front desk for [their business] — want to see it?"
2. **Advice-ask (→ referral):** "I'm building this — who do you know who's drowning in calls?" People who can't use it themselves will *refer* if you ask for advice instead of a sale.

**The Audit is free — for everyone, not just the first three (the Founder, 2026-08-16 — `decisions/2026-08-16_audit-is-free.md`).** The founders'-offer cap of 3 is retired; the give-first move is now the standing offer: *"I'll do a full audit of your business for free — I'll find where you're losing money and put a number on it. If it doesn't find you something worth acting on, you've lost an hour."* **Do not quote a price you are not charging** — the old script said "normally $1,000" to make the waiver read as founders' access, and repeating that now would be a false statement about a fee that does not exist. What to ask for instead, earned rather than demanded: an honest testimonial and intros.

## Step 1 — Mine the names (where to look)
Block 90 minutes, go source by source, dump everyone into a list:
- **Phone contacts** — scroll all of them; flag SMB owners + well-connected people.
- **Email** — search "owner", "founder", company names.
- **LinkedIn 1st-degree** — filter connections by title (Owner/President) + industry.
- **Past colleagues / employers / clients** (from any prior venture).
- **People who serve SMBs you know** — your **accountant, lawyer, banker, insurance agent, realtor**. Each knows *dozens* of SMB owners. (These double as channel-partner targets.)
- **Your own service providers** — your dentist, your landscaper, the restaurant you frequent, your gym. They're **warm + in-vertical + you can literally walk in.** Gold.
- **Community** — neighbors, gym, kids' sports, church, alumni, local founder groups, Chamber.
- **Family & friends** who own a business or work at a fit one.

## Step 2 — Sort each into a tier
- **A · Could be a client** — owns an SMB in a vertical we serve. → give-first demo.
- **B · Connector** — knows many SMB owners (accountant, realtor, banker, other founders). → advice-ask + offer to build their contacts a free demo. (Often also a *channel partner* — cross-ref `partnerships/`.)
- **C · Amplifier** — would share/intro/vouch. → ask them to share the build-in-public content / make one intro.
- **D · Cheerleader / advisor** — believes in you. → keep posted; they refer over time.

## Step 3 — Reach out (personal, 1:1 — pick the variant)
**To a friend who owns a fit business (Tier A):**
> "Hey [name] — I've been building something I think could actually help [business]: an AI employee that answers your calls and books jobs 24/7, even when you're on a site. I made you a quick demo off your website, no strings — want me to send it? Genuinely want your honest take."

**To a connector (Tier B):**
> "Hey [name] — I'm building AI employees for service businesses (they answer the phone, qualify, and book the job, live in 48 hours). You know a ton of [landscapers/owners] — anyone come to mind who's losing business because they can't get to the phone? Happy to build them a free demo, no pressure."

**To a former colleague / founder (Tier C/D):**
> "Building something new — an AI-employee consultancy. Would love your eyes on it, and if anyone you know comes to mind as a fit, I'd be grateful for the intro."

## Step 4 — Track it (David)
Every contact reached → CRM, `source: "warm network"`, with the tier (A/B/C/D) and the relationship. Replies/demos move the stage like any other lead. A "no for now" stays a Tier-D cheerleader, not a dead end.

## Cadence
A **sprint, not a forever channel**: list 50, reach ~10/week, give-first every time. The warm network is finite — work it once, well, then let referrals from it compound.

## Why this beats cold for the first clients
Highest trust, zero cost, fastest yes — and every warm client becomes a **case study + referral source** that powers every other channel. Start here.

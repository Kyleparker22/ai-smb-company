## The fit filter (why these, not others)
A vertical earns a slot when it scores on: **owner/manager decision-maker** (fast sale), **high value per customer** (a few recovered leads pay the retainer), **phone/lead-driven** (missed-call ROI), **lean team** (real owner-drain), **recurring/high-LTV revenue** (margin for $2–10k/mo), and **repetitive automatable ops** (intake/scheduling/quoting/follow-up/recall). The **AI front desk** (missed calls → booked) is the wedge across nearly all of them; the high-value ones add back-office + content + quoting employees (Tier-2 → the bigger retainer).

**Second axis — neglect (added 2026-07-27, Brett).** Among fit-equal verticals, campaign the most *competitively neglected* first: score 0–3 each on **unknown** (no data/coverage) · **buried** (rich sub-niche hidden in a boring sector average) · **misunderstood** (assumed dying/trivial) · **disdained** (low-status work). High-neglect niches have the fewest AI vendors selling into them, the tightest peer/referral networks, and the most durable pricing — the boring-business sweep (#51–53) scored high on this by instinct; this makes it repeatable. Method for future sweeps: de-aggregate a boring sector's average until the out-earning sub-niche shows (how porta-potty split out of septic). Framework: "Invisible Companies" (Barney/Zhang/Neumann, Colossus 2026-07) — verdict + the "target via people and aggregators, not cold screens" reframe in `decisions/2026-07-05_tool-triage.md` §Addendum 07-27.

## Why NOT (the patterns we skip)
Thin-margin retail & most restaurants (can't fund $2–10k/mo; no lead-intake pain) · pure e-commerce (different ops, no phone wedge) · enterprise/regulated procurement (slow, committee-driven — the opposite of "easy in") · **regulated consumer finance + brand-mismatch** (evaluated + rejected 2026-07-05: **bail bonds** — state-by-state licensing, collections from people in crisis, shrinking with bail reform, reputational mismatch with a premium executive-trust brand; **pawn shops & title loans** — TILA/state-usury/CFPB + AML/KYC exposure, and walk-in retail counters have no missed-call leak for our wedge. "Boring" is good; regulated-consumer-finance boring is not).

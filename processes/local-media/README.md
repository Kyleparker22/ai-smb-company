> Demand-gen **channel 7** (`processes/demand-generation.md`), added 2026-07-22 (the Founder's call; `decisions/2026-07-22_local-media-and-social-channels.md`). **Earned media first** — a feature is free, borrows the outlet's trust, and becomes a permanent "as featured in" proof asset in every proposal and outbound touch. The one paid-local exception: cheap **chamber / trade-association newsletter sponsorships, post-launch only** — that readership is literally the 50-list.

## The kit
- `pitch-angles.md` — the story angles + short pitch drafts (the glass-box story is the lead).
- `target-outlets.md` — ranked outlets with fit notes and pitch routes (**contacts must be verified at pitch time — none are confirmed here**).
- `founder-boilerplate.md` — the Founder bio, yourco boilerplate, fact sheet, and the hard external-surface rules that bind every media interaction.

## Sequencing (when the gate clears)
1. **St. Pete Catalyst** first — local-founder coverage is its core beat; most likely first yes, and a Catalyst piece is citable when pitching upward.
2. **Tampa Bay Inno / Tampa Bay Business Journal** — the readership *is* the ICP (SMB owners/operators); highest commercial value.
3. **Tampa Bay Times business desk** — biggest reach; pitch with the Catalyst/TBBJ clip in hand.
4. **Chamber newsletters + member spotlights** (St. Pete, Tampa Bay chambers) — near-free, ICP-dense; rides the existing channel-6 chamber motion.
5. **Trade-association newsletters** (FNGLA, FRSA, ABC/AGC chapters) — tiny, perfect audiences; the only place a small paid sponsorship is allowed, post-launch.

## Why this channel pays twice
Every piece of coverage is an authoritative third-party citation — exactly the signal Mario's AEO/GEO loop optimizes for. A Catalyst or TBBJ feature feeds "AI consultant Tampa / St. Pete" answer-engine results long after the news cycle. Mario logs each placement and works it into the AEO loop the month it lands.

## What "success" is (honest, pre-revenue)
Not leads, at first — **credibility assets**: 1–2 quality placements, the "as featured in" strip on the site, citations for AEO, and warm recognition when outbound says "you may have seen us in…". Judge the channel on that for the first two quarters; lead attribution comes later via the CRM source field (`source: "press — <outlet>"`).

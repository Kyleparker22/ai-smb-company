MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
YourCo LLC
This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of ____________________, 20__ (the "Effective Date"), by and between YourCo LLC, a Florida limited liability company with a principal place of business at 123 Example St, Riverton, FL 33713 ("YourCo", "we", "us", or "our"), and ____________________________________ ("Client", "you", or "your").
YourCo and Client are each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties". This Agreement governs the ongoing relationship between the Parties. The specific services Client orders from YourCo will be described in one or more Statements of Work executed from time to time under this Agreement.
The Parties agree as follows:
1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 Services
"Services" means any services provided by YourCo to Client under this Agreement, as described in a Statement of Work, including without limitation AI-operations consulting, AI agent design and deployment, automation and workflow design, technology strategy, and related implementation, integration, and support services.
1.2 Statement of Work
"Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties that describes specific Services, deliverables, timelines, fees, and any additional terms applicable to a particular engagement. Each SOW is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.
1.3 Client Data
"Client Data" means any data, information, records, or content that Client or Client's authorized personnel provide to YourCo, or that YourCo collects or processes on Client's behalf in connection with the Services, including without limitation business documents, internal communications, customer information, project records, and operational data.
1.4 YourCo Technology
"YourCo Technology" means all software, systems, workflows, prompts, configurations, models, methodologies, evaluation frameworks, internal tools, documentation, and other technology and know-how developed, owned, or licensed by YourCo and used to provide the Services, excluding Client Data.
1.5 AI Systems
"AI Systems" means artificial intelligence, machine learning, large language model, and automated decision-making systems used by YourCo to deliver the Services, including third-party AI services operated by subprocessors.
1.6 Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or electronically, that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, business plans, strategy documents, customer lists, pricing, financial information, technical materials, and Client Data.
1.7 Fees
"Fees" means all amounts payable by Client to YourCo under this Agreement or any SOW, including without limitation one-time fees, recurring retainer fees, and any additional fees for out-of-scope work.
2. SERVICES
2.1 Provision of Services
YourCo will provide the Services to Client in accordance with this Agreement and each executed SOW. The specific scope, deliverables, service levels, and fees applicable to each engagement will be set forth in the corresponding SOW.
2.2 Service Modifications
YourCo may from time to time update, modify, or enhance the Services, provided that such changes do not materially reduce the functionality or quality of the Services as set forth in the applicable SOW. YourCo will provide Client with reasonable notice of any material changes. Client acknowledges that underlying AI models and subprocessors may be updated, replaced, or substituted with equivalent or better-performing services at YourCo's discretion.
2.3 Client Responsibilities
Client will: (a) provide YourCo with timely access to all Client systems, accounts, documents, and information reasonably necessary for YourCo to provide the Services; (b) designate one or more authorized personnel to serve as primary contacts; (c) review and approve any deliverables, recommendations, or AI configurations before they are deployed in Client's production environment; (d) ensure that Client's use of the Services and any data shared with YourCo comply with all applicable laws, including without limitation data privacy and confidentiality laws; and (e) maintain responsibility for final business decisions made in reliance on any outputs of the AI Systems or YourCo's recommendations.
2.4 AI Output Limitations
Client acknowledges and agrees that: (a) AI Systems are probabilistic and may produce outputs that contain errors, omissions, or misinterpretations; (b) YourCo does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or suitability of any specific AI output for any particular purpose; (c) Client is responsible for reviewing and verifying the outputs of AI Systems before relying on them for business decisions; and (d) YourCo is not liable for business decisions, lost opportunities, or other outcomes resulting from AI outputs. The Services are intended to augment, not replace, human judgment.
3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 Fees
Client will pay YourCo the Fees set forth in each SOW. Each SOW will specify whether Fees are one-time, recurring, or both, and will state the timing of payment. Unless otherwise specified in an SOW, one-time fees are due and payable in full upon execution of the SOW (before work begins), and recurring fees are billed in advance and due on the first day of each billing period.
3.2 Payment Method
Client may pay Fees by ACH/wire transfer, Stripe-issued invoice or payment link, credit card, or other electronic payment method designated by YourCo. Where YourCo issues an invoice or payment link, payment is due according to the terms set forth on the invoice or in the applicable SOW. For recurring Fees, Client may authorize YourCo and its payment processors to automatically charge Client's designated payment method on the schedule set forth in the applicable SOW.
3.3 Late Payments
Payments not received within ten (10) days of the due date are considered late and will accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. YourCo reserves the right to suspend Services for any account that is more than fifteen (15) days past due, after providing Client with written notice and a five (5) day cure period. YourCo may recover reasonable costs of collection, including attorneys' fees, incurred in pursuing past-due amounts.
3.4 Taxes
Fees exclude all applicable sales, use, value-added, goods and services, and similar taxes. Client is responsible for paying all such taxes, except for taxes imposed on YourCo's net income. If YourCo is required to collect such taxes, YourCo will invoice Client and remit to the taxing authority.
3.5 Nonrefundable Fees
Except as expressly provided in this Agreement or an applicable SOW, all Fees are nonrefundable. If Client terminates this Agreement or an SOW for any reason other than YourCo's uncured material breach, Client will not be entitled to a refund of any Fees paid, and will remain liable for any Fees accrued through the effective date of termination.
4. TERM AND TERMINATION
4.1 Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated as provided herein. The term of each SOW will be set forth in the SOW. SOWs that establish recurring services (such as retainer engagements) will, unless otherwise specified, have an initial term of three (3) months from the SOW start date (the "Initial SOW Term") and will automatically renew for successive one (1) month periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term. SOWs that establish fixed-scope, fixed-fee engagements (such as one-time audits or projects) will terminate upon completion of the engagement, with no automatic renewal.
4.2 Termination of Recurring SOW for Convenience
With respect to any SOW establishing recurring services: beginning on the day after the end of the Initial SOW Term, either Party may terminate the SOW for convenience by providing the other Party with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Client remains liable for all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination, including recurring Fees for the notice period. Termination of an individual SOW does not terminate this Agreement or any other SOW. With respect to fixed-scope SOWs, termination rights, if any, will be set forth in the SOW.
4.3 Termination for Cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW immediately upon written notice if: (a) the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail; (b) the other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or otherwise ceases to operate as a going concern; or (c) the other Party engages in fraud, willful misconduct, or conduct that materially damages the reputation or legal interests of the non-breaching Party.
4.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination of this Agreement or an SOW: (a) YourCo will cease providing the Services under the affected SOW(s); (b) Client will pay all outstanding Fees through the effective date of termination; (c) each Party will return or destroy Confidential Information of the other Party, subject to the data handling provisions in any Data Processing Addendum; (d) provisions that by their nature should survive (including Sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12) will survive termination; and (e) YourCo will provide reasonable transition assistance for up to thirty (30) days at YourCo's then-current hourly rates, subject to Client's payment of all outstanding Fees.
5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
5.1 YourCo Technology
YourCo retains all right, title, and interest in and to the YourCo Technology, including all intellectual property rights therein. No license or ownership interest in YourCo Technology is transferred to Client except as expressly provided in this Agreement or an applicable SOW. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, an SOW may grant Client a limited license to use specific deliverables (such as AI agent prompts, configurations, or integration code) on the terms set forth in that SOW.
5.2 Client Data
As between the Parties, Client retains all right, title, and interest in and to Client Data. Client grants YourCo a limited, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to access, use, process, reproduce, display, modify, and transmit Client Data solely to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, and as otherwise permitted under any Data Processing Addendum executed by the Parties.
5.3 Feedback
If Client provides YourCo with any suggestions, comments, ideas, or other feedback regarding the Services ("Feedback"), YourCo may use and incorporate such Feedback in any manner without obligation to Client. Client hereby assigns to YourCo all right, title, and interest in and to Feedback.
5.4 AI Training and Model Use
YourCo will not use Client Data to train foundation artificial intelligence models, and will not share Client Data with AI vendors or subprocessors for the purpose of training their general-purpose models. Where YourCo uses third-party AI services to deliver the Services, YourCo will configure those services, to the extent the third-party provider offers the option, to opt out of training on Client Data and to disable the third-party's retention of Client Data beyond what is operationally necessary. YourCo may use aggregated, de-identified data derived from Services delivery for internal quality improvement, provided that such data cannot reasonably be used to identify Client or any individual.
5.5 Residual Rights
Nothing in this Agreement prevents YourCo from developing, using, or commercializing services, products, or technologies that are similar to or competitive with those provided to Client, or from providing similar services to other clients, including in the same industry as Client. YourCo's use of general knowledge, skill, and experience gained in the course of performing the Services, including patterns and learnings retained in YourCo's memory or general expertise, is permitted, provided that no Confidential Information of Client is disclosed.
6. CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Mutual Obligations
Each Party (the "Receiving Party") agrees to: (a) use Confidential Information of the other Party (the "Disclosing Party") solely for purposes of performing its obligations or exercising its rights under this Agreement; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to employees, contractors, advisors, and subprocessors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement; and (c) protect the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party using at least the same degree of care that the Receiving Party uses to protect its own Confidential Information, but in no event less than reasonable care.
6.2 Exceptions
Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was known to the Receiving Party before disclosure without obligation of confidence; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction on use or disclosure.
6.3 Required Disclosure
If the Receiving Party is required by law, subpoena, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it will (to the extent legally permitted) provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice and cooperate reasonably in any effort by the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.
6.4 Duration
The confidentiality obligations in this Section 6 will continue for a period of three (3) years after the termination of this Agreement, except with respect to Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret, which will be protected for as long as it remains a trade secret under applicable law.
7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
7.1 Mutual Representations
Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; (b) this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation enforceable against it in accordance with its terms; and (c) its performance of this Agreement will not conflict with or breach any other agreement to which it is a party.
7.2 Services Warranty
YourCo warrants that it will provide the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards. Client's sole and exclusive remedy, and YourCo's sole and exclusive liability, for breach of this warranty is for YourCo to re-perform the non-conforming Services at no additional charge, provided that Client notifies YourCo in writing within thirty (30) days of the alleged non-conformance.
7.3 Client Representations
Client represents and warrants that: (a) Client has the necessary rights, licenses, consents, and permissions to provide Client Data to YourCo and to authorize YourCo to process Client Data in accordance with this Agreement; (b) Client's use of the Services complies and will continue to comply with all applicable laws, including without limitation data privacy laws, confidentiality obligations to Client's own customers, and industry-specific regulations applicable to Client's business; and (c) Client will not use the Services to engage in, or enable others to engage in, unlawful, harmful, deceptive, or abusive activities.
7.4 Disclaimer
EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND "AS AVAILABLE". YOURCO EXPRESSLY DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED, STATUTORY, OR OTHERWISE, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, TITLE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT. YOURCO DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SERVICES WILL BE UNINTERRUPTED, ERROR-FREE, OR SECURE, OR THAT AI OUTPUTS WILL BE ACCURATE OR SUITABLE FOR ANY PARTICULAR USE. YOURCO MAKES NO REPRESENTATIONS OR WARRANTIES REGARDING REVENUE, COST SAVINGS, TIME SAVINGS, OPERATIONAL OUTCOMES, OR OTHER BUSINESS RESULTS.
8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
8.1 Exclusion of Damages
TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER PARTY OR ANY THIRD PARTY FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, LOST BUSINESS, LOSS OF DATA, OR LOSS OF GOODWILL, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER BASED ON BREACH OF CONTRACT, BREACH OF WARRANTY, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE), PRODUCT LIABILITY, OR ANY OTHER THEORY, EVEN IF THE PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.
8.2 Liability Cap
EXCEPT FOR LIABILITIES ARISING FROM SECTION 9 (INDEMNIFICATION), BREACHES OF SECTION 6 (CONFIDENTIALITY) OR SECTION 5.4 (AI TRAINING), OR A PARTY'S FRAUD OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THREE (3) TIMES THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT TO YOURCO UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.
8.3 Basis of the Bargain
The Parties agree that the limitations and exclusions of liability in this Section 8 are an essential element of the basis of the bargain between them and that the Fees would be substantially higher if these provisions did not apply.
9. INDEMNIFICATION
9.1 YourCo Indemnification
YourCo will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any third-party claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any allegation that the Services, as provided by YourCo and used by Client in accordance with this Agreement, infringe or misappropriate any third party's intellectual property rights. YourCo's obligations under this Section 9.1 do not apply to the extent a claim arises from: (a) Client Data; (b) Client's modifications to the Services or to YourCo deliverables; (c) Client's combination of the Services with products, services, or data not provided by YourCo; or (d) Client's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or applicable law.
9.2 Client Indemnification
Client will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless YourCo and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any third-party claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) Client Data, including any claim that Client Data infringes a third party's rights, was collected unlawfully, or violates applicable privacy or confidentiality laws; (b) Client's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or applicable law; (c) Client's products, services, or business operations; or (d) Client's failure to obtain necessary consents from Client's own customers, employees, or counterparties for Client to share with YourCo any data relating to those persons.
9.3 Procedure
The indemnified Party will: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party of the claim; (b) provide the indemnifying Party with sole control of the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement admitting fault on behalf of the indemnified Party or imposing obligations on the indemnified Party other than payment of money may be made without the indemnified Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld); and (c) reasonably cooperate with the indemnifying Party's defense at the indemnifying Party's expense.
10. INSURANCE
During the Term, YourCo will maintain, at its own expense, the following insurance coverage: (a) commercial general liability insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate; (b) professional liability / errors and omissions insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per claim and $1,000,000 aggregate; and (c) cyber liability insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per claim. Upon Client's written request, YourCo will provide certificates of insurance evidencing such coverage.
11. DATA PROTECTION AND SUBPROCESSORS
11.1 Data Processing Addendum
To the extent YourCo processes personal information (as defined under applicable data privacy laws) on behalf of Client, the Parties will execute a separate Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") concurrently with the first applicable SOW. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the DPA with respect to the processing of personal information, the DPA will control.
11.2 Subprocessors
YourCo may engage third-party subprocessors (such as cloud infrastructure providers, AI service providers, and software-as-a-service tools) to provide the Services. YourCo will maintain, and provide to Client upon request, a current list of subprocessors that process Client Data. YourCo will provide Client with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any new or replacement subprocessor that will process Client Data; if Client reasonably objects to a new subprocessor on data protection grounds, the Parties will work in good faith to resolve the objection, and if no resolution is reached, Client may terminate the affected SOW for convenience without penalty.
11.3 YourCo's Responsibility
YourCo remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subprocessors with respect to the Services to the same extent as it would be responsible for its own acts and omissions.
12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
12.1 Informal Resolution
Before initiating formal dispute resolution, the Parties will attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or related to this Agreement through informal discussions between authorized representatives for a period of at least fifteen (15) days.
12.2 Mediation
If the Parties are unable to resolve a dispute through informal discussions, the Parties will submit the dispute to non-binding mediation before a single mediator selected by mutual agreement or, failing agreement within fifteen (15) days, appointed by the American Arbitration Association under its Mediation Procedures. Mediation will take place in Pinellas County, Florida, unless the Parties agree otherwise, and will be completed within forty-five (45) days of the initial request for mediation. The Parties will share the mediator's fees and costs equally and each Party will bear its own attorneys' fees and costs.
12.3 Litigation
If mediation does not resolve the dispute, the dispute will be resolved exclusively in the state and federal courts located in Pinellas County, Florida. Each Party consents to personal jurisdiction in those courts and waives any objection to venue. Each Party will bear its own attorneys' fees and costs unless awarded by the court.
12.4 Equitable Relief
Notwithstanding the foregoing, either Party may seek temporary or preliminary injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent or enjoin a breach of Section 5 (Intellectual Property) or Section 6 (Confidentiality), without first exhausting the dispute resolution procedures in this Section 12.
12.5 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
12.6 Waiver of Class Actions and Jury Trial
THE PARTIES AGREE THAT ANY DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCEEDINGS WILL BE CONDUCTED ONLY ON AN INDIVIDUAL BASIS AND NOT IN A CLASS, CONSOLIDATED, OR REPRESENTATIVE ACTION. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL.
13. GENERAL PROVISIONS
13.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with all executed SOWs and any DPA, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, representations, or communications, whether oral or written.
13.2 Order of Precedence
In the event of any conflict between the documents comprising this Agreement, the order of precedence is: (a) any DPA (with respect to data processing matters only); (b) the applicable SOW; (c) this Agreement.
13.3 Amendments
No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.
13.4 Assignment
Neither Party may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets. Any attempted assignment in violation of this section is void.
13.5 Force Majeure
Neither Party will be liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including without limitation acts of God, natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, civil unrest, government action, labor disputes, internet or telecommunications failures, or failures of third-party services.
13.6 Notices
All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and will be deemed given when: (a) delivered personally; (b) sent by certified or registered mail, return receipt requested; (c) sent by nationally recognized overnight courier; or (d) sent by email to the address on file, with confirmation of receipt. Notices to YourCo will be sent to founder@yourco.example.com and to the address on the first page of this Agreement.
13.7 Waiver
No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party. A Party's failure to enforce any provision does not constitute a waiver of that provision or any other provision.
13.8 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision will be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable.
13.9 Independent Contractors
The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates any agency, partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship between the Parties.
13.10 No Third-Party Beneficiaries
This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns. Nothing in this Agreement creates any rights in or for any third party.
13.11 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Electronic signatures and copies of signatures delivered by email or electronic signature platform are deemed originals.
13.12 Publicity
YourCo will not use Client's name, logo, or other identifying information in any external communication (including websites, marketing materials, sales presentations, social media, or press releases) without Client's prior written consent. Notwithstanding the foregoing, YourCo may reference the engagement on an anonymized basis (with no Client name, logo, or identifying detail) in case studies, benchmark data, methodology descriptions, and similar materials. Either Party may at any time grant or revoke specific consents by written notice to the other Party.

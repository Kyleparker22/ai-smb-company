DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM
To the Master Services Agreement between YourCo LLC and Client
This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is entered into pursuant to, and forms part of, the Master Services Agreement (the "MSA") between YourCo LLC ("YourCo") and the Client identified in the MSA ("Client"). Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the MSA. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the MSA with respect to the Processing of Client Data or Personal Information, this DPA will control.
1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 Personal Information
"Personal Information" means any information within Client Data that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with a particular individual, including without limitation names, business contact information, employment details, content of business communications, and any other identifiers.
1.2 Data Subject
"Data Subject" means the identified or identifiable individual to whom Personal Information relates.
1.3 Processing
"Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Information, including collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, transmission, alignment, restriction, erasure, or destruction.
1.4 Privacy Laws
"Privacy Laws" means all laws applicable to the Processing of Personal Information under this DPA, including without limitation the Florida Information Protection Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act ("VCDPA"), the Colorado Privacy Act ("CPA"), and other U.S. state privacy laws, as each may be amended from time to time.
1.5 Security Incident
"Security Incident" means any unauthorized access to, acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of Personal Information Processed by YourCo in connection with the Services.
1.6 Subprocessor
"Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by YourCo to Process Personal Information in connection with the Services.
2. ROLES OF THE PARTIES
For purposes of the Services and Processing of Client Data: Client is the "Business" or "Controller" (as such terms are defined under applicable Privacy Laws), and YourCo is the "Service Provider" or "Processor". Each Party will comply with its respective obligations under applicable Privacy Laws.
3. SCOPE AND PURPOSE OF PROCESSING
3.1 Categories of Data Subjects
Personal Information Processed under this DPA may relate to the following categories of Data Subjects:
	•	Client's partners, principals, employees, contractors, and other authorized personnel.
	•	Client's customers, prospective customers, and other counterparties whose information appears in materials Client shares with YourCo (such as proposals, project records, internal communications, or operational data).
3.2 Categories of Personal Information
The categories of Personal Information Processed under this DPA may include:
	•	Business contact identifiers: names, business email addresses, business phone numbers, business addresses, job titles, and organizational affiliations.
	•	Communication content: business emails, messages, meeting notes, project documents, and other written or recorded business communications.
	•	Operational and project data: project records, deliverables, internal process documentation, time-allocation data, and tool usage information.
	•	Technical metadata: timestamps, document version history, AI agent interaction logs, and similar operational telemetry.
3.3 Purposes of Processing
Personal Information will be Processed for the following purposes:
	•	Delivering the Services described in the MSA and any SOW.
	•	Configuring, building, testing, and operating AI agents and automations on Client's behalf.
	•	Producing reports, analyses, and deliverables for Client.
	•	Operating, maintaining, and improving the Services through aggregated, de-identified analysis (as permitted under Section 5 of this DPA).
	•	Complying with legal obligations and responding to Data Subject or regulatory requests.
	•	Enforcing the Parties' rights under the MSA.
3.4 Duration
YourCo will Process Personal Information for the duration of the MSA and for the additional retention periods permitted under Section 8 of this DPA.
4. YOURCO'S OBLIGATIONS
YourCo will:
	•	Process Personal Information only on documented instructions from Client, including as set forth in the MSA, an SOW, or this DPA, except where otherwise required by applicable law.
	•	Not sell or share Personal Information, as those terms are defined under applicable Privacy Laws.
	•	Not retain, use, or disclose Personal Information outside the direct business relationship with Client, or for any purpose (including marketing to Data Subjects) other than providing the Services or as permitted under this DPA.
	•	Not combine Personal Information received from Client with personal information received from or on behalf of another entity, or collected from YourCo's own separate interactions with any Data Subject, except as necessary to provide the Services.
	•	Ensure that personnel and contractors authorized to Process Personal Information are bound by obligations of confidentiality.
	•	Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures as described in Section 7 below.
	•	Notify Client promptly if YourCo determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under applicable Privacy Laws.
5. AGGREGATED DATA AND AI MODEL USE
5.1 Aggregated and De-Identified Data
YourCo may create aggregated, de-identified, and/or anonymized data derived from Personal Information ("Aggregated Data") and may use such Aggregated Data for internal quality improvement, service operation, analytics, and product development, provided that: (a) the Aggregated Data cannot reasonably be used to identify Client or any Data Subject; (b) YourCo implements technical safeguards and business processes to prohibit re-identification; and (c) YourCo does not attempt to re-identify the data or disclose it in a manner that would allow re-identification.
5.2 No Training of Foundation Models
YourCo will not use Personal Information to train foundation artificial intelligence models, and will not share Personal Information with AI Subprocessors for the purpose of training their general-purpose models. Where YourCo's AI Subprocessors offer data-training opt-outs, YourCo will exercise such opt-outs on behalf of Client and will configure those services, to the extent the third-party provider offers the option, to disable the third-party's retention of Personal Information beyond what is operationally necessary.

6.2 Changes to Subprocessors
YourCo may add, replace, or remove Subprocessors from time to time. YourCo will provide Client with at least thirty (30) days' advance written notice of any new or replacement Subprocessor that will Process Personal Information. Client may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable, good-faith grounds related to data protection within the notice period. If Client reasonably objects, the Parties will work together in good faith to find a mutually acceptable resolution; if no resolution is reached, Client may terminate the affected SOW for convenience without penalty.
6.3 Subprocessor Obligations
YourCo will ensure that each Subprocessor is bound by written contractual obligations that are no less protective than those in this DPA. YourCo remains liable to Client for the acts and omissions of each Subprocessor to the same extent YourCo would be liable if performing the services directly.
7. SECURITY MEASURES
YourCo will implement and maintain reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including:
	•	Encryption of Personal Information in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent), to the extent the underlying systems support such encryption.
	•	Access controls based on the principle of least privilege, multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts, and prompt revocation of access when no longer needed.
	•	Regular review of access logs and anomaly monitoring on systems that store or transmit Personal Information.
	•	Reliance on Subprocessors that maintain industry-recognized security certifications or attestations (such as SOC 2) where commercially available.
	•	Personnel and contractor practices, including written confidentiality obligations and reasonable training on data protection and AI-specific risks.
	•	A documented incident response approach (including the Incident Runbook maintained internally by YourCo), reviewed periodically and updated as YourCo's stack and risks evolve.
8. RETENTION AND DELETION
8.1 Retention During Term
During the Term, YourCo will retain Personal Information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services and as directed by Client.
8.2 Deletion on Request
YourCo will delete or return Personal Information upon Client's written request within thirty (30) days, except to the extent that retention is required by applicable law or is necessary for the defense of legal claims.
8.3 Deletion Upon Termination
Within thirty (30) days after termination of the MSA or any SOW under which Personal Information was Processed, YourCo will, at Client's election, either return all Personal Information to Client in a reasonably usable format or securely delete such Personal Information. YourCo may retain Personal Information as required by applicable law, provided that such retained data remains subject to the protection obligations of this DPA. Aggregated, de-identified data that cannot be used to identify Client or any Data Subject may be retained indefinitely.
9. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
YourCo will provide reasonable assistance to Client to enable Client to respond to Data Subject requests under applicable Privacy Laws, including requests to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port Personal Information. If YourCo receives a Data Subject request directly, YourCo will promptly forward the request to Client and will not respond to the Data Subject except to acknowledge receipt or as otherwise instructed by Client or required by law.
10. SECURITY INCIDENTS
YourCo will notify Client of any Security Incident affecting Client's Personal Information without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of the Security Incident. The notification will include, to the extent known at the time of notification:
	•	The nature of the Security Incident.
	•	The categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected.
	•	The likely consequences of the Security Incident.
	•	The measures taken or proposed to address the Security Incident and mitigate its effects.
YourCo will cooperate reasonably with Client in Client's investigation, remediation, and regulatory notification obligations arising from the Security Incident. Neither Party will make any public statement attributing a Security Incident to the other Party without the other Party's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld), except as required by law.
11. AUDIT AND ATTESTATION
Upon Client's written request (not more than once per calendar year unless a Security Incident or regulatory requirement justifies greater frequency), YourCo will provide Client with: (a) a written summary of its information security program and practices; (b) copies of any third-party security audit reports or certifications (such as SOC 2 reports) that YourCo or its Subprocessors have obtained, to the extent YourCo has the right to share them; and (c) reasonable responses to a written security questionnaire provided by Client. In-person audits may be conducted only if required by a regulator or based on a good-faith concern that cannot be addressed through the foregoing means, on reasonable advance written notice, during normal business hours, and subject to appropriate confidentiality and fee arrangements.
12. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS
The Parties acknowledge that Personal Information will primarily be Processed in the United States. If any Personal Information is transferred to jurisdictions outside the United States, YourCo will ensure that such transfers are carried out in compliance with applicable Privacy Laws, including through the use of standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms where required.
13. STATE-SPECIFIC PROVISIONS
13.1 California (CCPA/CPRA)
For Personal Information subject to the CCPA/CPRA, YourCo certifies that it: (a) will not sell or share Personal Information; (b) will not retain, use, or disclose Personal Information for any purpose other than for the specific purpose of performing the Services specified in the MSA, or as otherwise permitted by the CCPA/CPRA; (c) will not retain, use, or disclose Personal Information outside of the direct business relationship with Client; and (d) will not combine Personal Information received from Client with personal information received from another entity or collected from YourCo's own interactions with the Data Subject, except as permitted by the CCPA/CPRA.
13.2 Other States
For Personal Information subject to the VCDPA, CPA, or other state privacy laws that impose substantially similar obligations, YourCo will provide assistance and comply with requirements substantially equivalent to those set forth for CCPA/CPRA above, adapted to the specific requirements of the applicable state law.
14. GENERAL
14.1 Effective Date and Term
This DPA is effective on the effective date of the MSA and continues for the duration of the MSA. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination will survive.
14.2 Amendment
YourCo may amend this DPA without Client's prior consent only to the extent reasonably necessary to comply with changes in applicable Privacy Laws or regulatory requirements, by providing Client with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice describing the change and the legal basis. Any other amendment to this DPA, including changes to YourCo's data-handling practices that are not required by law, requires the mutual written consent of both Parties. Subprocessor changes are governed exclusively by Section 6.2 and are not considered amendments to this DPA.
14.3 Order of Precedence
In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the MSA or any SOW regarding the Processing of Personal Information, this DPA controls.
14.4 Limitation of Liability
Any claims under this DPA are subject to the limitation of liability provisions in the MSA.
SIGNATURES
The Parties, intending to be legally bound, have executed this DPA as of the effective date of the MSA by their duly authorized representatives.

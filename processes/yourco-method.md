# The yourco Method — Discover · Distill · Design · Deploy · Deepen

> yourco's branded client process — the canonical names + framing for the Audit-first → custom-AI-OS motion (`decisions/2026-06-16_audit-first-os-as-product.md`). Use these names consistently on the site, in sales (Reilly/Michelle/Bella), in proposals, and in the demo/audit collateral. The throughline: **yourco means "I learn" — we learn your business first, then build the system to run it.**

## The five steps
| # | Step | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discover** | The **Audit**: diagnose how the business runs, find the bottleneck costing the most, quantified in the client's own numbers. | Bella |
| 2 | **Distill** | Capture how the work actually happens — workflows, the owner's judgment, what only they know — into a living operating manual. | Bella → Kimi (scaffolder) |
| 3 | **Design** | Shape a **custom AI OS** to the business from what was learned — the right capabilities working as one system, not a template. | Kimi + scaffolder (`runtime/scaffold_engagement.py`) · Kemba's template |
| 4 | **Deploy** | First capability **live in 48 hours**. Client approves anything customer-facing; yourco owns reliability, security, evals. | Kimi · Janice (provisioning) |
| 5 | **Deepen** | Operate it, refine weekly, expand the OS as it earns its place — it gets sharper the longer it runs. | Kortney (health) · Bird (expansion) · the learnings loop |

## Why the names
- **Branded + ownable** — a clean 5-D arc ("the yourco Method") that's memorable without being gimmicky (writing-rules-compliant: no jargon-as-drama).
- **They encode the strategy:** *Discover → Distill* = learn the business first (the moat-fit + what the incumbents skip); *Design → Deploy* = build the custom OS fast; *Deepen* = the operated, compounding relationship (where retention + the cross-business pattern library live).
- **"yourco = I learn"** — the method literally embodies the name. Lead with it.

## How it maps to the offering hierarchy
The Method always steers toward the **custom AI OS** (the product + primary revenue). A single employee is the entry rung, surfaced last — landing there is a win, not a concession (`decisions/2026-08-10_lead-high-land-anywhere.md`). Hierarchy: **custom AI OS > a few employees > single employee.**

Homepage (`index.html`) + the method page (`positioning.html`) + the Audit offer page + per-vertical landing pages + Reilly/Michelle outbound + Bella's audit readout + proposals. Anywhere the question is "how does yourco work," the answer is the five steps.

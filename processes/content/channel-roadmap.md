Nothing here is live — staged like everything external until the launch gate (`processes/launch-runbook.md`). Owners (function-split per the roster, 2026-06-15): **Katie** = scripting + **posting to the social platforms**; **Reed** = **all content production** (video + social visuals); **Mario** = answer-engine + trend signal; **Brett** = which bets are worth making.

## The principle
Every channel is the same engine pointed at a new surface: a real YourCo workflow → proof-led content → published under approval, applying `brand/writing-rules.md`. We don't chase channels for presence; we add one when there's a repeatable trigger and a believable proof angle. Add the channel only when its eval bar (accuracy, brand, claims) can be held.

## The event-trigger mechanic (the highest-leverage new idea)
Real-world events create sudden, concentrated demand in specific verticals — and the business that shows up first wins. The trigger:
1. **Mario** (already runs answer-engine visibility) watches Google Trends + event signals per active vertical.
2. On a spike (hail storm in a roofing market, heat wave for HVAC, freeze for plumbing), it flags the event + the affected geo/vertical.
3. **Katie scripts** the blast (post + Short copy + a per-vertical demo angle) → **Reed produces** the assets → **Katie posts** it for that moment.
4. **Human approves** before anything publishes — no auto-fire. (Roofing/insurance seasonality is **Polo's** domain — coordinate, since storm-surge timing is the whole pitch there.)

This is also a *client* feature, not just YourCo's own marketing: a client's content/marketing agent can run the same event-trigger for *their* business — a concrete Tier-2 expansion angle (a "ride the demand spike" employee).

- **Parked:** Reddit *automation* (compliance) — manual human posting is fine.

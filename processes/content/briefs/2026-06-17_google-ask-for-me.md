## The angle (yourco's POV)
The phone call just changed. A growing share of the calls hitting local businesses aren't customers — they're **AI agents asking for a price on a customer's behalf.** A human receptionist, mid-job, fumbles a scripted robot caller (or never picks up). The businesses that **win the booking** will be the ones whose front desk answers that AI cleanly — accurate price range, real availability, captured lead. That's not science fiction; it's shipping nationwide this summer. *Is your business ready to be interviewed by an AI?*

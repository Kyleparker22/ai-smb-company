# Content engine — founder-led, proof-first

> The compounding channel (`processes/demand-generation.md` #2). yourco doesn't *claim* it's good at AI — it **shows** it: the company runs itself on AI, the demos work, the reliability is visible. Founder-led, because a solo technical founder building this in the open is the most credible, least-copyable signal there is. Owner: **the Founder** (voice/face) + **Katie** (scripting + social posting) + **Reed** (all content production — video + social visuals) + the **content loop** (reads what landed → adjusts next week). **Internal-only:** draft + stage a backlog now; publish at launch. *(Build-in-public is the one pillar that's arguably about building, not selling the blocked service — startable earlier if the Founder chooses; his call.)*

## The 5 pillars
1. **Glass box / build-in-public** — "I'm building an AI consultancy that runs itself on AI — here's the actual OS." The most ownable: nobody else can show their own company being operated by the product they sell. (the runtime, the agent roster, the dashboard, the loops)
2. **Proof / demos** — the Instant Employee + personalized demos. "Watch a working AI front desk for a real business." Show, don't tell.
3. **Teach the vertical** — practical, SEO-friendly: "how a landscaper stops losing $X/mo to missed calls." Useful to the exact buyer.
4. **Reliability / honesty POV** — thought leadership on the moat: "why most AI agents fail in production — and the layer nobody sells." Owns the eval/approval narrative.
5. **The franchise** — recurring "What my AI employees did this week" (the content loop). Serialized proof.

## The carousel format (LinkedIn primary, Instagram repurpose)
One slide deck → a LinkedIn document post **and** an Instagram carousel (same copy, different export). **Katie owns the script** (slide-by-slide copy + structure); **Reed produces** the rendered deck (brand kit, under Luka's voice/visual rules); **Katie posts** to LinkedIn/Instagram. (Function-split per the roster, 2026-06-15: Katie scripts + posts to social; Reed produces; Webb publishes to the site.) the Founder-approval before any post; internal-staged until launch like everything else.
- **Structure (6–10 slides):** Slide 1 = the hook (scroll-stopper claim/question, no logo clutter). Slides 2…n−1 = one idea per slide, building the argument, ≤25 words each. Slide n−1 = the payoff/takeaway. Slide n = a soft CTA ("see yours" / follow / comment).
- **Specs:** 1080×1350 portrait (best for both feeds). LinkedIn = PDF/document; Instagram = exported PNGs. Big type, lots of whitespace, brass-on-indigo per brand kit, one idea per frame — readable on a phone with no sound.
- **Best-fit themes:** teach-the-vertical ("how a landscaper loses $X/mo to missed calls" → one leak per slide), reliability POV ("why 80% of AI agents die in production" → the layers nobody sells), glass box ("18 AI employees, one human" → the org chart), and the weekly franchise.

## Angle menu (Katie's brief starter — pick an archetype, not a blank page)
Seven repeatable angles to generate a post/carousel against any pillar or vertical. The two that fit yourco best are bolded — they carry the moat narrative.
1. **Contrarian, data-backed** — challenge the conventional take ("most 'AI agents' are demos that die in production"). Our strongest lane — owns the reliability/eval POV.
2. **Comparative deep-dive** — yourco vs. the alternative (no-code operator / DIY / hire a human). Positions the operated moat without naming names.
3. Pain-point inversion — start from the buyer's frustration ("you lose $X/mo to missed calls"), end on the fix. The teach-the-vertical spine.
4. Behind-the-scenes — process reveal that builds trust ("here's the approval gate refusing to send"). The glass-box pillar.
5. Hero's journey — a transformation/case study (client before→after) — unlocks once we have live outcomes.
6. Trend analysis — tie a current event (Google "Ask for Me", AI Mode) to the buyer's world. Mario/Brett feed these.
7. Future-casting — where this goes ("the front door to your business is becoming an agent"). Use sparingly; pairs with proof.
8. Milestone-as-content — company news delivered as a candid founder video, not a press release (launch day, first client, first save, even honest setbacks). The Portnoy "emergency press conference" pattern: the announcement *is* the content, and the candor is the trust signal. Use for real milestones only — never manufacture one (triage: `decisions/2026-07-05_tool-triage.md` §Portnoy addendum).

*Voice constraint:* every draft runs through `brand/writing-rules.md` as an **injectable constraint block** (tone + vocabulary + forbidden phrases), not just a style reference — the rules are a guardrail on the prompt, the way a brand's "Voice DNA" should be.

## The loop
Content loop reads engagement (what hooked, what converted to a site visit / capture) → writes a `learnings/` note → next week's backlog reweights toward what worked. Closed-loop, like every yourco process.

## Source material — the agent extracts and remixes, it never invents (added 2026-08-09)
**Standing constraint on every generated asset.** Never hand a model a blank prompt ("write a good LinkedIn post"); hand it **real human material** and let it *extract* the idea, then remix per the flow above. `brand/writing-rules.md` governs how the output must read; this governs what the input must be — and it's the actual anti-slop control, because generic input produces generic output no style guide can rescue.

**Where yourco's material comes from** (we have a better substrate than the tools that sell this): Granola transcripts of real calls · the audit findings and discovery notes · `learnings/` and `decisions/` (the "why we chose X" posts write themselves) · client work in `clients/<name>/` (subject to the white-label + external-surface rules) · sessions like this one. **The rule of thumb: if the claim in a post can't be traced to something that actually happened, it doesn't ship.** That is the same standard as the credibility gate, applied one step earlier.

Practical form: a recurring short interview/voice-note ("what did you learn this week / what did a prospect say that surprised you") is enough to keep the queue fed — the *conversation* is the raw material, not the writing session.

## Competitive-creative intelligence (a free swipe file — use now)
**Human outliers are the fix for agent entropy (added 2026-08-09).** Left to loop on its own, a generative content agent converges on the same few ideas forever — more volume, no new signal. The fix is to track a small set (~10) of **real human creators** in the category, watch for the **outliers** in their output (the post that broke out, not their average), and remix *those* — a fresh idea source that refreshes itself without a human curating a backlog. This is the organic twin of the Meta Ad Library method below (an ad still running months later = validated; a post that broke out = validated).
**Feed it only from the compliant collector we already run** — `runtime/intent_collect.py` (official YouTube Data API + Google News / Google Alerts RSS). **No platform scraping** to build the creator set or read their engagement: scraping LinkedIn/X engagement graphs is a settled no (`agents/rafi/social-platform-scraping-assessment.md`), and it is precisely how the tools selling this pattern implement it. Sadie owns the collector; Katie/Mario consume. Triage: `decisions/2026-07-05_tool-triage.md` §Addendum 08-09.

Before drafting in a vertical, mine the **Meta Ad Library** (public) for the competitors' ads. The signal: an ad that **started months ago AND is still running** is market-validated — they're paying to keep it live because it works. Tear it down (hook, structure, the one CTA, the outcome it sells) and let it inform Katie's copy + Reilly's outreach — adapt the *proven angle*, never copy. Costs nothing and needs no tool (manual library search). The automated version of this method ("Advertising Ops" — scrape long-running+active ads, frame/transcript teardown, generate via Higgsfield) is the creative engine we plug in **when paid ads turn on** (`decisions/2026-06-12_paid-ads-stance.md`); the *insight* is usable today.

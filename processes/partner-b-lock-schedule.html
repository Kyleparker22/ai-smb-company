Rules for the run

Four things that decide whether "locked" means anything a month from now.

  1. Every lock produces a decision entry the same day. A domain locked with nothing in decisions/ is not locked — it is a conversation that was had once.
  2. Change one value, sweep every surface. Locked numbers and names live in more than one place — site, packets, CRM, CLAUDE.md. Fix them in the same commit, and add the invariant to runtime/consistency-check.py if a human caught the drift by eye.
  3. Membership is three. the Founder 50 / Partner B 35 / Mike 15. Anything still written for two partners is wrong, including inside the financial model.
  4. Nothing locked here goes external until the launch-gate clears.

## The sorting rule
- **Employee (single shape):** one job / one workflow. $1–5k setup + $1,500–2,500/mo (top band = compliance/production). Up to 3 stacked, then graduate.
- **AI OS:** a coordinated, multi-agent system running a domain / department / whole business. $2–5k implementation + $3–10k/mo. The flagship.
- **Cross-cutting filters:** *operated-for-the-client* (on-model) vs *self-serve* (the parked lane — deletes the moat); *regulatory drag* (health/legal/tax → licensed pro + counsel in the loop, per the yourco Care precedent, `decisions/2026-06-16_caregiving-dtc-offering.md`).

## A. Employees (single-job shapes) — B2B / individual

### A1. Diligence copilot (ETA / search funds)
- **Type:** Employee (1–2 agents) · **Buyer:** individual acquirers, searchers, small holdcos.
- **What:** ingest a CIM / data room → risk flags, diligence question lists, first-cut quality-of-earnings view. Pre-close (distinct from the post-close Company OS, B1 below).
- **Pricing:** per-deal fee or a low monthly retainer (the buyer runs few deals at once).
- **Status:** build candidate. Front-of-funnel complement to the Company OS. Reach via the ETA/Searchfunder/Acquiring-Minds peer community.

### A2. Deposition / legal-exhibit prep
- **Type:** Employee · **Buyer:** solo & small-firm litigators.
- **What:** assemble documents, timelines, and exhibits from a matter's files — the manual, painful part of litigation prep.
- **Pricing:** $1–5k setup + ~$1,500–2,500/mo (legal = top band).
- **Status:** build candidate. ⚠️ accuracy-critical; crowded legal-tech, slow cycles. **Design partner: Sample Contact (law firm — CRM `c25`).**

### A3. Reference / win-loss intelligence
- **Type:** Employee · **Buyer:** sales teams.
- **What:** record + structure reference, win/loss, and discovery calls into reusable sales intelligence, testimonials, and auto-drafted case studies (productizes the Nuggets / Pain-Grenades / Peer-Moves framework).
- **Pricing:** $1–5k setup + $1,500/mo.
- **Status:** build candidate. Clean — no regulatory drag. Novel.

### A4. Institutional-knowledge capture ("the memory layer")
- **Type:** Employee · **Buyer:** any org with knowledge walking out the door (SMB acquisitions, consultancies losing seniors).
- **What:** capture a departing owner/employee's institutional knowledge into a queryable agentic knowledge base. Sells the single sharp pain: "don't lose the brain."
- **Pricing:** $1–5k setup (or per-capture project fee) + $1,500/mo.
- **Status:** build candidate. **This is also the wedge feature of the Company OS (B1)** — sell it standalone, expand into the OS.

### B7. SaaS-replacement wedge — "build-vs-rent" *(added 2026-08-07)*
- **Type:** entry **wedge into the OS**, not a standalone line · **Buyer:** an owner/ops lead with an **angry invoice** — a specific overpriced single-workflow tool their team half-uses.
- **What:** replace *that one tool* with a custom module the **client owns** and **yourco operates** — *"you pay $28k/yr for X, your team uses seven of its screens; we'll build those seven as a module you own and we run, for less than the renewal."* Ships as form factor 2/3 (`processes/ai-os-modules.md`); the replacement is the on-ramp, the OS is the expansion.
- **Why it fits:** concrete, quantified cold hook (attacks the first-client bottleneck) that leads into the OS; already expressible in the model (no new machinery); sharpens the positioning line — *SaaS = own nothing · dev shop = own it but it rots · yourco = own the asset, we operate the reliability.*
- **Guardrails (non-negotiable — decision `decisions/2026-08-07_saas-replacement-wedge.md`):** (1) build from the client's **workflow spec, never the incumbent's code or a named-product clone** (clean-room; never market a competitor's name); (2) **operated, never hand-off** (else it's the CharlieOS anti-model); (3) **clonable tier only** — forms, scheduling, e-sign, approval flows, internal dashboards, reporting, light PM; **out of scope:** systems of record, compliance-locked, network-effect tools; (4) not a repositioning — one product, one motion holds.
- **Pricing:** as an OS module (per the sorting rule); target retainer below the replaced tool's annual cost. ⚠️ **Ownership/IP terms are open** — counsel-gate #13; **no "you own it" promise in a proposal until Polo + Ray rule.**
- **Status:** wedge adopted; paired with the Audit build-vs-rent teardown lens (`processes/audit-sop.md`). Not sold until the ownership terms clear + OtherVenture.

## C. DTC (individuals) — COUNSEL-GATED, not built for sale until cleared

> Both fit the operated-DTC carve-out (yourco Care family, `decisions/2026-06-16_caregiving-dtc-offering.md`), but carry advice liability. **Spec'd internally; do not offer/launch until counsel clears the disclaimers + data handling.** Design rule (and the reason neither needs a licensed-pro partner): these tools **track, surface patterns, educate, and prep the user's own professional** — they do **not** diagnose, prescribe, or give personalized medical/legal/tax advice. AI never freelances on the regulated parts; Rafi + Ray gate.

### C1. Hormonal-health OS (menstrual migraine / cramps / PMDD)
- **Type:** AI OS (personal health-tracking & insight) · **Buyer:** individuals (DTC); employer channel TBD.
- **What it IS (no clinician partner needed):** a cycle + symptom tracker (the headache-menstrual calendar — a real clinical *tool*, not a diagnosis), pattern detection on *her own logged data* ("your migraines cluster 2 days before your period"), a cited general-education library, calendar sync + reminders, and — the key move — a **clinician-ready report she brings to her own doctor.** Coordination = one shared record across migraine/cramps/mood for her existing care team. Dataset is the moat; the daily-open habit is the wedge.
- **What it is NOT (this is how we avoid needing a clinician):** no diagnosis, no *personalized* treatment recommendations, no dosing, no prescribing. The cycle-timed playbook (mini-prophylaxis, hormonal stabilization, CGRP/gepants, the aura→stroke-risk screen) may appear **only as general, non-personalized education** — never as advice triggered by her live data.
- **The "options" wording line (the Founder's question, 2026-06-16):** we *can* present cycle-aware treatment *concepts* ("for the pre-period window clinicians consider options like…") — but it must pass three tests: **(1) general, not triggered** — it lives in an education library, not as a reaction to her symptom log ("clinicians consider…", never "because *you* logged X, take Y"); **(2) categories, not prescriptions** — "triptans / anti-inflammatories / magnesium," not brand/dose-specific suggestions; **(3) route every specific to her own doctor**, with the "not medical advice" disclaimer **in-context** beside the content, not just a footer. A disclaimer does not launder personalized drug advice — substance beats labels. **Exact strings are counsel-gated** (folded into `t_legal`).
- **The trade-off (clear-eyed):** without a prescriber we give up being the *treating* clinic (that clinical edge needs one). What we keep — still strong — is the **best-in-class tracking + pattern-insight + doctor-prep layer**, exactly what period apps do poorly. **Same dataset moat, zero medical-practice exposure.**
- **Still required:** counsel on "general wellness, not medical advice" disclaimers + **health-data privacy** (FTC Health Breach Notification Rule + state health-data laws; HIPAA likely N/A for a non-covered DTC app — counsel confirms). Rafi gate. CGRP here = *blocking* a peptide (drugs), unrelated to peptide-*testing* (no Sample Contact synergy).

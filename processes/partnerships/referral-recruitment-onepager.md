# Become an yourco referral partner — earn recurring income (v0, staged)

> Connector-facing pitch. Internal/staged — not sent until launch and until the Founder locks the numbers. Voice follows `brand/writing-rules.md`.

## The offer
Know a business drowning in calls, intake, follow-up, or back-office work — or a person who wants a personal AI agent of their own? Send them to yourco. We build and run a named AI agent for whoever needs one — live on its first job in about 48 hours. You earn a cut of what they pay us, every month, for as long as they're a client.

No quota. No base. No cost to you. You make the introduction; we do discovery, the build, and the day-to-day. You get paid when they do.

## What you earn
A recurring percentage of each referred client's monthly fee, and your rate goes up as your book grows:

- **1–5 clients:** **10%** of their monthly fee, every month
- **6–10 clients:** **12.5%** — and it applies to your whole book, not just the new ones
- **11+ clients:** **15%** on everything

It's recurring, so a book of referred clients becomes income that keeps coming, built from introductions you made once. (No income promises — what you earn depends on who you refer and whether they stay.)

**Bring in other referrers, too.** Refer someone else into the program and you earn an extra **1%** on every client they sign, for as long as it stays — your own introductions, plus a slice of theirs. (You earn on their clients' real revenue — never for signing someone up, and joining costs nothing.)

## How it works
1. Join and get your referral link.
2. Send us a business that's a fit, or just make the intro.
3. We close it, build it, and run it. You stay hands-off.
4. You're paid monthly, on what the client actually pays us, starting the month they go live.

## Who this is for
Consultants and fractional execs, bookkeepers and MSPs, anyone who talks to small-business owners all day, and current clients who want their referrals to pay them back. If you know owners who'd rather hire an outcome than learn another tool, this pays you to introduce them.

## The fine print (plain version)
You're an independent referrer, not an yourco employee. We handle pricing, contracts, and delivery. You're paid on collected revenue; a refund in the first 60 days is clawed back. Don't spam people or oversell — the agreement requires honest, compliant outreach, and it protects you and us. W-9 on file, 1099 at year end.

# Channel partner — revenue-share model (v0, proposed)

> ⚠️ **v0 proposal — the Founder + Polo lock every number; counsel reviews the partner agreement.** The economics behind the partner one-pager. Owner: **Bird** + **Polo** (numbers) + **the Founder**. Principle: pay partners **recurring** (not a one-time bounty) so a book of referred clients becomes a compounding line for them — and protect yourco's margin + the quality moat.

## Two partner modes

### Mode 1 — Referral partner *(launch with this)*
The partner **sends a fit**; yourco runs discovery, closes, builds, operates, and **owns the client relationship + quality**. The partner stays hands-off.
- **Why lead with this:** yourco keeps direct control of delivery, eval, approval, and the client relationship — critical before delivery is hardened. The moat (reliability/trust) can't be outsourced yet.
- **Commission:** now governed by the **tiered escalator in `referral-program.md`** (**10% / 12.5% / 15%** recurring on the retainer at 1–5 / 6–10 / 11+ active clients, on collected revenue — locked `decisions/2026-06-30_referral-program-v1.md`) — which supersedes the earlier flat "15% build / 10% retainer" proposal. Read `referral-program.md` for the full structure, terms, and the rep journey.
- **Attribution:** referrer tagged in the CRM (`referrer: "<name/code>"`); Charles computes the tier + commission and runs payouts.

### Mode 2 — White-label / reseller *(unlock as delivery hardens)*
The partner **owns the client relationship + sets the price**; yourco delivers under their brand at a **wholesale rate**, partner keeps the markup.
- **Why later:** putting the brand in the partner's hands only makes sense once the delivery loop + eval gates are proven enough that yourco's reliability survives someone else's pricing/positioning. Premature white-label risks the moat.
- **Structure (proposed):** yourco charges the partner a **wholesale build + wholesale retainer**; partner marks up to their client. Indicative wholesale ≈ **65–75% of yourco's standard price** (partner margin = 25–35%). Volume tiers possible.
- **Guardrails:** yourco still runs evals/approvals/security; minimum service standards in the agreement protect both brands; yourco can pause a white-label account that's being mis-sold.

## Rules of the model
- **Recurring, not one-time** — the whole point is to make partners want the account to *thrive*, aligning them with retention.
- **On collected revenue only** — commission pays on what the client actually pays (no commission on unpaid/churned).
- **yourco absorbs all infra/token cost** regardless of mode — partners and clients are never billed for usage (consistent with `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Quality gate stays with yourco** in both modes (eval/approval/security). Non-negotiable — it's the moat.
- **Clawback** on refunds/chargebacks within [[the first 60–90 days]].
- **Term & exit:** commissions continue while the partner agreement is active; define what happens to recurring commission if the partnership ends (e.g., tail for [[N]] months) — counsel.

## 1. Parties & relationship

1.1 **Parties.** This Referral Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is between **YourCo LLC**, a `[[Florida]]` limited liability company, `[[registered address]]` ("YourCo," "we"), and the undersigned referral partner ("Partner," "you"). Pull entity facts from `finance/legal-docs/business-info.docx`.

1.2 **Independent contractor — not an employee or agent.** The Partner is an **independent contractor**. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint-venture, franchise, or agency relationship. The Partner:
- is **not** an employee, has no authority to bind YourCo, and receives no benefits, wage, salary, or expense reimbursement;
- controls their own manner, means, hours, and methods of making introductions;
- is solely responsible for their own taxes, licenses, and business expenses;
- may **not** hold themselves out as an employee, agent, representative, or spokesperson of YourCo, and may not use the title "YourCo [anything]" except "YourCo Referral Partner" `[[approve exact permitted title]]`.

1.3 **No authority.** The Partner has **no authority** to: quote or set pricing; negotiate, accept, sign, or modify any contract on YourCo's behalf; make representations, promises, warranties, or commitments about YourCo's services, timelines, outcomes, or results; or speak for YourCo. The Partner's role is limited to **introducing** prospects. YourCo alone closes, prices, contracts, builds, operates, and owns every client relationship.

## 2. What the Partner does

2.1 **The referral.** The Partner introduces prospective clients to YourCo by warm introduction or by sharing the Partner's assigned referral link/code.

2.2 **Attribution.** A referred prospect is credited to the Partner when the prospect is **tagged to the Partner in YourCo's CRM at first contact** (`referrer` field). `[[Define the attribution tie-breaker: first-touch wins; window of [[N]] days; what happens on conflicting/duplicate claims; whether a self-referral or an existing-pipeline prospect is excluded.]]`

2.3 **Conduct standard.** The Partner will make introductions honestly and lawfully and will comply with §8 (Compliance). The Partner will not misrepresent YourCo, fabricate endorsements, or make income/earnings/outcome claims of any kind.

## 3. No buy-in / no purchase required

3.1 **No purchase, fee, inventory, or investment** of any kind is required to become or remain a Partner. There is no enrollment fee, no product to buy, no minimum quota, and no pay-to-play. Participation and all earnings are **performance-only** — based solely on real client revenue actually collected by YourCo. *(This clause is load-bearing for the pyramid-vs-legitimate analysis; do not remove without counsel.)*

## 4. Commission structure (Type-2)

> All commission is computed on **collected revenue only** — amounts YourCo has actually received from the referred client — never on invoiced, booked, projected, or churned revenue.

### 4.1 Direct commission — tiered escalator

Recurring commission on each referred client's monthly retainer, at a rate set by the Partner's count of **currently active (paying)** referred clients:

| Tier | Active referred clients | Recurring rate | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referrer | 1–5 | **10%** | the Partner's whole active referred book |
| Senior | 6–10 | **12.5%** | the Partner's whole active referred book |
| Partner | 11+ | **15%** | the Partner's whole active referred book |

4.2 **Whole-book + active-count tiering + residual.**
- The rate applies to the Partner's **entire active referred book**, not just the marginal client (reach 6 active → all 6 pay 12.5%; reach 11 → all 11 pay 15%).
- Tier is recomputed from the **active (paying)** count each period; if churn drops the Partner below a threshold, the rate steps back down for the next period. `[[Confirm tier is measured per payout period and applies prospectively for that period.]]`
- Commission is **residual** — paid for as long as the referred client remains active and paying.

## 6. Clawback

6.1 **Refund / chargeback clawback.** If a referred client obtains a refund or chargeback, or a payment is reversed, within **`[[60]]` days** of the payment on which a commission was paid, the corresponding commission (and any override on it) is **reversed**, and YourCo may **offset it against future payouts** or, if none, the Partner will repay it within `[[30]]` days of notice.

6.2 **Misconduct clawback.** Commission tied to a referral procured through fraud, misrepresentation, spam, or any breach of §8 is forfeited and subject to clawback regardless of the §6.1 window.

7.2 **Termination.** Either party may terminate **for convenience on `[[30]]` days' written notice**. YourCo may terminate **immediately for cause** (breach of §8, fraud, spam, reputational harm, misrepresentation, or unlawful conduct).

## 8. Compliance obligations

8.1 **Anti-spam / outreach laws.** All Partner outreach must comply with **CAN-SPAM**, the **TCPA**, and applicable state analogues (incl. Florida `[[FTSA]]`) and Do-Not-Call rules: no unsolicited bulk email without required opt-out and identification; no auto-dialed/pre-recorded calls or texts without required consent; honor opt-outs. `[[Counsel to confirm which obligations bind a referral partner vs. YourCo, and whether YourCo provides approved templates.]]` Rafi (compliance) gates outreach assets.

8.2 **No deceptive or income claims.** The Partner will make **no** earnings, income, or lifestyle claims about the program, and **no** deceptive, exaggerated, or unsubstantiated claims about YourCo's services, results, timelines, or outcomes. The Partner will use only YourCo-approved descriptions and the income-disclosure statement when describing the opportunity.

8.3 **No spam / no list rental / no impersonation.** No purchased lists, no scraping, no impersonating YourCo, no fake reviews/testimonials.

8.4 **Lawful conduct.** The Partner will comply with all applicable laws, including the MLM/business-opportunity and seller-assisted-marketing-plan rules counsel identifies (`counsel-review-checklist.md`).

## 9. Confidentiality & IP

9.1 **Confidential information.** YourCo's pricing, client lists, prospect data, methods, agent architecture, and non-public business information are confidential; the Partner will not use or disclose them except to make introductions, and will not retain them after termination.

9.2 **YourCo owns the relationship and the work.** YourCo **owns the client relationship, the pricing, the deliverables, and all work product, IP, and the AI OS**. The Partner acquires **no** ownership, license, or residual interest in any client, deliverable, or YourCo IP by making an introduction. The Partner gets **no** right to service, contact post-introduction, or solicit YourCo clients for competing offerings. `[[Non-solicit / non-circumvention scope + duration — counsel to set, FL-enforceable.]]`

9.3 **Marks.** The Partner may use YourCo's name/marks **only** as YourCo authorizes in writing and only to identify themselves as a referral partner; all goodwill inures to YourCo.

9.4 **Data protection.** Any personal data the Partner shares with YourCo must be lawfully obtained; the Partner will not transfer YourCo prospect/client data to third parties.

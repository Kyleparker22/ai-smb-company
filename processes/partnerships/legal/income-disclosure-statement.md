## YourCo Referral Partner Program — Income Disclosure Statement

**Last updated: `[[date]]` · Applies to: Type-2 Sales Referral Partners**

Please read this before applying. It explains, plainly and honestly, what you can and cannot expect to earn.

### 1. Earnings are performance-only — there is no guaranteed income

Becoming an YourCo Referral Partner gives you the **opportunity** to earn commission. It is **not** a job, a salary, or a guarantee of any income. **You earn only when a business you introduce becomes — and stays — a paying YourCo client.** If you refer no one, or the businesses you refer do not become paying clients (or later stop paying), **you earn nothing.**

### 2. The published rates show *how* commission is calculated — they are not a promise or a projection

The program's tiers (for example, 10% / 15% / 20%, and the recruiting override) describe the **formula YourCo uses to calculate commission on collected client revenue**. They are **not** a representation, promise, projection, or guarantee that you will reach any tier, refer any particular number of clients, or earn any particular amount. A rate means nothing until you have produced real, collected client revenue for it to apply to.

### 3. Your results depend entirely on your own effort — and on clients you bring and retain

What you earn depends on factors largely within your own control (the effort you put in, the quality and number of businesses you introduce) and on factors outside it (whether those businesses choose to buy, and whether they remain paying clients). **Individual results vary widely.** YourCo's published worked examples are **illustrations of the math**, not typical or expected outcomes.

### 4. Many participants may earn little or nothing

As with any performance-based referral program, **a meaningful share of participants can be expected to earn little or no commission.** Some may earn nothing at all. You should not join expecting a specific income, and you should not make financial decisions in reliance on earning any amount.

### 5. No investment, no purchase, no buy-in

There is **no fee, purchase, inventory, quota, or investment** required to join or remain a Referral Partner. This is **not** a business opportunity that requires you to buy anything. `[[Counsel: confirm this framing keeps the program outside applicable "business opportunity" / "seller-assisted marketing plan" registration definitions, or add the required disclosures/registrations if it does not.]]`

### 6. Independent contractor; you are responsible for your own taxes

If you earn commission, you do so as an **independent contractor**, not an employee. You are responsible for your own taxes; YourCo will issue a Form 1099 where required and needs a Form W-9 on file before paying you.

### 7. No data yet — this program is new

### 8. Acknowledgment

By applying to or participating in the program, you confirm you have read and understood this Income Disclosure Statement and that **no one has promised or guaranteed you any income.**

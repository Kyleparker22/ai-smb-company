# Partner outreach sequence — recruiting channel partners (PAUSED)

> The Touch 1–4 to recruit referral/white-label partners (agencies, MSPs, vertical SaaS, fractional COOs, bookkeepers — `target-list.md`). Same proof weapon as prospect outreach: lead with a **working demo**, not a pitch. Owner: **Bird** + **the Founder**. **Internal-only:** draft + stage; send nothing until launch + Rafi (CAN-SPAM). Economics: `rev-share-model.md`.

## The angle
Partners don't want another vendor pitch. They want **recurring revenue without delivery risk.** Lead with: *your clients are asking about AI, you don't want to build/host/babysit it, we do — you earn for the life of every account.* Then prove it with a demo of what we'd deliver for *their* client.

## Welcome — what this is

yourco builds and runs AI operating systems for businesses: a coordinated team of AI agents that answers the
calls, chases the leads, drafts the paperwork, and handles the busywork — built custom after an audit, then
operated by us. The client never touches the technology; they get an outcome.

**Your part is the introduction.** You know business owners. When you connect one to us and they become a
client, you earn a percentage of what they pay us — every month, for as long as they stay. No quota, no base
salary to justify, no cost to join, and none of the delivery work is yours.

## Built around your life

Most side income asks you to trade more of your time for it. This is designed to work the other way:

- **Work when you want.** No schedule, no quota, no minimum. One great introduction a quarter makes you a
  connector in good standing; ten a month makes you a bigger one. The pace is entirely yours.
- **Work from wherever you already are.** The whole job is a conversation with people you already know — at
  the coffee shop, on the job site, from your phone. Your network *is* the office.
- **Earnings that match your effort.** Nothing to buy, no ceiling on the escalator, and because commissions
  recur monthly for as long as a referred client stays, the work you do once keeps paying — your book
  compounds instead of resetting to zero each month.
- **Keep the life. Add the income.** This is meant to fit around a business, a career, or a family — not
  compete with them. Work-life balance isn't the trade-off here; it's the design.

*(The honest footnote, as always: none of this is a promise of income — what you earn depends entirely on
the strength of your introductions and whether those clients stay. See the escalator + disclaimer below.)*

## Your role (and what is never your role)

**You do:**
- Spot owners who are drowning in calls, follow-up, scheduling, or admin — and would rather buy an outcome than
  learn a tool.
- Make a warm introduction (a three-way email or text is the whole job).
- Tell the truth about us: we audit first, build custom, run it for them, and nothing goes out without their
  approval.

**You never:**
- Quote a price, negotiate, or sign anything on yourco's behalf — **yourco owns the client relationship and the
  price.** If a prospect asks what it costs: *"They scope it in the audit — I'll connect you and they'll walk
  you through it."*
- Promise results, timelines, or savings we haven't put in writing.
- Cold-spam. Your value is that you're warm. (The agreement covers CAN-SPAM/TCPA; honest outreach only.)
- Do discovery, demos, delivery, or support — that's us, start to finish.

## How it works — the five steps

1. **Join.** Sign the Partner Agreement, put a W-9 on file, get your referral link + code.
2. **Refer — two ways, and you can use both.**
   - **Introduce.** A warm intro, or your link/code. Best outcome, every time.
   - **Or just hand us the name.** Submit a business owner's details in your console and **yourco makes the
     approach** — you stay out of the conversation entirely. **$25** once we verify the contact is real and
     reachable (we aim to review within 24–48 hours), **another $25** if it turns into a real conversation, and
     the normal commission on top if they become a client. We ask **how you know them** and whether they're
     expecting to hear from anyone — because on a submitted contact yourco is the one making the call, and we
     have to be able to say where it came from. Never submit a bought, scraped, or copied list.

   Either way, the company is tagged to you in our CRM at first contact — attribution is locked from that
   moment, and it's one referrer per business, ever.
3. **We take it.** yourco runs the audit, proposes, closes, builds, and operates. You stay hands-off.
4. **Credit.** The client signs and their first payment clears → they're an *active referred client* on your book.
5. **Get paid.** **The 2nd Friday of every month**, on revenue actually collected — at your current tier, on your whole book. (Clients pay on the 1st; a 3-day collection window closes the books; your payout lands that month's 2nd Friday. Anything collecting later rolls into the next payout.)

## What makes a great referral

- A **business owner or operator** (any industry — we're horizontal) with real lead flow or real admin drag:
  missed calls, slow follow-up, quotes that take nights, an inbox that never empties.
- Someone who wants it **handled** — "run this for me," not "teach me the software." (DIY tinkerers are a bad fit.)
- Big enough that the pain is monthly-recurring money, small enough that no one's hired ops staff to fix it.
- Bonus signals: they already tried a chatbot/no-code tool and it fizzled; they're growing and dropping balls;
  they complain about paperwork more than about demand.

**The play, in practice:**
> "You know how you keep saying half your week disappears into [calls/quotes/paperwork]? I know a company that
> builds an AI system that runs that for you — they audit your business first, build it custom, and operate it.
> Nothing sends without your sign-off. Want an intro?"

Then a three-way email to your yourco contact (or your link). That's the whole motion.

## Compensation — the escalator

Your rate is set by how many **active referred clients** are on your book — and when you level up, the new rate
applies to your **entire book**, not just new clients.

| Tier | Active referred clients | Your rate (of each client's monthly fee) |
|---|---|---|
| **Referrer** | 1–5 | **10%** |
| **Senior** | 6–10 | **12.5%** |
| **Partner** | 11+ | **15%** |

**Illustrations** (not promises — your results depend on who you refer and whether they stay). yourco's AI OS
engagements run at monthly retainers like **$3,000, $5,000, and $10,000** depending on scope:
- 3 active clients at $3,000/mo → 10% → **~$900/mo**, recurring.
- 6 active at $5,000/mo → 12.5% on the whole book → **~$3,750/mo**.
- 12 active at $10,000/mo → 15% → **~$18,000/mo** — a genuine income line, earned by introductions you made once.

**Mechanics:**
- Paid on **collected revenue only** — nothing on unpaid invoices or churned clients.
- **Paid the 2nd Friday of every month.** Clients are billed the 1st; collections close after a 3-day window; the
  payout run follows on the 2nd Friday. Late-collecting payments roll to the next month's run. W-9 before first
  payout; 1099 at year end. You're an independent referrer, not an employee.
- **Clawback** if a client refunds/charges back in the first 60 days.
- A monthly statement shows your book, tier, and payout (and how many clients to the next tier).

## Building a team — referring other connectors

You can also refer **people** into the program. When someone you brought in signs clients of their own, you earn
**1% of the revenue their clients generate** — through your **entire downline** (their recruits' clients too),
for as long as those clients stay active.

Worked example (the program's reference math):

| Person | Their clients' MRR | Direct commission | Downline override (1%) | Total/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **You** | $18,000 (5 clients) | $1,800 (10%) | $300 (1% of your team's $30k) | **$2,100** |
| **Your recruit** | $10,000 (3 clients) | $1,000 (10%) | $200 (1% of their recruits' $20k) | **$1,200** |

**When you can start:** at **R1** — once one of your referrals has reached a real conversation (a sit-down or
audit actually held), plus the R1 training. *(Changed 2026-08-11; it was previously R2, a client live and
retained 90 days. `decisions/2026-08-11_connector-program-v2.md`.)*

**The rules that keep this honest (and legal):**
- You are paid on **real client revenue only — never for the act of recruiting.** Signing up a connector pays $0.
- There is **no buy-in, no fee, no inventory** — joining costs nothing, ever.
- An income-disclosure statement accompanies this program; nobody may make income claims beyond it.

## Where this can go — becoming an Advisor

For some people, connecting stays a great side income forever. For others, it becomes the on-ramp: you've
built a real book, you know the pitch cold, and you want a bigger seat at the table. That's what our
**Advisor** role is — yourco's full-time salespeople, the ones who run the audit conversation, scope the OS,
and close. Connectors who've proven it are exactly who we want to talk to first. If that's where you're
headed, say so — let's discuss what it looks like.

## Rules of the road (the short version of the agreement)

1. No income promises or lifestyle claims — use only the numbers in this packet, framed as illustrations.
2. No spam, no purchased lists, no robocalls — warm, honest, compliant outreach (CAN-SPAM/TCPA).
3. Never quote pricing, sign, or commit yourco to anything.
4. Represent the product truthfully: audit-first, custom-built, operated, approval-gated.
5. Don't refer businesses you have no genuine connection to just to farm the code.
6. yourco may decline any referral and can end the agreement for violations. Rate changes never apply retroactively —
   your existing book keeps its terms.

## FAQ

- **Do I sell?** No. You introduce; we run discovery, the audit, the proposal, and the close.
- **When exactly do I get paid?** The **2nd Friday of each month**, for everything collected in that month's billing window (clients pay the 1st + a 3-day buffer). A payment that lands late simply moves to the next payday.
- **What does the client pay?** Scoped per engagement in the audit — you never need to know or quote it. Your
  statement shows your commission, which is what matters to you.
- **When does a referral become "mine"?** At first contact with your tag/link — earliest attribution wins.
- **What if my client churns?** They come off your active book (and your tier count). You keep everything already
  paid — no retro clawback past the 60-day window.
- **Any industry?** Yes — we're horizontal. If they run a business with leads, customers, and admin, they're a fit.
- **Is there a quota or exclusivity?** Neither. Refer one client a year or twenty — your rate simply reflects your book.
- **Can I refer other connectors?** Yes — see "Building a team." You earn on their *clients' revenue*, never for recruiting.
- **What support do I get?** A one-pager to forward, a demo video, the "leak" talking points, and a named contact
  at yourco. You'll never be asked to explain the technology.

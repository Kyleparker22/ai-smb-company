How it works

1

Join

Sign the partner agreement, W-9 on file, get your referral link and code.

2

Introduce — or just hand us the name

Introduce: a warm three-way intro (or your link). Or submit: give us the owner's details in your console and we make the approach — you stay out of the conversation entirely. Either way the company is tagged to you at first contact, attribution locked from that moment, one referrer per business.

3

We take it from there

yourco runs the audit, proposes, closes, builds, and operates. You stay hands-off — you never quote, contract, or support.

4

You're on the books

The client signs, the first payment clears, and they're an active client on your book.

5

You get paid — the 2nd Friday, every month

Clients pay on the 1st; a 3-day collection window closes the books; your payout lands on the month's second Friday — at your current tier, across your whole book. Late-collecting payments roll to the next payday.

"You know how half your week disappears into calls and paperwork? I know a company that builds an AI system that runs that for you — audited first, built custom, operated for you. Nothing sends without your sign-off. Want an intro?" — that sentence is the whole job.

Hand us a name, get paid before it closes

You don't have to make the introduction yourself. Submit a business owner's details and $25 once we verify the contact is real and reachable — we aim to review within 24–48 hours — plus another $25 if it turns into a real conversation, and the normal commission on top if they become a client. A submission never costs you the commission you'd have earned by introducing them.

We ask how you know them and whether they're expecting to hear from anyone, because on a submitted contact we are the ones making the call and we have to be able to say where it came from. Never submit a bought, scraped, or copied list — those are rejected.

Build a team

Refer other partners and earn 1% of the revenue their clients generate — through your downline, for as long as those clients stay. You're paid on real client revenue only: signing up a partner pays $0, and joining never costs anything.

You can start once one of your referrals has reached a real conversation — a sit-down or audit actually held. You can't build a team on a thing you haven't done yourself yet.

Your role — and what's never your role

You do

  • Spot owners drowning in calls, follow-up, quotes, or admin
  • Make a warm, honest introduction — or submit the details and let us make the approach
  • Answer honestly how you know anyone you submit, and whether they're expecting to hear from us
  • Describe us truthfully: audit-first, custom-built, operated, approval-gated

You never

  • Quote pricing, negotiate, or sign — yourco owns the relationship and the price
  • Promise results or income; make claims beyond this packet
  • Cold-spam or buy lists — warm and compliant only. Never submit a name from a bought, scraped, or copied list
  • Do demos, delivery, or support — that's us, start to finish

Where this can go

For some people, connecting stays a great side income forever. For others, it becomes the on-ramp: you've built a real book, you know the pitch cold, and you want a bigger seat at the table. That's our Advisor role — yourco's full-time salespeople, who run the audit conversation, scope the OS, and close. Connectors who've proven it are exactly who we want to talk to first. If that's where you're headed, say so — let's discuss what it looks like.

# Partner enablement kit — turning a "yes" into referrals

> What happens *after* a partner says yes. A signed partner who doesn't know how to spot or send a client is a dead partnership. This is the kit + the process that turns a handshake into a flow of referrals. Owner: **Bird** (runs it) + **David** (deal-reg/CRM) + **Charles** (commission). Pairs with `partner-one-pager.html`, `partner-outreach-sequence.md`, `rev-share-model.md`.

## What the partner gets (the kit)
1. **Co-branded one-pager** — `partner-one-pager.html` with their logo, to share with their clients.
2. **A referral link / deal-registration form** — how they submit a client (protects them from channel conflict, starts commission tracking).
3. **The rev-share terms** — plain summary of what they earn + when (`rev-share-model.md`).
4. **The "ideal client" cheat sheet** — which of their clients to flag (below).
5. **Client-facing talking points** — the 30-second pitch they use.
6. **Access to the Instant Employee** — so they (or we, for them) can generate a working demo for any client on the spot. Their best closing tool.

## The "ideal client" cheat sheet (give the partner this)
Flag a client when they:
- **Miss inbound calls/leads** (on job sites, after hours, slammed front desk).
- **Book appointments or estimates** as a core motion.
- Are **owner-operated, ~1–50 people** — the owner feels the pain.
- Have **high job/customer value** (so a recovered lead is real money).
- Are in a vertical we serve (landscaping, dental, med-spa, home services, auto, restaurants, law, fitness, e-commerce…).
> One line for the partner: *"Which of your clients is losing business because they can't answer the phone? That's the one."*

## Client-facing talking points (30 seconds, in the partner's voice)
> "You know how you lose leads when you can't get to the phone? I work with yourco — they build a named AI employee that answers every call, qualifies the job, and books it, live in 48 hours. It never quotes a price or goes off-script — you approve anything customer-facing. Want me to have them build you a quick demo off your site?"

## The deal-registration process (no channel conflict)
1. Partner submits a client (name + domain) via the referral form / to Bird.
2. **David logs it** in the CRM, `source: "partner — <partner name>"`, owner Bird → the Founder. Tagged so commission attaches.
3. We generate a **personalized Instant Employee demo** for that client (Mode A) — the partner can send it, or we co-send.
4. yourco runs discovery → proposal → 48h build (referral mode), keeping the partner looped.
5. **Charles tracks the commission** (recurring, on collected revenue, per `rev-share-model.md`).

## Onboarding a new partner (the 5 steps)
1. Sign the Partner Agreement (counsel-reviewed; referral or white-label tier).
2. Send the kit (co-branded one-pager + cheat sheet + talking points + referral link).
3. **Build one demo together** — pick a real client of theirs, generate the Instant Employee live. The "aha" that makes them believe.
4. Get the first referral registered.
5. Set a check-in cadence (Bird) — keep the partner active; a partner who refers once and goes quiet needs a nudge, not a write-off.

## Internal-only
Build + stage the kit now; partner onboarding runs at launch (needs the counsel-reviewed Partner Agreement + the ability to actually deliver). The kit being ready means a launch-day "yes" converts immediately.

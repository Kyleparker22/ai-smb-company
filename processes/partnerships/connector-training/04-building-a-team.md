## Why this unlocks at R1 and not sooner

You may recruit other connectors once **one of your referrals has reached a real conversation** — a sit-down
or an audit actually held. That order is deliberate: nobody in this program builds a team before they have
done the thing the team is supposed to do. A referral network where people recruit before they produce
anything at all is a different, worse kind of business, and yourco is not building that one.

*This moved on 2026-08-11.* It used to require a client live and retained for 90 days, which in practice
meant nobody could recruit for the better part of a year. One real conversation is a lower bar and an
honest one — it is still evidence you have actually done the job, not just signed up for it.

## The rules that keep it honest

- You are paid on **real client revenue only — never for the act of recruiting.** Signing up a connector pays
  you nothing.
- There is **no buy-in, no fee, no inventory.** Joining costs nothing, ever.
- An income-disclosure statement accompanies this program, and nobody — you included — may make income claims
  beyond it.

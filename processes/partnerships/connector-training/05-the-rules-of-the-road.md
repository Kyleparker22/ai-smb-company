## The six rules

1. **No income promises, no lifestyle claims.** Not about what yourco will do for a business, and not
   about what this program pays you or anyone else. If a number is not in a document yourco gave you,
   it does not get said out loud.
2. **No spam, no purchased lists, no robocalls.** Warm, honest, compliant outreach only — your partner
   agreement carries the CAN-SPAM and TCPA language, and it is not decoration. Your entire value in
   this program is that you are warm; a cold list makes you worth less, not more.
3. **Never quote pricing, sign, or commit yourco to anything.** yourco owns the client relationship and
   the price. You are making an introduction, not a deal.
4. **Represent the product truthfully:** audit first, built custom, operated by yourco, and
   approval-gated — nothing customer-facing goes out without the client's sign-off. That last part is
   the true thing most people do not expect, so it is worth saying every time.
5. **Do not refer businesses you have no genuine connection to** just to get your code on a record.
   The program is built on warm relationships; farming it is the one behaviour that breaks it.
6. **yourco may decline any referral, and may end the agreement for violations.** In return: a rate
   change never applies backwards — your existing book keeps the terms it was earned under.

## The price question, and the line that answers it

It will come up on your very first introduction, and it is the moment people improvise. Do not
improvise. There is one answer:

> "They scope it in the audit — I'll connect you and they'll walk you through it."

That is not a dodge. yourco prices an engagement from what the audit finds, so any number you guessed
would be wrong, and a wrong number is worse than no number: it either kills a real deal or sets up a
conversation that has to walk itself backwards later.

## What you are, exactly

You are an **independent referral partner — not an yourco employee.** That sentence appears in your
agreement, in the packet, and on every page yourco generates on your behalf, and it needs to be true in
how you describe yourself too. You choose your own hours, there is no quota, no minimum, and no
schedule, and you are free to do this alongside anything else you do.

## Where the rules actually live

This lesson is a summary. **Your partner agreement is the document that governs** — if anything here
ever reads differently from it, the agreement wins, and yourco would want to know about the gap.

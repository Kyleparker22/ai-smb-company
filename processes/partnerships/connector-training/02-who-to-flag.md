## The signals

Flag someone when they:

- **Miss inbound calls or leads** — on job sites, after hours, or at a slammed front desk.
- **Book appointments or estimates** as a core part of how they make money.
- Are **owner-operated, roughly 1–50 people** — small enough that the owner personally feels the pain.
- Have **high job or customer value**, so one recovered lead is real money.
- Want it **handled** — "run this for me", not "teach me the software". DIY tinkerers are a bad fit.
- Are big enough that the pain is recurring monthly money, small enough that nobody has hired ops staff to fix it.

**Bonus signals:** they already tried a chatbot or a no-code tool and it fizzled; they are growing and dropping
balls; they complain about paperwork more than they complain about demand.

## The one question

If you remember nothing else from this lesson, remember this line:

> *"Which of the business owners you know is losing business because they can't get to the phone?"*

That is the one.

## A note on industry

yourco is horizontal — any industry. There is no list of approved verticals you have to check a name against,
and you do not need to know whether yourco has done work in someone's trade before. The fit test is the
bottleneck, not the industry.

## Why your console was locked

Because you hadn't done this yet.

A new connector sees exactly one thing: this training. Not the earnings, not the pipeline, not the
resources. That's deliberate — yourco would rather you know what you're representing before you have the
tools to represent it. Finish R0 and the rest of the console opens.

It's the same standard yourco holds its own AI agents to: **nothing gets permission before it has
demonstrated it can be trusted with it.** You're being held to the company's own rule, not a special one.

## The five rungs

| Rung | What earns it | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **R0 · Joined** | Signed agreement, W-9 on file, this training done | Make warm introductions · this console · the referral-spotter |
| **R1 · Proven** | One referral that reaches a real conversation, plus R1 training | **Generate live demos** on the spot · **your own digital employee**, built and run free · **introduce other connectors** |
| **R2 · Producing** | Your first referred client live and still there at 90 days, plus R2 training | Quote yourco's locked prices · co-branded materials |
| **R3 · Trusted** | Three live referred clients, retention holding, no conduct flags, plus R3 training | Run audit conversations yourself, with yourco's oversight |
| **R4 · Advisor track** | A sustained book, and a conversation with the Founder | Carry your own book with yourco delivering underneath |

**Two things earn every rung: the evidence and the training.** Producing the result isn't enough on its
own, and neither is finishing the lessons. If your results run ahead of your training, your console will
say so in plain words — *"you've earned R2; you're holding R1 until you finish R1 training."*

From R2 upward — **and on the recruiting lesson at R1** — an yourco operator confirms your training
rather than you self-marking it. That's not distrust. Those are the points where money and a team start,
and a real conversation before you cross either line protects you more than it protects yourco.

## Why you can't recruit on day one

**R1** is the earliest rung that lets you bring in other connectors, and it takes one referral that
reached a real conversation — a sit-down or an audit actually held.

That's on purpose. **You can't build a team on a thing you haven't done yourself — or been trained on.**
Nobody here earns anything for the act of signing someone up: recruiting a connector pays $0, and the
override pays only on real revenue their clients generate.

*(This used to sit at R2 and require a client live for 90 days. It moved on 2026-08-11 — that bar meant
nobody could build a team for the better part of a year, which helped no one.)*

## Rungs can go down

If the evidence reverses — a referred client churns, a pattern of bad-fit referrals shows up, a conduct
flag lands — the rung drops, and the permissions drop with it.

Nothing about this is a judgment of you as a person, and nothing about it is arbitrary. Rungs are
computed from what's actually in yourco's records, never granted or withdrawn by mood, and every movement
is written to a permanent log you can read in your own console under **Your history.**

## What this means practically

Don't act outside the rung you hold. If you haven't reached R2, you don't quote prices — you use the
line from the rules lesson and hand it to yourco. If you haven't reached R1, you don't generate demos.

The ladder isn't a hoop. It's the reason a connector's word means something when they finally do carry
yourco's brand into a room.

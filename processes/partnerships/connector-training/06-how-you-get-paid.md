## The escalator

Your rate is set by how many **active referred clients** are on your book — clients who are live and
paying:

| Tier | Active referred clients | Your recurring rate |
|---|---|---|
| Referrer | 1–5 | 10% |
| Senior | 6–10 | 12.5% |
| Partner | 11+ | 15% |

**The rate lifts your whole book, not just the next client.** Reach six active clients and all six pay
at 12.5%. Reach eleven and all eleven pay at 15%. That is why the 5→6 and 10→11 steps are worth
knowing about — this console shows you exactly how far away yours are, on your real book.

It works the other way too. Tier is set by the **active** count, so a client churning from 11 back to
10 moves you back to 12.5% until you are over the line again. You are paid to care whether referred
clients stay happy, not just to hand over names.

**No illustrations here on purpose.** The packet shows worked examples with sample retainers; this
console shows your own book instead. A real number about you beats an illustration about somebody
hypothetical.

## The payday

- **The 2nd Friday of every month.**
- Clients are billed the 1st, a 3-day collection window closes, and the payout run follows.
- **On collected revenue only** — nothing on an unpaid invoice, nothing on a churned client. A payment
  that lands late simply rolls into the next month's run.
- **A W-9 has to be on file before any payout can be computed.** You can refer without one; you cannot
  be paid without one. yourco issues a 1099 at year end. You are an independent referrer.
- **Clawback** if a client refunds or charges back early in the engagement.

## When a referral becomes *yours*

- **Credit is the first logged touch** — your link or code, or a named introduction you log with yourco
  at the time you make it. Log the intro when you make it; that is the whole discipline.
- **One referrer per company, ever.** If two people could claim the same business, the first logged
  touch takes the whole company. There are no splits, because splits are what turn referral programs
  into arguments.
- **Commission rides the client's full retainer for as long as they stay active**, including growth in
  the engagement.

## What is not settled yet

Being straight with you about the edges, because you will eventually hit one:

- The exact **retroactive-claim window** and **attribution window** (how long an introduction stays
  yours before it goes cold) are written down but not finally locked.
- Whether there is a **one-time bonus on a build fee** is undecided; today commission is on the
  recurring retainer only.
- The **downline override** — earning on connectors you recruit — is with outside counsel and is not
  payable to anybody. See the R2 lesson.
- The whole program is in counsel review, so no payout is owed or payable to anyone yet.

If a dispute ever arises about credit, it goes to yourco, the decision is written down on the record,
and any rule it clarifies is added going forward — never applied backwards to somebody's book.

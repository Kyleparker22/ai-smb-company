## What changes at R2

Two things unlock together, and both are a small transfer of yourco's voice to you:

- **You may quote yourco's locked prices** — directly, in a conversation, without deflecting to the
  audit.
- **You get co-branded materials** — assets that carry your name alongside yourco's.

## Why it is this rung and not an earlier one

Both of these put you in front of a stranger holding something that reads as yourco speaking. Until you
have produced a live, retained client, you have not yet seen what yourco actually delivers — and a
price quoted by someone who has not is a guess wearing a suit.

## The rules that come with it

- **Only the locked numbers, exactly as issued.** A price yourco did not give you is not a price. You
  do not round it, bundle it, discount it, or "start" a negotiation with it — the moment a conversation
  turns into negotiation, it is yourco's.
- **yourco's public surfaces carry no prices at all.** Do not put a number on anything public: not a
  post, not a page, not a forwarded document. Quoting is a thing you do in a conversation.
- **A co-branded surface is still an external surface.** No fabricated claims, metrics, or
  testimonials, and no client named without that client's approval.
- **Your independence still has to be legible.** Co-branded does not mean you now work for yourco, and
  nothing you put your name on may suggest that it does.

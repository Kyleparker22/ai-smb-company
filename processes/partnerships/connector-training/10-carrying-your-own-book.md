## What R4 is

**Carrying your own book, with yourco delivering underneath.** For most people connecting stays a good
side income permanently, and that is a complete and respectable outcome. For a few it becomes an
on-ramp: you have built a real book, you know the offer cold, and you want a bigger seat.

## The one structural thing that is decided

There are two distinct roles at yourco, and they are not the same job:

- **Connectors** are independent referral partners. That is what you are.
- **Advisors** are yourco's own salespeople — they run the audit conversation, scope the system, and
  close.

If you end up doing both, you hold **two separate records**, and commission is earned only on what came
through your connector side. This is deliberate: the line between the two roles is the line the whole
program's compliance posture rests on, and it does not get blurred for convenience.

## Why there is no counter for this rung

R0 through R3 are computed from evidence in yourco's records — you can see the arithmetic on this page,
and there is nothing to argue about. **R4 is a judgment call by the Founder, and it is deliberately not
computed.** There is no number to hit and therefore no number to game. The conversation happens because
somebody has been carrying a real book for a sustained stretch, and it happens directly with him.

## What is not written yet

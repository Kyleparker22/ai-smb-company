## What you just earned

Reaching R1 unlocks a second thing, and most connectors underestimate it: **yourco will build and
operate a working digital employee for your own business or side hustle, free, for as long as you're
active.**

Not a demo. Not a trial. The real thing — built for your work, running on yourco's infrastructure, kept
current as the models improve, with yourco owning the reliability and the upkeep. The same thing a client
pays for every month.

## Why yourco does this

Partly because you've earned it. Mostly because of what it does to your conversations.

The single most persuasive sentence in this entire program is **"I use this myself."** Not a claim, not a
brochure — a thing you can open on your phone at a table and show somebody. Every referral program in the
world hands its people a link and a script. Almost none of them can hand their people the product,
because almost none of them sell something a person can actually use.

You will describe yourco more accurately after two weeks of using an agent than after reading everything
in this training. That's the point.

## How to get yours

1. **Say what you'd want it to do.** The strongest candidates are the things you already avoid: the
   inbox you don't get to, the follow-ups that slip, the quote you rewrite from scratch every time, the
   scheduling ping-pong.
2. **yourco scopes it with you** — the same short conversation a client's build starts with, so you also
   get to *feel* what your referrals will feel.
3. **It gets built and it runs.** You use it. You tell yourco what's wrong with it, and it improves —
   which is exactly what a live client relationship looks like from the inside.

If you don't run a business of your own, this can be pointed at the connector work itself — the intro
drafting, the follow-up you keep meaning to send, the list of people you meant to circle back to.

## What it is, precisely — and what it isn't

This matters, so it's stated plainly rather than buried:

Your agent, your yourco email, your Slack access and this console are **licensed, revocable tools
provided for your convenience.** They do not make you an yourco employee. You remain an independent
referral partner: you set your own hours, there are no minimums, nobody supervises how you spend your
time, and you are free to work and refer for anyone else you choose.

That's not legal throat-clearing — it's the actual shape of the arrangement, and yourco would rather you
know it precisely than be surprised by it later.

## The part people miss

The agent isn't a perk bolted onto a commission plan. It's the fastest way to become genuinely good at
this. You'll stop describing "an AI system that answers your phone" in the abstract and start saying
"here's what mine did on Tuesday" — and that sentence closes doors that no script ever will.

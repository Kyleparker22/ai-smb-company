## "Couldn't I just do this with ChatGPT / Zapier / an agent builder?"

Building an agent is the easy 20%. Making one reliable enough to run unsupervised — evals, watchdogs,
approval gates, rollback, observability — is the other 80%, and it is the part a no-code operator
cannot deliver. That is the whole business.

## "Won't this be obsolete in a year?"

The **model-upgrade dividend**: because yourco owns the stack and sells the outcome, every model and
tooling improvement reaches the client as a free upgrade. **The offering appreciates as AI advances,
while any tool they could buy today depreciates.** This is the standing answer and it is genuinely
yourco's strongest.

## "Why not just hire someone?"

No recruiting, ramp, turnover or benefits; live in days; improves weekly; and the marginal cost of the
next role is a fraction of a headcount. Do not oversell this into "never hire again" — the honest
version is that it absorbs the work nobody wanted to hire for.

## "Who else have you done this for?"

**The hard one, and the one you must not lie about.** yourco is pre-revenue with one unsigned
engagement. Say so, then move to what you *can* show: the diagnosis you just handed them, the system
yourco runs on itself, and the control map they helped write. A prospect who catches an invented
client is gone permanently — and no proposal is worth that.

## The moat in one sentence

Anyone can build the agent. yourco owns whether it can be trusted to run without you watching it.

## The formula

> "Your answering service + your CRM + your email marketing + your scheduler + your bookkeeper +
> your SOP binder. Except they talk to each other, and they do the work. **That's your AI OS.**"

It works because it composes something unfamiliar out of six things the owner already pays for. They
infer it rather than being taught it.

## Then close on what they cannot buy anywhere else

*"You own the outcome; we own everything that makes it reliable."*

## The trap

**The formula sells the WHAT. It cannot sell the WHY US.** Reliability, evals, approvals and the
model-upgrade dividend are all invisible in a sum of familiar parts — a no-code operator could recite
the same formula honestly. So **the formula opens and the moat closes.** If you let the formula carry
the whole pitch, you have described a category and left yourco competing inside it on price.

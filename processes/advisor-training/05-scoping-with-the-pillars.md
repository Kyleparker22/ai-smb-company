## The eight pillars

Intake/Front Desk · Sales/Revenue · Marketing/Demand · Customer/Retention · Operations/Delivery ·
Back Office/Finance · Company Brain/Knowledge · People/Training.

Every bottleneck you found in the audit maps to one of these. That mapping is the scoping vocabulary —
it turns "they're drowning" into "Intake and Back Office, in that order."

## Three shapes, and "no agent" is one of them

A module ships as a **digital employee**, a **headless automation**, or an **embedded AI surface** (a
client-facing AI product, like Sample Client's Design Studio). If a module has no agent attached, that
is a shape, not a gap — do not invent a persona to make it look like the others.

## Scope becomes a tier

**Core** (~3 agents) · **Suite** (~5) · **Operation** (~7) · **Command** (up to 10). The agent count is
an *included guide*, not a per-agent meter — never price it as a per-seat count. Beyond ten is
case-by-case. Polo owns the bands; you scope, you do not quote.

## The horizontal rule

yourco does not segment by trade. The offer is audit → custom AI OS for any business in any industry.
Depth in a vertical emerges from referral density, not from pre-selection — so do not promise
industry specialisation yourco has not built.

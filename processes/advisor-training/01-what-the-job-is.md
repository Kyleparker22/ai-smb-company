## The role, in one line

An **advisor** is a full-time yourco salesperson: you run the audit-shaped sales conversation, scope
the OS against the eight pillars, and carry the proposal. A **connector** refers and stays hands-off.
You are not a connector with a bigger commission — different job, different authority.

## What you decide, and what you don't

| You | the Founder |
|---|---|
| Run the diagnostic call, score the bottlenecks, draft the report and proposal | Approves and **sends** anything that reaches the client |
| Scope which pillars and which tier fit | Signs, negotiates, and settles any price outside the locked band |
| Say honestly when yourco cannot help | Every legal, contractual, or gate question |

## The three that do not bend

1. **the Founder sends; advisors draft.** Anything going to a human outside yourco — email, text, a link — is
   produced as copy and handed over. This is not a formality about tone; it is the control that keeps
   an unreviewed claim from reaching a buyer.
2. **No unlocked pricing.** Polo owns the bands. The public site carries no number. If a prospect
   presses, the honest line is that the audit is free right now and pricing comes with the proposal.
3. **No fabricated proof.** yourco is pre-revenue with one unsigned engagement. You may describe what
   the OS does and what it is built to do; you may not invent a metric, a client, or a testimonial. If
   you need a number you do not have, the answer is "we haven't got that number yet."

## The thing that makes this job different

You are selling a system nobody has bought yet. That means the credibility comes from **diagnosis, not
proof** — from telling an owner something true about their own business that they had not seen. The
audit is the product demo.

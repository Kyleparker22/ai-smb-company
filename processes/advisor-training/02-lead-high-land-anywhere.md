## The hierarchy

**Custom AI OS → a few employees → a single employee.** You lead high. Every proposal *opens* with the
OS, and the audit is what makes that opening credible — you have just shown them their whole operation
mapped, so proposing the system that runs it is the natural next sentence.

## Landing below it is a good outcome

Most deals will land under the full OS. That is **not** a failed sale. The smaller unit is the **first
module of a system the client has already seen mapped** — the expansion path is planned from day one.

The mistake to avoid is pre-framing. The moment you present the single employee as "the cheaper
option" or "if the OS is too much", you have made the later upsell awkward, because you told them they
bought the lesser thing. Present it as where the system starts.

## The failure this replaces

The old habit was leading with the digital employee because it is easier to explain and cheaper to say
out loud. It anchors the whole relationship at the entry rung, and growth then has to come from
discounting into a bigger deal. **Expansion comes from upsell inside an account, never from cutting
price to win a bigger one.**

## Say it honestly

n=0 clients. Nobody has expanded yet, because nobody has signed. The hierarchy is yourco's stated
model and a belief about how these accounts grow — present it as the plan, not as a track record.

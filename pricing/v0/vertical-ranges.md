## The model (the Founder-locked 2026-06-16)
Two ways to buy. Pricing reflects the offering hierarchy: **custom OS > a few employees > single employee.**

**A. Individual employees (à-la-carte, non-OS)**
- **Per-employee setup:** **$1,000–5,000 one-time** each, by build/complexity (production-grade = top of band).
- **Retainer:** **$1,500/mo** first employee · **+$500/mo** each additional.
- **Cap: 3 individual employees.** 4+ coordinated = an AI OS.
- (Company onboarding $1,000 one-time still applies on the Tier-1 anchor — `landscaping-hardscaping.md`.)

**B. AI OS (multiple employees as one coordinated system — the flagship)**
- **Implementation / onboarding:** **$2,000–5,000 one-time** (one consolidated fee — replaces separate onboarding + per-employee setup).
- **Retainer:** **$3,000–10,000/mo** consolidated.

> **Tier-2 build folded in:** the old "Tier-2 production build $5k–10k" is superseded — a single production employee's setup now lives at the **top of the $1k–5k per-employee setup** band. Bigger than that → it's an OS. Tier-2 *value* is captured in the per-employee retainer (toward $2–2.5k for production/compliance employees) + the Pro Audit.

## How a vertical moves off the anchor
Adjust the Tier-1 anchor by three factors:
1. **Job/customer value** — higher value per booked job → higher willingness to pay (one recovered lead pays the retainer many times over).
2. **Compliance lift** — regulated verticals (dental, medical, legal, financial) carry a higher reliability/BAA bar → top of range.
3. **Volume / complexity** — multi-location, high call volume, or multiple integrations → up.

### Graduating individual employees → OS (the Founder-locked 2026-06-16)
A client moving from individual employees to an OS does **not** pay the implementation fee again.
- **No second onboarding/implementation fee.** Onboarding (tenant, brand/voice, integrations, eval criteria) and the per-employee setups are already done and live — the $2–5k implementation fee is scoped for net-new cold clients only.
- **Prior onboarding + setup fees credit 100% toward the OS implementation fee** — which, for any 3-employee client (~$7–10k already in), more than covers it, so **implementation is effectively $0 on graduation.**
- **Only net-new orchestration work is chargeable** — wiring the existing employees into a coordinated system (shared context, system-level eval + approval, cross-agent handoffs) + any new employee's setup. Keep it small, or **waive it to land the expansion** (à la Client Owner's waived build).
- **The expansion is captured in the retainer step-up**, not an upfront re-charge: from the individual total (3 employees = $2,500/mo) up into the OS band ($3–10k/mo). Recurring is where the moat + value live.
- **Why:** this makes the 3-employee cap a *positive* nudge, not a wall — "you've outgrown à-la-carte; let's turn these into one coordinated system, no new setup cost, just the system retainer." Easy yes, and it's the same land-and-expand principle as the per-agent add-on (reduced incremental build, substrate already exists).

The 3-employee individual cap exists to force this conversation. **No re-charged implementation fee on graduation** — see "Graduating individual employees → OS" above; the expansion is captured in the retainer step-up.

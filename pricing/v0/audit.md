# The AI Audit — pricing (v0, Polo proposes · the Founder locks)

> **Owner: Polo.** The Audit is the diagnostic front door (`processes/audit-sop.md`). This file is Polo's *internal* proposal space — **the website shows no prices** (the Founder's call, 2026-06-15). The actual number is set on the intro call / proposal, never published.

## ⚠️ CURRENT STATE — the Audit is FREE (the Founder, 2026-08-16)
**There is no charge for an Audit.** `decisions/2026-08-16_audit-is-free.md` supersedes the fee locked below;
everything from "The fee" down is preserved as **the price when it returns**, not the price today. The trip-wire
is **3+ completed engagements with measured outcomes** — at that point there is evidence to sell against and the
fee can come back.

**What the free decision costs, precisely.** The fee was credited 100% to anyone who proceeded, so a converting
client always paid the same total — free changes nothing for them. What yourco gives up is (a) revenue from
**non-converting** audits, (b) upfront cash float, and (c) **the qualification filter**. (c) is the one to watch:
the principle below explicitly priced the Audit so that paying it *signals a real buyer*, and that filter is now
gone. The replacement filter is **Bella's capacity**, and if demand outruns it the answer is a waitlist or a
qualification step — not a quiet return to charging.

## The principle (unchanged in intent; the pricing lever is currently set to zero)
Make the Audit an **easy first yes** for an SMB owner, and structure it so it **funnels into the build** rather
than competing with it. The original design added a third goal — a fee high enough that paying it **signals a
real buyer** — which the free decision deliberately trades away while yourco has no case studies to answer the
"why should I pay you to tell me something" objection with.

- **Fee credited toward the first build** — 100% credit, applied to the build/implementation fee, **conditioned on the client proceeding on a minimum 6-month engagement** (the Founder-locked 2026-06-16). This is the key mechanic: it makes the Audit read as a *smart first step*, not a sunk cost, removes the "what if I pay and don't use you" objection, and ties the credit to a commitment that protects the retainer LTV. The condition is a commitment (6-mo contract), not a time window — no 30-day clock.
- **Honest-no refund** — if yourco tells them on the call that AI can't meaningfully help (the SOP guardrail), refund or don't charge. Cheap insurance for the brand; it almost never fires for a qualified intake.

## Why this shape (the levers)
- **Anchors against the loss, not the hours.** The Audit quantifies a bottleneck often costing thousands/month — the fee is trivially small next to that number, which the report makes vivid.
- **Credit-toward-build protects close rate.** The Audit shouldn't slow the path to a build; crediting it keeps momentum.
- **It's margin-positive on its own** — it's discovery we'd do anyway, now funded. Even Audit-only clients (who take the map and run) are break-even-or-better and become referral sources.

## Locked (the Founder)
- **Audit fee — SUPERSEDED 2026-08-16 (now $0).** Return price: $1,000 Standard / $1,500 Pro (compliance + multi-location). A near-universal flat price, not a per-vertical grid — the Audit is the qualifier and funnel, not the profit center, so it's optimized for top-of-funnel yeses, not vertical value-capture.
- **Founders' offer — RETIRED INTO THE GENERAL RULE 2026-08-16** (it was: `decisions/2026-07-14_founders-audit-offer.md`, the **first 3
  warm-network audits run at $0 upfront** (free if they don't proceed; 100%-credited as always if they
  do). Cap counted by audits *started*; #4 pays the locked price. Warm-network 1:1 only — never in cold
  copy or on the site. The offer message states the normal price so the waiver reads as founders'
  access, not discounted goods). **Now moot: every audit is free, not just the first three.** The one piece worth keeping from it — asking, earned rather than demanded, for **an honest testimonial + intros** — now applies to every free audit, and is the closest thing to a price yourco charges.
- **Credit condition (2026-06-16):** the Audit fee is credited 100% **when the client signs a minimum 6-month engagement** (replaces the earlier "credit within a 30-day window" idea). Reflected on `audit.html` + `pricing.html` (no numbers, per the standing rule).
- **What the credit applies to — full upfront (Polo's call, 2026-06-16):** the Audit fee credits 100% against the **entire upfront** — individual path: onboarding ($1,000) + per-employee setup; OS path: the implementation/onboarding fee. *Not* build/setup-only. Rationale: the real margin is the recurring retainer, so the upfront is a customer-acquisition lever — crediting the full upfront costs little real margin and gives the cleanest "your audit was effectively free, just get started" message. No spillover risk: the upfront ($2k–6k individual; $2–5k OS) always exceeds the audit fee ($1,000/$1,500), so the credit never reaches the retainer. The customer-facing phrase "credited toward your build" *means* this full-upfront credit operationally.
